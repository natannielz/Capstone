## C. Landing: "Satu kardus" — one box carries the whole story

**Concept.** The protagonist is a single corrugated box. The scroll story follows exactly what happens to a branch request, told through that box:

1. **Diajukan:** the requested items float above the open box, each with its package count.
2. **Ditinjau toko:** items drop into the box one by one (arc, settle and a small bounce), and a check appears per item.
3. **Disiapkan:** flaps fold shut with a slight overshoot, tape unrolls across the seam, and the label prints and presses on.
4. **Dikirim:** the box lifts onto a hand trolley and rolls out, with a short route line and the shipment number.
5. **Diterima:** the box arrives on the branch desk; a receipt stamp lands; the invoice slides out.

**Realism.**
- Cardboard: procedural PBR corrugated board, with fibre noise, flute normals on cut edges, darker folds, light edge wear and a slight bevel. The flaps have real thickness.
- Items: matched to catalog products. PET bottles with transmission and wrapped labels, printed paper cartons and pouches, a notebook stack.
- Lighting: soft studio light (environment plus a key light), blurred contact shadows for grounding, and AgX tone mapping. Ambient occlusion, light bloom and focus pulls stay on the "cinematic" tier; the "balanced" tier keeps shadows and contact shadows.
- Motion: spring-eased keyframes driven by scroll, with small idle motion when scrolling stops. One orchestrated moment per act, nothing decorative.

**Theme and page.** A refined palette that keeps the brand's teal and kraft with one orange accent, a lighter studio stage instead of the dark void, clearer type rhythm and fewer, better sections. The hero, the five acts, the catalog sample (now from the expanded catalog), how-it-works, sign-in and footer. Reduced motion shows a composed still per act. Works under the strict CSP with no remote assets. WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard order.

**Tests and evidence.** Pure choreography (`journeyFrame`) unit tests per act; scene construction tests; visual baselines at 1440 and 390 px in both tiers; contrast audit; a frame-time check that keeps the adaptive downgrade.
