# Design and motion plan — v12

Date: 23 September 2026

## Direction

Use a contemporary store catalogue: white surfaces, petroleum ink (#103f47), cool silver backgrounds (#edf2f4), and restrained orange actions (#b64c27). Preserve the existing wordmark and local fonts; loosen headline tracking and use body typography for product names, prices and controls. The signature feature is a large photographic collection carousel, not floating overlapping product cards.

## Composition

- Shop: short unboxed introduction, a controlled collection carousel, compact category navigation, then a stable product grid. Remove the decorative eyebrow dot and tilted product pile.
- Product: a large unobstructed product photo and compact specifications beside a clearly ordered purchasing column: name, description, price, stock, package, quantity, subtotal, actions and fulfilment information.
- Landing: aligned photography and product contact sheet, real collection links and generous section rhythm.
- Login: visible return navigation, a stationary form and a photographic story panel.

## Motion

Six-second collection autoplay with visible pause/play and previous/next controls. Hover suspends; focus, dragging and manual navigation stop rotation until explicitly resumed. Hidden and offscreen carousels stop timers. Reduced motion disables autoplay and animation. Use brief text/image entrances, subtle image hover and successful cart feedback; keep purchase controls stationary.

## Critique and acceptance

The previous design had overlapping rotated product labels, repeated mint containers and over-animated grid refreshes. The replacement must remain readable at 320, 390, 768 and desktop widths, with no horizontal page overflow, clipped labels, inaccessible slides or fixed action bars obscuring final content. Verify real carousel movement, pause semantics, navigation, filters, cart feedback and console errors in a browser. Research and implementation reports record evidence and limits separately.
