# Payment allocation

Status: implemented and connected locally through the production-built browser, API and isolated MongoDB. No staging or production acceptance. The existing production goal and all M3–M6 gates remain open.

Finance opens **Pemeriksaan pembayaran**, selects the submitted declaration, loads its accepted verification, then chooses **Muat saldo alokasi**. The screen shows the canonical bank receipt's received, allocated, refund-reserved and available amounts, and the selected issued invoices' current paid, credited, outstanding, payment and aging states. **Cari invoice lain pembeli ini** loads eligible issued invoices with an outstanding balance from the same buying organization, fulfillment organization, shop and channel. Every underlying scope is checked before paging; the browser cannot supply a broader party filter. Fully paid or fully credited invoices are omitted, and an empty page can still have a next-page cursor. Add the intended invoice, select it explicitly, enter positive IDR amounts, state the reason and review the resulting invoice balances and unapplied funds before saving. Discovery does not reserve funds, post accounting or change invoices.

Allocation requires its own `payment.allocate` grant, the underlying invoice/verification/bank read rights, current scoped membership and a verified session with MFA within five minutes. The original declaration maker, verification checker and bank importer/checker must still have valid person authority; the allocator must be independent of the declaration maker. Recovering an original operation also rechecks present access and person authority. No role, grant or approval is automatically installed.

Migration 41 follows the preserved 1–40 history. It creates strict `paymentAllocationPolicies`, `paymentAllocations`, `paymentReceiptBalances` and `paymentInvoiceBalances` collections and uniqueness constraints. It rejects existing unexplained business records, unsafe indexes/counters and orphan effects. It seeds no approved procedure or live chart. The operating owner must provide the approved scope, procedure/approval references and suspense/receivables mappings through the existing operational-input gate. Suspense must match the original receipt's approved mapping; receivables must match each issued invoice's original approved mapping.

One Mongo transaction records the immutable allocation, receipt/invoice balance revisions, accounting journal, audit, outbox and actor-scoped idempotency receipt. The journal debits existing suspense and credits receivables on the current Jakarta business date under the accounting book/period controls. It never adds cash or mutates the original invoice or bank receipt. Both receipt and invoice versions are checked, including simultaneous allocations using separate declarations or receipts. Amounts and IDs remain exact strings across the browser/API boundary. The review binding canonicalizes monetary scale, so equivalent values such as `1000` and `1000.00` mean the same reviewed amount.

The resolved suspense and receivables account identities/codes are pinned to their original receipt and invoice postings. A later chart version cannot silently reuse the same mapping label for a different account. Such an allocation fails and rolls back; an authorized accounting reclassification process must resolve that change first.

The owner verifies immutable source/provenance chains and persisted effects before exposing available balances. Missing heads/events/audits/operation receipts or surviving orphan accounting must not reopen funds. Refund reservations and approved credits participate in the arithmetic, but their independent writers, reversals, cases and payout workflow remain subsequent M3 work; no fixture value is a production refund or credit.

## Linked partial corrections

Migration 52 adds `paymentAllocationCorrectionPolicies` and immutable `paymentAllocationCorrections`. Migrations 1–51 and their existing validators are preserved. Run it with the financial writer quiescent under the existing migration/restore gate. The preflight validates populated original allocations against their actual payment verification, bank import/confirmation/physical identity, original cash journal, issued invoice/shipment/stock anchors, accounting journal, operation effects, shared ID counter and canonical balance heads. It refuses unexplained heads/effects, unsafe target rules or indexes and nonempty new targets. Compatible stronger operator validators survive installation and empty-target resume. This local preflight does not establish production migration, restore or provider acceptance.

The operating owner must supply a separately approved correction policy for the original receipt scope, with the same approved receivables/suspense mappings, current approval references and `correctionProcedureReference`. No procedure, policy or permission is seeded for real use. Corrections require both `payment.allocate` and `journal.reverse`, all underlying financial source read permissions, five-minute MFA, and current verified person authority. The correcting person must be independent of both the original allocator and the payment declaration maker, including when two accounts belong to one person. Original-key recovery rechecks these current controls.

From **Pemeriksaan pembayaran**, load the accepted verification and **Muat alokasi asli**. History is scoped to that verification and bank receipt, with older-page navigation. Select the specific original allocation, select invoice lines, enter positive correction amounts and an audit reason, then inspect and explicitly confirm the frozen procedure, original allocation and resulting balances. Its invoice choices come from that exact original allocation, including invoices not named in the buyer's declaration. The available correction is limited by each original allocated amount minus all prior linked corrections and by its current paid balance. An unrelated allocation cannot increase that original capacity. Readers can inspect the history without command controls; the server independently enforces the actual grants and scopes.

One transaction records the linked correction, debit to receivables/credit to unapplied suspense, exact revision changes in the existing receipt/invoice heads, audit, outbox and actor-scoped receipt. The original allocation, bank receipt, original journal and invoice remain intact. A partial correction is a separate compensating posting; it does not pretend to fully reverse the original journal. It neither touches bank/cash nor approves credit, reserves a refund, records a payout or claims a buyer refund. Corrections and allocations race against the same canonical heads. All historical corrections must reconcile, and missing correction effects or source anchors fail closed.

If a reply is lost, **Periksa hasil** reads the exact `billing.payment.allocation.correct` operation under its original `pc.<verification>.<receipt>.<original-allocation>.<uuid>` key. Only actor, operation, key and timestamp are retained in the browser. Unknown, denied, malformed or late results keep the reference; there is no automatic resend, ordinary-allocation fallback or manual close. Reconnect and recover the original attempt before another financial command. Future credit/refund entitlement must combine any required correction, credited amount and refund reservation atomically, so money cannot become spendable between entitlement steps. That workflow remains unfinished M3 work.

If a response is lost, the browser retains only an actor/operation/key/time reference. Use **Periksa hasil** with that original key after reconnecting. An unknown result remains unresolved; do not create another allocation to guess the outcome. A stale preview requires a fresh balance review. If provenance cannot be established, stop that allocation and involve the reconciliation owner. Policy, person, MFA or session expiry after result persistence aborts the transaction before commit. Closed accounting periods and audit/outbox failures also roll back all financial effects.

Local test artifacts are under the ignored `tmp/production/ocr-results/` directory; the single progress ledger records which runs passed or failed. Synthetic bank access, operational approvals, Auth0 sessions and OCR fixtures provide local workflow evidence only. Actual provider acceptance, migration/restore, one-writer cutover and the authorized branch pilot remain required at M6.
