# Evidence-bound payment verification

Finance can connect a submitted customer declaration to an independently confirmed bank receipt in `/billing/payment-reviews`. The decision binds the exact original, immutable OCR result and human corrections or an approved manual outage review, confirmed bank transaction and an approved payer-verification procedure. It establishes a payer claim on existing cash. It neither posts cash again nor reduces an invoice balance. Allocation uses the separate [canonical allocation workflow](payment-allocation.md).

## Authority and operational inputs

Migration 40 adds empty strict `paymentVerificationPolicies`, `paymentVerifications` and `paymentReceiptParties` collections. Applied migrations 1–39 stay unchanged. It creates no real policy, person proof, grant, customer balance or fixture. Preflight rejects unexpected business rows, related orphan effects, unsafe indexes/retention/options and invalid allocator state before target changes. Compatible stronger validators are preserved, and interrupted empty installation can resume.

Use the existing scoped `payment.review.read` and `invoice.read` permissions, plus `payment.verify` for decisions and original-account recovery. Verification now requires MFA completed within five minutes. Selecting a receipt also requires `bank.receipt.read`, `bank.statement.read`, `receiving.account.read` and `receiving.account.details.read` in the fulfillment scope. No role pack receives these grants automatically. The current verified person must differ from the original declaration maker and evidence uploader; a different login is insufficient. Unknown, expired or equivalent person evidence blocks the decision.

The controlled payer policy identifies the exact fulfillment organization/shop/channel, approved payer-checking procedure, responsible Finance owner, approval provenance and validity. There is no browser endpoint to create this policy. The receiving-bank channel and Finance-owner decision remains pending in the [decision ledger](../quality/report-decisions.md). A typed reference or matching sender name cannot establish this operational approval or payer identity. This workflow records an accountable human check; it does not connect to an external bank or establish payer identity automatically.

## Review and save

1. Open an explicitly submitted declaration. Compare its selected invoices and declared transfer against **Lihat bukti asli**, immutable OCR and the separately recorded corrections, or the approved manual outage review below. Evidence must remain privately stored, validated, pinned to that submission and eligible for current access.
2. Load the current verification result. For an unverified declaration, select one actual confirmed bank receipt. The server verifies its original confirmation, receiving-account version, immutable statement row, audit/receipt/outbox provenance and canonical cash journal. Full receiving-account scope must match the declaration's fulfillment scope.
3. The declared transfer amount, corrected image amount, IDR currency and full receiving-account number must agree exactly. Unknown or masked critical facts block this route. Record a reason for each surfaced date, recipient, bank, reference or time discrepancy. An imported booking date does not supply a verified transfer time or timezone.
4. Follow the approved independent payer procedure for the displayed buying organization, record its evidence reference, and explicitly attest to reviewing the exact image, bank receipt and payer. No checkbox is preselected. Review the frozen declaration/receipt/amount/party and reason before saving.
5. A single transaction records the immutable decision, first receipt-to-party anchor, required audit, original operation receipt and metadata-only outbox event. The reviewed declaration/evidence/OCR/correction and bank histories are fenced with current scope/session/grants/person/policy authority. Corrections cannot race a verification of the old review; accepted correction history becomes fixed for that decision. Time-sensitive authority and the held original-read window are checked through transaction completion.

One declaration has one accepted verification. Further declarations from the same buying organization and fulfillment scope may identify the same canonical receipt, with the original payer claim's complete history validated. They create no extra cash or spending balance. A conflicting party is refused. The allocation owner consumes the receipt's single available balance across every linked declaration; summing declarations would double-count funds. Explicit reversal/replacement and the remaining duplicate-investigation work are still required.

## Approved OCR outage review

Migration 42 installs empty strict `paymentManualReviewPolicies` and `paymentManualReviews` collections. It extends the payment decision validator with an explicit manual-source format (schema 2), retaining the original OCR format (schema 1), prior migration receipts and compatible additional operator constraints. No applied migration is rewritten and no real policy or grant is seeded. Before deployment, quiesce all writers, back up and verify restore, run the explicit migration and confirm its receipt. API/worker startup never installs these tables. Interrupted empty installation, including an already-extended custom validator, can resume. Unsafe rows, orphan manual effects, indexes, retention/options or weakened decision validation stop preflight before target changes.

The named Operations/Finance owner must approve the exact fulfillment/shop/channel scope, procedure, provenance, validity, eligible failure codes, reviewer authority and response-time rules under [D06](../quality/report-decisions.md). No screen creates that operational approval. `payment.review.manual` is a separate explicit scoped permission with five-minute MFA; it is added to no role automatically. The actual reviewer must be a currently verified person independent of the declaration maker and evidence uploader. An independent bank review is still required before payer verification; new allocation also checks the original manual reviewer's current person evidence.

1. Load **Muat hasil OCR**. The exception panel is available only for an actual recorded retry/dead attempt with `ocr_unavailable`, `ocr_timeout` or `ocr_invalid_output`, permitted by the current approved policy. Queued/running/successful work, missing originals, malware and security/integrity failures cannot use this path.
2. Open **Lihat bukti asli**, then **Muat pengecualian terbaru**. The server holds and hash-verifies the same pinned original, checks its current clean eligibility and validates the actual failed attempt/read provenance. The owning transaction fences the failed job against a competing worker claim; it never cancels the worker, marks OCR successful or manufactures extracted values.
3. Record only visible facts. Leave unknown fields empty. Explicitly attest to reviewing the original, enter the exception reason and review the frozen confirmation before saving. Submission, failure, policy and prior review revision must still match. Immutable consecutive versions, audit, operation receipt and metadata-only event commit together; saving creates no cash, verification or invoice balance change.
4. Follow the normal bank/payer verification procedure with the displayed **manual** source. Critical amount, currency and full receiving-account facts must match exactly; unknown critical values block verification. The original failed attempt and manual version stay bound to the decision even if OCR later recovers. Historical reads validate that original history without reopening image bytes or treating a new OCR result as its replacement.

After an uncertain save, **Periksa hasil** checks the original actor's recorded result with current permission/person/session/MFA. The browser stores only actor, operation, key and time; it never resends or closes the review. A changed body cannot reuse the original key. A fresh manual version remains a deliberate action, not an automatic retry.

## Selecting recovered OCR before verification

If a manual exception was recorded and OCR later succeeds, Finance can select **Hasil OCR dan koreksi petugas** under **Sumber isian untuk pemeriksaan**, then inspect the bank receipt again. The default retains the saved manual review; an expired or changed manual procedure never silently switches the source. Explicit manual selection requires that saved review and its current matching approved procedure. Explicit OCR selection requires the actual successful extraction and current correction history; it cannot manufacture recovery while OCR is queued, running or failed.

Changing the source clears the displayed preview, confirmation, payer reference, reasons and attestations. The chosen review's existing binding is checked again against the current source, bank receipt, corrections and payer procedure at commit. A stale or different binding creates no verification. Verification write bodies and historical OCR/manual decision formats remain unchanged. An already accepted manual decision retains its original manual version after OCR recovery; this choice only applies before verification. Both earlier manual/failure records and later OCR/corrections remain immutable. Current scope, original eligibility, fresh MFA, person independence and bank/payer review remain mandatory.

Fresh five-minute MFA and currently verified independent maker/reviewer people are required. Taking responsibility grants no other authority.

2. Choose claim/take-over, release, rejection or a request for replacement, enter the internal reason and inspect the frozen confirmation. For rejection or replacement, also enter **Penjelasan untuk pelanggan**. This separate text is shown to the original buyer; it is never copied from the internal reason. Explain the necessary correction and omit internal notes or account data. A current authorized reviewer may deliberately take over or release an assignment. Source binding and the exact follow-up revision must still match at commit.
3. Save once. Immutable history, audit, receipt and a metadata-only pending event commit together. Rejection or replacement closes fresh verification, correction and manual-observation writes on this original submission. Historical decisions/results remain readable; cash, allocations and invoice balances stay intact.
4. After a dropped response, use **Periksa hasil** with the original account/key. This is lookup-only, never a resend or closure of an unknown operation. The original result remains recoverable after a later owner action; current permission, MFA and person checks still apply.

A separately enabled worker can make an in-app notice available on the original buyer's private account; see [outbox consumers](outbox-consumers.md). External notification channels remain pending. A replacement request does not itself create new evidence, edit the old submission, send an external message or authorize a refund. Complete the remaining connected flows and approved operational inputs before real use.

## Scoped review queue

The workspace's **Pengajuan masuk** list shows each submitted declaration's current follow-up, deciding/assigned account, server-snapshot age and recorded OCR state. **Tampilan halaman ini** filters the loaded authorized page by unassigned, assigned, OCR attention or closed follow-up. The filter does not search hidden records or return a total; **Pengajuan lebih lama** advances the original unfiltered cursor. Reload to obtain a new snapshot after an action. Closed follow-up retains the account responsible for that decision.

Discrepancy cues compare retained source-bound OCR and human corrections with the immutable buyer declaration. Unknown amount/date/currency stays unknown; quality warnings and recorded human corrections remain visible. These summaries return no extracted values, receiving-account facts or internal staff reasons. Stored OCR results do not certify the current original-file security verdict or funds received. Open the declaration and follow the independent bank/verification procedure. Missing submission, processing receipt, extraction or correction history denies the summary rather than presenting a clean result.

Queue reads require current review and invoice permissions for every constituent scope, expected actor, same-origin CSRF and audited private access, with final grant/session fences. Viewing never grants assignment, correction, bank review or financial authority.

For closed follow-up, **Notifikasi aplikasi** shows the actual source event's pending, running, retry, failed or available state. **Tersedia pada akun pembeli** requires the worker's exact durable receipt, not merely a completed outbox row. It proves private in-app availability, not reading, email delivery, bank acceptance or refund authority. Missing or inconsistent source/receipt history denies the read. Failed work needs operator investigation; the screen never resets attempts, replays a dead event or sends an external message. Approved escalation and other notification channels remain unfinished.

## Exact duplicate hints

Use **Periksa kecocokan pengajuan** in the submitted declaration to compare the exact original-file SHA-256 and the buyer's exact reference. **Berkas gambar asli identik** and **Referensi yang dinyatakan pembeli sama** are separate advisory reasons. Blank references remain unknown; references are not fuzzy-matched or promoted to canonical bank identity. Open a comparison only through the authorized detail link and inspect its actual bank evidence.

The private, audited lookup binds the displayed declaration/submission/evidence revision, applies both review and invoice scope to every constituent before matching or pagination, and exposes no hidden counts, hashes, raw reference values, images or account data. Pages contain at most 25 matches. Missing matches do not certify uniqueness; cropped/recompressed images and other similarity signals remain pending calibrated review. It never approves/rejects a declaration, posts cash, allocates money or changes an invoice balance. No new role grant or migration is needed for these existing immutable sources.

## Uncertain responses and historical access

The browser retains only actor, operation, key and time. **Periksa hasil** performs an original-account lookup under current scoped authority and fresh MFA. It never resends, allocates, deletes or closes an unknown decision. Changed bodies cannot reuse a committed key. After a known transaction conflict, an intentional same-key retry can recover the original result. Missing decision, payer anchor, approval metadata, bank journal, submitted evidence/OCR/correction or audit/outbox history is denied, never repaired by retry.

Historical decision access checks current read authority and the accepted immutable graph. It does not reopen or return image bytes merely to report an earlier decision, and does not require today's policy to reapprove the original decision. Current person independence is still required for protected receipt recovery. Reading a historical decision never permits another financial effect.

Accounts, sessions, approved procedures/person records and OCR-engine output in this new workflow fixture are synthetic. These checks do not establish OCR-model acceptance, a live Auth0 session, bank connectivity, staging or production acceptance.

No purchase, live message, bank transfer or real-data cutover is authorized by this runbook. Track status in the [single progress ledger](../quality/production-progress.md).
