# Independent bank receipt confirmation

Finance uses `/billing/bank-receipts` to inspect a staged private bank source, enter independently observed credit facts, review the effect, and confirm a canonical receipt. This records cash once. It does not identify the payer, approve a customer payment declaration, reduce invoice receivables, allocate cash, or initiate a bank transfer.

## Operational inputs and authority

Migration 39 creates empty `bankReceiptPolicies`, `bankReceiptAccounts`, `bankReceipts` and `bankReceiptConfirmations` collections with strict validation and unique identities. It installs no account, policy, person authority, grant, balance or fixture. Applied migrations 1–38 are unchanged. The receiving-account/source/Finance-owner decision in the [decision ledger](../quality/report-decisions.md) is still pending.

An approved receipt policy supplies the physical receiving-account identity, canonical bank code, stable bank transaction identity domain, exact fulfillment scope, cash and unidentified/unallocated-cash mapping keys, independently established checking channel, named Finance owner, approval provenance and validity interval. These are controlled deployment inputs, with no browser policy-writing endpoint. The checking channel must be independently obtained and actually usable by the reviewer. A source reference or attestation typed into this screen cannot establish that approval. The current implementation records a human bank observation; it does not fetch a bank feed or verify an external bank login.

The approved account version and export profile must also be current. Actual bank samples must establish that transaction IDs are stable and unique in the approved identity domain for a physical account. Do not create multiple bank aliases, identity domains or accounting owners for the same physical account to evade reconciliation. The first receipt permanently anchors the account's domain and scope; changing that combination requires a separately reviewed reconciliation/migration process, which is not implemented by this command.

The existing Accounting owner must have the correct active book, chart mappings and an open booking period. Receipt dates come from the independently matched bank booking date, never a browser-selected substitute posting date. Closed periods require the approved correction/reopening process; this screen cannot reopen them or move transactions to a convenient date.

Assign `bank.receipt.read` and, separately, `bank.receipt.confirm`, together with scoped `bank.statement.read`, `receiving.account.read` and `receiving.account.details.read`. Confirmation and its recovery require fresh MFA. Finance suggestions include these permissions; Finance v1 packs and the six role templates are not automatically expanded. Technical administration does not grant receipt authority.

## Connected workflow

1. Select an explicitly staged source. Its original file, profile, receiving-account version, parser output and recorder provenance must remain intact. The statement owner authorizes and fences this history in the same transaction as its financial consumer.
2. Select an incoming row with a stable bank identity. Debit rows, absent identities and identities repeated within a file remain unresolved. Payer names and free-text references never serve as transfer identity. Distinct bank IDs may legitimately share a reference.
3. The reviewer must be a currently verified person independent of the original importer, including after account/role changes or a later finding that two accounts belong to the same person. Previously unknown importer evidence must become valid through the approved person process before it can support confirmation.
4. Read the approved independent bank channel and enter the observed account number, exact transaction ID, bank booking date and exact IDR credit amount. These fields start empty. All four must match the immutable source exactly. Record the bank observation reference, reason and explicit attestation, then inspect the frozen confirmation.
5. On the first confirmation, a unique canonical receipt, immutable independent decision, account-domain anchor, actual balanced cash/suspense journal, audit, operation receipt and metadata-only outbox effects commit atomically. The Accounting owner fences the book and period. No separate source-only financial writer exists.
6. A later overlapping or renamed import with the same physical account/domain/transaction ID links to the existing receipt after an independent review of that source. It posts no additional cash. A different amount, booking date or scope for the same identity is blocked as a conflict. Later approved account versions do not create a new transfer identity.
7. Reopen the private receipt or source linkage using current scoped access. The browser stores only actor, operation, key and time for recovery. After a lost response, **Periksa hasil** looks up the original result. Unknown outcomes cannot be closed as failed or resolved by creating a new key. A bounded, known transaction conflict may require a deliberate same-key retry after the competing writer finishes; the unique source and receipt checks still apply.

Every confirmation binds the exact import/row, policy revision, approved account/profile snapshot, observed facts and original participants. Policy/profile, actor/session, scope/grants, current verified-person evidence and fresh MFA are checked at the final transaction boundary. Historical receipt recovery preserves the original account/policy after later changes while rechecking current caller and person authority. Missing or changed source, receipt, approval metadata, journal, anchor, audit or required outbox provenance is denied rather than reconstructed by a retry.

## Remaining release and reconciliation work

The current connected slice supports exact stable-ID credits only. Missing/colliding identities require a future explicit adjudication route; staff must not invent an ID, alter an original file or start another namespace. Cross-declaration duplicate warnings, evidence-bound payer/declaration matching, partial/multi-invoice/overpayment allocation, allocation concurrency, reversals, supplier flows and returns/refunds remain separate required work. The cash/suspense journal by itself does not make money available to settle an arbitrary invoice.

Local rehearsals use production-built Next, HTTPS, .NET and real isolated MongoDB with explicitly synthetic bank policies, people and accounting approvals. They are not bank/provider acceptance. Actual channel/operator/business approval, production migration and restore rehearsal, deployed API/worker/Mongo/Auth0/private storage connections, full release validation and the approved branch pilot remain mandatory. No real message, financial transfer, purchase or data cutover is authorized by this runbook. See the [single progress ledger](../quality/production-progress.md).
