### Independent financial approval

The finance desk loads actual received return batches and consumes the exact immutable financial request. `billing.return.financial.approve` is a separate permission/person receipt: `credit.approve`, or `refund.approve` plus `credit.approve` for a refund with an invoice credit. Current case/invoice read and actual funding-history read scopes remain required. Commands, preview and recovery require five-minute provider-verified MFA and independent current person evidence for the financial requester, original customer and selected funding makers. Receiving, inspecting or proposing a physical resolution does not itself require a different financial approver from every past operator. Historical balance readers verify permanent past approval proof without requiring past staff to retain current approval grants.

The supported explicitly approved policy route credits exactly the stored original net/tax/charge components. A credit without funding release may apply only where current paid plus credited plus this credit fits the issued invoice. A refund names actual allocation links and a positive paid amount, at most the credit, current paid funds and each original allocation's remaining capacity. Selected links can span receipts; every affected receipt advances once. The unpaid part credits original AR; released paid funds become the approved refund liability. Receipt allocated decreases while reserved-for-refund increases by the same amount, so spendable funds do not increase. Original cash, invoices, allocations and journals remain immutable. This command records no payout, tax-document issuance, inventory valuation, quarantine release or case closure.

Credit/refund posting, canonical invoice/receipt heads, original-link release history, person receipt, audit, idempotency and metadata outbox commit together. Allocation and partial-correction readers consume that same combined chain. Deleted or inconsistent request, approval, money head, original journal or required effect denies access and cannot recreate capacity. Original-component and AR account identities, the explicitly pinned liability account and open-period controls are checked after posting; any mismatch rolls back the entire command.

Recovery uses only `fa.<caseId>.<returnId>.<credit|refund>.<uuid>` for the original actor and `billing.return.financial.approve`. Uncertain, denied, malformed or late lookup retains the reference with no automatic resend, alternate owner fallback or manual close. The review is frozen before consent; changing the input requires another server preview. Offline dispatch is blocked.
