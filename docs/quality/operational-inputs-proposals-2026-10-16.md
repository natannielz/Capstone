# Turning the Unit Toko SOP into eight decisions

The shop's own SOP already settles most of *who does what*, but not *who may approve, what must be kept, or where the system runs*. Those gaps are what the eight gates D01–D08 must close, and two of the owner's wishes need to change before they can be adopted. **The 30-day automatic hard delete is unlawful for anything that is a bookkeeping record**: UU KUP Pasal 28(11) and UU 8/1997 Pasal 11 require books, supporting documents and their electronic processing results to be kept **10 years, in Indonesia** ([DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1805429/alasan-dokumen-dasar-pembukuan-wajib-disimpan-selama-10-tahun); [pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-8-tahun-1997/pasal-11)). The 30-day purge can therefore apply only to drafts, rejected uploads and other non-records. **"Free hosting" cannot carry this stack in production**: Auth0 Free does not include MFA (Essentials, the cheapest plan that does, costs **$35/month**), Atlas M0 has **no backups and 512 MB**, and the only free VM large enough (Oracle A1) was **cut to 2 OCPU/12 GB in June 2026**, has no Jakarta region and runs only Linux on ARM ([Auth0 Pricing](https://auth0.com/pricing); [Atlas limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/); [InfoQ](https://infoq.com/news/2026/07/oracle-cloud-free-tier-limits/)). The realistic floor is about **$41–70/month** on Linux in Jakarta, or about **$75–90/month** if the Windows build is kept. The owner's Super Admin design (administers everything, does no business work) matches ISO 27001 A.5.3/A.8.2 and should be adopted with a two-person rule and a sealed break-glass account. On payments, the receipt image must never settle an invoice by itself. Settlement should come from the cooperative's own BNI statement, and a BNI eCollection virtual account per division is the cleanest long-term fix for the **~Rp475 million of unidentified funds** sitting in suspense. What remains open is mostly evidence the cooperative alone holds: sample bukti transfer and BNI statements, an OMI SPH, the koperasi's PKP and BNI-pemungut status, and a signed retention schedule.

*Scope and sources.* This report synthesises five research notes (October 2026) with the shop's SOP (cited as SOP BAB I–VII). Facts the owner supplied outside the SOP are cited as "owner's brief": handwritten, unnumbered Surat Jalan, WhatsApp ordering and the Rp475 million suspense balance. People in the SOP diagrams are referred to by role only. This is not legal or tax advice. Every legal point below should be confirmed with the cooperative's accountant or tax adviser before adoption.

## The SOP fixes the roles but leaves approvals, limits and evidence open

The SOP describes a two-system shop. **Sistem OMI** records sembako restock and sales, and **Sistem Smart** records merchandise and selected products such as tumblers, shirts, shopping bags, Le Minerale gallons and "dus 330" (SOP BAB I, II.2.3). Three shop roles carry the work. The **Kepala Toko** decides off-OMI procurement, negotiates with vendors (MCM for shirts and tumblers, Mayora for gallons and dus 330), pays them by DP or in full, runs Pasar Kering purchases with funds requested from the Koperasi, and receives division orders (SOP BAB II, VII.1). The **Staf Toko** receives goods, prepares Surat Jalan, runs the POS, handles returns, keys every transaction into OMI or Smart, performs stock opname, and **pays OMI against the SPH (rincian tagihan)** (SOP BAB VII.2). A single **Pengolah Laporan** merges the OMI and Smart daily reports **by hand** into a printed journal for the Koperasi (SOP BAB V.2, VII.3).

Inside the Koperasi, **Bagian Penagihan** filters the journal, issues the invoice and faktur pajak, checks incoming transfers "one by one" against outstanding items, and traces un-narrated payments manually (SOP BAB VI.1). **Pimpinan Unit** initials (paraf) the month-end report and approves refunds of unidentified funds. **Bagian Akuntansi** posts journals and prepares the financial statements (SOP BAB VI.2). External parties are the **division PICs**, who order "directly or through the marketing WhatsApp group", the shop's **Kurir** (target 10:00, 15–20 drop points a day, two vehicles at month start and end, fuel, tolls and parking reimbursed), the **Kurir OMI** (Monday and Thursday koli deliveries, checks returns at handover), and vendors (SOP BAB III.1, II.1, IV.1).

Several controls are already present. Input into OMI or Smart is **final and cannot be cancelled** (SOP BAB V.1). Unidentified funds are held and refunded only **with Pimpinan Unit approval** after the division confirms (SOP BAB VI.2 note 1). Payments flow through a **centralised transfer system** in which the branch requests approval before head office pays (SOP BAB VI.2 note 2). OMI's faktur pajak for purchases arrives **about two weeks after purchase** (SOP BAB VI.2 note 3). Stock-opname shortages are expensed at HPP and covered by a reserve "with a certain tolerance against turnover", but the SOP never states that tolerance (SOP BAB V.3).

The SOP is silent on the limits and approvals the system needs. It gives no PO approval threshold and no limit on Kepala Toko vendor payments. Nobody reviews the Staf Toko's OMI payments, so the same role receives goods, checks the SPH and pays it. It gives no customer-return rule, no document numbering, no retention rule and no system administration role. It also names no backup for the Pengolah Laporan, a role the SOP itself says rests on one person whose availability the whole reporting chain depends on (SOP BAB VII.3). The gates below fill these holes.

| Gate | SOP already answers | Biggest open decision | Who must provide it |
|---|---|---|---|
| D01 People & identity | Roles and duties (BAB I.F, VII) | Staff roster source, named backups, break-glass holders | Pengurus/Pimpinan Unit, Koperasi HR/secretariat |
| D02 Access rights | Pimpinan Unit approves month-end and refunds | Approval thresholds; Super Admin holders; retention schedule replacing 30-day purge | Pimpinan Unit, Pengurus (decree) |
| D03 Commercial/finance | Invoice and faktur by Penagihan; OMI per-item return windows | PKP status, BNI pemungut status, member/non-member rule, customer refunds | Koperasi tax adviser, Bagian Akuntansi |
| D04 Master data/stock | OMI vs Smart split; Pasar Kering pricing | OMI item codes/exports, HET list, opening stock date | Kepala Toko, OMI/Indogrosir |
| D05 Bank | Manual one-by-one matching; Pimpinan-approved refunds | VA feasibility, statement export format, suspense policy | Bagian Penagihan, BNI relationship manager |
| D06 Evidence/OCR | Signed Surat Jalan as primary proof | Sample proofs; file formats; legal hold owner | Bagian Penagihan, division PICs |
| D07 Hosting | Nothing | Budget ceiling; Windows vs Linux; data residency | Owner/Pengurus |
| D08 Pilot/cutover | Nothing | Pilot division, cutover month, suspense opening list sign-off | Pimpinan Unit, Bagian Akuntansi |

## D01 and D02: separate the people who administer, transact and approve

### D01 — identity should come from the Koperasi's roster, not from Dukcapil

**What the SOP answers.** The internal cast is fixed: Kepala Toko, Staf Toko, Pengolah Laporan, Bagian Penagihan, Pimpinan Unit and Bagian Akuntansi. The external cast is division PICs, the shop Kurir, the Kurir OMI and vendors (SOP BAB I.E–F, III, VI). The SOP does not say where staff identities come from, who verifies them, or who recovers access.

**Proposed answer.** The source of truth for internal staff should be the **Koperasi staff roster**. BNI-assigned staff can carry a BNI employee number confirmed by the Koperasi secretariat. Internal accounts are created only on a Pimpinan-approved request that references a roster ID, and self-registration is never allowed. NIK should **not** be verified through Dukcapil. Private-sector access is limited to a match/no-match answer, requires a PKS/MoU, NDA and SPTJM, and costs **Rp1,000 per NIK** under PP 10/2023 for profit sectors ([Pajak.com](https://www.pajak.com/pajak/verifikasi-nik-di-dukcapil-kena-tarif-rp-1-000/amp/); [Ombudsman](https://ombudsman.go.id/news/download/hanya-akses-verifikasi-ombudsman-anggap-sistem-pemanfaatan-data-kependudukan-aman)). That is disproportionate for a dozen staff. KTP images and full NIK stay in HR records, outside the shop system. Division PICs and couriers should be invite-only **external identities** tied to the division or courier record and verified through the BNI work-email domain. They should never hold internal staff roles.

Every account is named, with no shared POS logins. MFA is mandatory for Super Admin, approvers, billing/payment and accounting roles. A short timeout plus a PIN quick-switch tied to the named user covers the shared POS terminal. Joiners, movers and leavers follow a fixed pattern: a mover loses old roles before gaining new ones, and a leaver is disabled the same day but **never deleted**, because retained records reference the account. Privileged access is reviewed quarterly and business roles semi-annually, which follows ISO 27001 A.8.2/A.5.18 practice ([ISMS.online A.8.2](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/8-2-use-of-privileged-access-rights-2022/)). The exact cadences are industry convention, not a stated rule.

For independent review, the SOP's natural independent approver is Pimpinan Unit, and Bagian Akuntansi is the independent recorder. The opening-balance pack and the annual access review should additionally go to the Koperasi's **Pengawas** or an external accountant. For admin and recovery, there should be **two named Super Admins**, enough to avoid the "impotent admin" lock-out Odoo warns about ([Odoo docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/general/users/access_rights.html)). Add one **break-glass account** whose credentials are split between two officers, for example a Pengurus member and Pimpinan Unit. Its use alerts all admins, it expires automatically, it is rotated after use, and even it cannot transact ([Akeyless](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/break-glass-access-best-practices)).

**What needs a real decision or input.** The Koperasi HR/secretariat must supply a current roster with the employment basis of each shop worker (Koperasi employee or BNI secondee). Pengurus must name the two Super Admins and the break-glass holders. Pimpinan Unit must name a **backup for the Pengolah Laporan** and a backup Staf Toko for POS close. The owner must decide whether division PICs log in at all during the pilot or whether staff key their orders.

**Risks and legal constraints.** UU PDP has been fully binding since **17 October 2024**. Its implementing regulation **PP 33/2026** was set on 16 July 2026 and takes effect **16 January 2027**, but the supervisory agency is still not formed ([Katadata](https://katadata.co.id/finansial/makro/6a9979b7c5745/pp-pelindungan-data-pribadi-berlaku-januari-2027-ini-aturan-baru-data-anak); [Bisnis](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20260913/84/2003698/pembentukan-badan-pdp-dipercepat-implementasi-uu-pdp-dinilai-belum-efektif)). Breaches must be notified within **3×24 hours** (UU PDP Pasal 46) ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-46)). A shop processing staff and B2B contact data is unlikely to hit the Pasal 53 "large scale" DPO triggers ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-53)), but a named privacy PIC is cheap and needed to run that 3×24-hour workflow. The lawful basis for processing is employment or contract for staff and PICs, and legal obligation for tax identity.

### D02 — adopt the Super Admin design, but replace the 30-day purge with a retention schedule

**What the SOP answers.** The SOP gives three approval points: Pimpinan Unit initials the month-end report, approves suspense refunds, and approves (through the Koperasi) Pasar Kering funding and reimbursements (SOP BAB II.4, VI.2). It also treats keyed transactions as final (SOP BAB V.1).

**Proposed role and SoD model.** Roles should be bundles of (document type, action) permissions, checked twice. At **grant time**, toxic combinations are blocked or require a documented exception. At **transaction time**, maker ≠ checker is enforced on the same document. The second check matters because in a shop this small, people will legitimately hold several roles, and ISO A.5.3 accepts activity logs and supervision as compensating controls for small organisations ([ISMS.online A.5.3](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/5-3-segregation-of-duties-2022/)). The standard purchase and cash rules give the matrix: the PO initiator does not approve it, the approver does not pay, and no one person collects, records and reconciles cash ([NJCPA](https://www.njcpa.org/learn/article/2024/09/23/why-segregation-of-duties-is-essential-for-internal-control)).

| Cycle | Conflict to prevent | Proposed rule for Unit Toko |
|---|---|---|
| Smart / Pasar Kering purchase | Kepala Toko negotiates **and** approves **and** pays | Pimpinan Unit approves above a threshold; below it, post-hoc weekly review |
| Vendor master | Same person edits vendor bank account and pays | Second-person verification of any vendor bank change |
| OMI payable | Staf Toko receives, checks SPH **and** pays (SOP BAB VI.2) | Kepala Toko reviews SPH vs receipts/returns before Staf Toko pays (compensating control) |
| Division sale | Order taker also edits delivered qty | Kepala Toko takes order; Staf Toko prepares; PIC signature is the external check |
| Billing | Invoice re-keyed from journal | Invoice generated from the signed delivery record, no re-keying |
| Receipts | Penagihan matches **and** releases suspense refunds | Pimpinan Unit approves refunds and suspense reclassification (already SOP) |
| POS cash | Staf Toko runs till **and** reconciles | Pengolah Laporan reconciles; Kepala Toko reviews |
| Stock | Custodian posts own opname adjustment | Two counters; Kepala Toko/Pimpinan approve adjustments |
| Access | Super Admin grants self a business role | Blocked; second approver required |

**Super Admin, as the owner wants it.** The owner's design is exactly what A.5.3(e/f), "using vs administering applications", and A.8.2 privileged-access guidance recommend ([ISMS.online A.8.2](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/8-2-use-of-privileged-access-rights-2022/)). The proposed rule set has six parts:

1. Super Admin can manage users, roles and configuration and has a read-only audit view. It cannot create, approve, pay or post any business document, and cannot edit logs.
2. Super Admin cannot grant itself any role. Granting any business role to an admin, or any approver, payment or posting role to anyone, needs Pimpinan Unit's second approval.
3. A person who needs both admin and business work gets **two accounts**.
4. Admin actions require re-authentication and land in an append-only log that Super Admin cannot delete. This fits the project's existing provenance-seal work.
5. Super Admin **cannot hard-delete financial records** under any circumstances, which is what makes the retention rule enforceable.
6. Privileged access is reviewed quarterly.

**The 30-day hard-delete conflict.** The owner's wish cannot be applied to records. UU KUP Pasal 28(11) requires books, records and supporting documents, **including results of electronic data processing**, to be kept **10 years in Indonesia**. Pasal 39 attaches criminal sanctions to deliberate non-retention where state revenue can be lost ([DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1805429/alasan-dokumen-dasar-pembukuan-wajib-disimpan-selama-10-tahun)). UU 8/1997 Pasal 11 sets the same 10 years, counted from the **end of the financial year**, for journals, ledgers, bookkeeping evidence and financial-administration supporting data. It requires the company's leadership to adopt a **jadwal retensi** by decree ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-8-tahun-1997/pasal-11); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/jangka-waktu-penyimpanan-dokumen-perusahaan-lt646efc4036ca5)). UU PDP pulls the other way, requiring deletion when the purpose ends (Pasal 43). But Pasal 42–44 each defer to "laws and regulations", and Pasal 44(1)(a) ties destruction to the archive retention schedule ([pasal.id Pasal 44](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-44)). So the tax and company-document rules win for records, and PDP governs only personal data that is not part of a record.

The proposed compromise keeps the owner's 30-day mechanism but changes what it does per class:

| Class | Examples | "Delete" means |
|---|---|---|
| Transient | Drafts never issued, rejected/duplicate uploads, abandoned carts, temp imports | Soft delete → 30-day restore → **automatic purge** (owner's wish, lawful here) |
| Issued record | Invoices, faktur pajak, nota retur, Surat Jalan, POs, OMI SPH/receipts, payment proofs, bank matches, suspense items, refunds, daily/merged reports, journals, opname sheets | **Void/cancel with reason** (number kept). Soft delete = hide → 30-day restore → **archive tier, read-only**, 10 years from fiscal year-end |
| Audit trail / seals | Logs and provenance seals of the above | Kept as long as the record they describe |
| Staff accounts | User records referenced by retained documents | Deactivate; minimise profile fields after exit; keep name/ID link |
| Pure personal data | Phone numbers, KTP images, photos not tied to a record | Delete when purpose ends or on request (PDP Pasal 43–44) |

Final destruction happens only after expiry, through a destruction list approved by Pimpinan Unit and recorded in a **berita acara pemusnahan**, and is skipped while a legal hold (tax audit, dispute) is set. The 10-year rule for Surat Jalan and opname sheets is a conservative reading, not an explicit rule. They are treated as bookkeeping support because the invoice is derived from them.

**What needs a real decision.** Pimpinan Unit must set the **PO and payment approval thresholds** and the opname tolerance the SOP leaves blank. Pengurus must sign the **jadwal retensi** as a decree, as UU 8/1997 Pasal 11 expects. The owner must accept that "hard delete in 30 days" becomes "archive in 30 days" for records.

**Risks.** If the owner keeps the blanket purge, the system would destroy faktur and payment evidence that the tax office can demand for 10 years. If Super Admin can bypass retention, a single compromised admin account becomes an evidence-destruction tool. Banking-grade maker-checker rules from OJK do not legally bind a koperasi retail unit. Shop-sector cooperatives stay under the Ministry of Cooperatives, not OJK ([Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/nilai-aset-21-koperasi-open-loop-yang-telah-dialihkan-ke-ojk-capai-rp-33912-miliar)). The rules here are good practice, not regulatory mandate.

## D03 and D04: tax status and the OMI relationship drive most commercial rules

### D03 — the invoice should be born from the signed delivery, with tax flags per SKU

**What the SOP answers.** Bagian Penagihan creates the invoice and faktur pajak from the merged journal and sends both to the division (SOP BAB VI.1). PPN is "calculated automatically on input into OMI" for Pasar Kering goods (SOP BAB II.4.C). The margin is the difference between buy and sell price. The shop takes cash, credit and voucher at the POS (SOP BAB V.2). OMI returns follow **per-item windows before expiry**: Coca-Cola 3 months, SGM 6, Lactogrow 9, Chiki 1 and Panadol 9. The Kurir OMI inspects returns and hands back non-conforming ones (SOP BAB IV). When stock runs short, staff may substitute a similar item, split quantities evenly across divisions, or defer one division's delivery (SOP BAB III.1).

**Proposed answer on tax.** PPN stays at **12% nominal**. For non-luxury goods it is computed on **DPP nilai lain = 11/12 × price**, which gives an effective **11%**, and the faktur is issued with **code 04** under PMK 131/2024 and PMK 11/2025 ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/review/konsultasi/1807964/tarif-ppn-di-2025-jadinya-11-atau-12-begini-ketentuan-terbarunya); [DDTC code 04](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1807950/ppn-dihitung-dengan-dpp-1112-dari-harga-jual-djp-kode-fakturnya-04)). Staples under **PP 49/2022** (beras, jagung, kedelai, garam, daging, telur, susu segar, buah, sayur, and plain white cane sugar) are BKP **exempt** from PPN. They still need a faktur, with code 08 ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1801517/ingat-beras-masih-masuk-barang-kena-pajak-yang-dibebaskan-dari-ppn); [Pajakku](https://pajakku.com/artikel/pp-492022-atur-fasilitas-ppn-dibebaskan-dan-tidak-dipungut-cek-poin-pentingnya-disini)). Since 1 January 2025 faktur are created in **Coretax** under PMK 81/2024 ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1806618/isi-bab-ketentuan-teknis-pelaksanaan-coretax-djp-dalam-pmk-812024)). Each SKU therefore carries a `ppn_treatment` (taxable 04 / exempt 08 / non-BKP) and a 6-digit Coretax goods code ([DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1807234/coretax-pkp-bisa-cantumkan-kode-barangjasa-6-digit-ke-faktur-pajak)). Each invoice line stores price, DPP nilai lain and PPN.

The PKP obligation starts at **Rp4.8 billion** turnover ([pajak.go.id](https://pajak.go.id/id/berita/pajak-sinjai-jelaskan-relaksasi-batas-waktunya-pengajuan-pkp)). The fact that Penagihan already issues faktur means the **Koperasi** (not the Unit) is evidently a PKP, so the shop's sales are in scope. OMI's late faktur masukan (SOP BAB VI.2) should be tracked as a `pending_faktur_masukan` state per SPH. DJP has clarified that PMK 81/2024 does not restrict crediting to the same period ([DJP KT-08/2025](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-02/KT-08_2025%20Pengkreditan%20Pajak%20Masukan%20Pasca%20Implementasi%20Coretax...pdf)).

**Proposed answer on invoicing.** The invoice should be generated from the **signed, numbered delivery record**, not re-keyed from a printed journal. It should carry the invoice number, the cooperative's account or VA, and the required transfer-narrative format (see D05). A delivered-but-not-invoiced alert should fire at month-end. The sales header needs a `customer_type` of member, non-member or institution. Permenkop 2/2024 and SAK EP (effective 1 January 2025) require **member and non-member transactions to be separated**, and only member-transaction SHU is distributed as jasa anggota ([IAI/Kemenkop](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Materi_Permenkop%202%20thn%202024_Kemenkop%20dan%20UKM.pdf); [tirto.id](https://tirto.id/mengenal-sisa-hasil-usaha-shu-cara-pembagian-dan-contohnya-gnKJ)).

**Proposed answer on pricing.** Sell prices are dated price-list rows with an approver. Pasar Kering repricing is proposed by Staf Toko and approved by Kepala Toko. A **"cost > HET" flag** replaces automatic cost-plus mark-up for capped goods (see D04).

**Proposed answer on returns and refunds.** OMI return windows become per-SKU supplier-policy data, never hard-coded. Customer returns and refunds, which the SOP never covers, should be allowed only against an original receipt or Surat Jalan, approved by Kepala Toko, and issued as a **nota retur** linked to the original faktur. PMK 81/2024 moved electronic nota retur rules into Coretax, though article numbers are from a secondary source ([jurnal.id](https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/pencatatan-retur-penjualan-otomatis/)). Refunds of unidentified funds keep the SOP's Pimpinan Unit approval.

**What needs a real decision or input.** The Koperasi tax adviser must confirm three things. First, **PKP status**. Second, whether **BNI acts as pemungut PPN** for these purchases, because if it does the faktur code and the cash expected both change. Third, whether divisions withhold PPh on payment, which would create systematic short payments. Bagian Akuntansi must decide whether a BNI division purchase counts as a member transaction (divisions are not members, employees are) under the AD/ART. Kepala Toko must state the customer-return policy. Bagian Penagihan must provide a **sample invoice and faktur** as the template.

**Risks.** Faktur rules changed repeatedly in 2025, and sources disagree on the scope of faktur code 10 ([online-pajak](https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-efaktur-ppn/kode-faktur-pajak/)). The system should prepare faktur data for Coretax import rather than claim to issue faktur itself. Credit sales to members (potong gaji) are mentioned in the POS recap but not described. They need their own receivable flow before go-live.

### D04 — treat OMI as a system of record to reconcile with, not replace

**What the SOP answers.** OMI auto-restocks on **Monday and Thursday** in cable-tied koli with a struk listing koli count and contents. Staf Toko counts koli against the struk, signs, stores and keys receipt into OMI (SOP BAB II.1). DDO special orders are keyed in OMI's back office, emailed as a file and confirmed by phone, and shortfalls are covered through Pasar Kering (SOP BAB II.2). Smart merchandise comes from MCM and Mayora (SOP BAB II.3). Pasar Kering buys from Pasar Pagi, Indogrosir or others, priced against HPP (SOP BAB II.4). Stock opname uses a scanner/DCP or rack-ordered sheets, daily or weekly (SOP BAB V.3). There is one shop outlet plus 15–20 division drop points a day (SOP BAB III.1).

**Who OMI is.** "OMI" is **Outlet Mitra Indogrosir**, a franchise programme of **PT Inti Cakrawala Citra (Indogrosir)** aimed explicitly at company and agency cooperatives. Partners get **loaned POS software**, a planogram and pricing guidance, and may also sell non-Indogrosir goods ([detikFinance](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-6391805/mau-buka-indomaret-tapi-pakai-nama-sendiri-begini-cara-dan-biayanya); [waralabagram](https://waralabagram.com/franchise-omi/)). The supplier master should hold that legal entity and its NPWP, because it issues the faktur masukan. No public source documents DDO, SPH, payment terms or OMI data exports. Those live in the franchise contract.

**Proposed answer.** OMI remains the **system of record** for sembako PLUs and costs. The new system holds a **crosswalk** from OMI code to Smart code to internal SKU and reconciles daily against OMI totals rather than duplicating OMI's POS. SKUs have zero or more GTINs and packaging levels (unit, pack, case), each with its own GTIN and `contains_qty`. GTINs are stored as **14-digit zero-padded strings** with a mod-10 check. GTIN-14 is for cases and is not scanned at POS, and only the brand owner, through GS1 Indonesia, may assign GTINs ([Wikipedia: GTIN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Trade_Item_Number); [GS1 Canada](https://gs1ca.org/documents/standards/GS1CA-What-is-GTIN-14-and-How-to-Use-it-EN.pdf)). Items without a barcode get internal PLUs. GS1 restricted-circulation prefixes (02/20–29) are the standard option for printed in-store codes, but this was not verified from a primary GS1 page.

**HET correction.** The SOP describes HET as a **"batas bawah"** (lower bound) for Pasar Kering pricing (SOP BAB II.4.C). Legally, HET is a **ceiling**. Retailers must sell MinyaKita at or below **Rp15,700/L** (Permendag 18/2024) and must be registered in SIMIRAH, with sanctions for unregistered sellers above HET ([Bisnis](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20240817/12/1791749/tok-harga-minyakita-naik-jadi-rp15700-per-liter-usai-hut-ri); [Bisnis](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250113/12/1831175/kemendag-endus-pengecer-nakal-jual-minyakita-di-atas-het-tak-terdaftar-di-sistem)). Medium rice HET rose to **Rp13,500/kg** in August 2025 and is zone-based; Jakarta is zone 1 ([Bisnis](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260107/12/1942252/harga-pangan-hari-ini-7-januari-harga-beras-turun-minyakita-masih-mahal)). No confirmed 2026 sugar HET was found. HET must therefore be **dated, zoned reference data** with a regulation reference, and the price workflow must **block or warn above the cap**. Branded goods such as Coca-Cola, SGM, Panadol and Chiki have only manufacturer RRPs, which should sit in a separate `manufacturer_rrp` field.

**Opening stock.** Do a full count on the cutover date. For OMI-managed sembako, OMI's on-hand quantity is the reference, and count-versus-OMI variances are recorded as reconciling items rather than overwriting OMI.

**What needs real input.** Kepala Toko or Staf Toko must provide **an SPH sample, a DDO email file, a delivery struk, a retur form**, and an OMI back-office export if one exists. Without these the reconciliation cannot be designed. Kepala Toko must provide the **current Smart item list** and confirm whether the shop sells MinyaKita, rice or LPG. The owner must decide whether Smart is retired or kept.

**Risks.** OMI's software is on loan under contract, so scraping or automating it may breach the franchise terms. HET-capped goods bought at street price can be **negative-margin**, and the SOP's "lower bound" reading could put the shop above a legal cap.

## D05 and D06: the bank statement settles, the receipt only claims

### D05 — move the narrative out of the critical path

**What the SOP answers.** Divisions pay by transfer after receiving the invoice and faktur. Penagihan checks each credit "one by one" against outstanding items, traces un-narrated payments by hand, holds unidentified funds, and refunds them only after division confirmation and Pimpinan Unit approval (SOP BAB VI.1–2). Payments pass through a centralised approval-then-head-office-pays system (SOP BAB VI.2 note 2). Per the owner's brief, roughly **Rp475 million** of unidentified funds now sits in suspense. The SOP also does not say whether the centralised system belongs to the payer (BNI head office) or the Koperasi, and that is worth confirming because it determines who can supply remittance advice.

**Transfer types to model.** Four rails matter, plus QRIS at the till:

| Rail | Speed and hours | Limit | Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intrabank BNI overbooking | Real-time | — | Usually free; likely most division payments |
| BI-FAST | Real-time, 24/7 | **Rp250 juta**/transaction | **≤Rp2,500** |
| SKNBI | Batch, business hours | Up to Rp1 miliar | — |
| RTGS | Real-time, business hours | Large values | — |
| QRIS (till) | — | Rp10 juta/transaction | 0% MDR up to Rp100,000 from 1 Oct 2026 |

The BI-FAST figures are confirmed by an April 2026 bank RIPLAY ([CIMB Niaga RIPLAY](https://www.cimbniaga.co.id/content/dam/cimb/tb/2026/riplay/umum/RIPLAY%20Umum_Layanan%20Ver%202.0_BI%20FAST.pdf); [detik](https://finance.detik.com/moneter/d-6817089/bi-fast-adalah-kenali-manfaat-serta-bedanya-dengan-rto-rtgs-dan-sknbi)). Sources conflict on the exact SKN and RTGS hours. The QRIS terms are from [Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/apakah-belanja-pakai-qris-ada-minimal-transaksi-simak-penjelasan-bank-indonesia) and [Infobanknews](https://infobanknews.com/bi-bebaskan-tarif-mdr-qris-transaksi-rp100-ribu-mulai-1-oktober-2026). Matching windows should be same-day for BI-FAST, RTGS and intrabank, and D+1–2 working days for SKN.

**What proofs and statements show.** No bank publishes a receipt specification. Public guidance only confirms that receipts show recipient name, bank, account, amount, date/time, status and a reference "if available", and that the narrative ("Berita" or "catatan") is entered before confirmation ([Bank Mandiri](https://www.bankmandiri.co.id/web/guest/livin/edukasi/cara-transfer-antar-bank); [BCA](https://bca.co.id/en/informasi/Edukatips/2021/12/23/09/28/transfer-antarbank-kini-hanya-rp2500-via-bi-fast)). During 2026, expect both **wondr by BNI** and legacy **BNI Mobile** layouts as BNI winds the old app down ([Bisnis](https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20260915/90/2004256/bni-tutup-fitur-transfer-luar-negeri-di-bni-mobile-mulai-16-oktober-2026)). Divisions paying through **BNIDirect** use a maker/approver token flow, so their "proof" may be a BNIDirect report rather than a phone screenshot ([BNI](https://bni.co.id/en-us/home/bni-news/news/articleid/21807)). BNI mobile mutasi shows sender name, date and amount ([IDX Channel](https://www.idxchannel.com/technology/begini-cara-mengetahui-siapa-yang-transfer-ke-rekening-kita-di-bni-mobile-banking)). BNIDirect offers downloadable statements, but the column layout and export formats (CSV/MT940) are not public. The narrative field length is undocumented and is often truncated on the receiving statement.

**OCR matching proposal.** The core rule is that **an uploaded receipt is a claim, never settlement**. Fake proofs, now AI-generated, are common enough that Polri issued a public warning, and the universal advice is to verify against the recipient's own mutasi ([JatimTIMES](https://jatimtimes.com/baca/336043/20250425/174900/bukti-transfer-palsu-bermodus-ai-marak-beredar-ini-ciri-dan-cara-menghindarinya); [Kompas TV](https://www.kompas.tv/info-publik/619585/begini-cara-mendeteksi-bukti-transfer-palsu-agar-tidak-jadi-korban-penipuan)). Invoice status therefore moves from *Klaim diunggah* to *Terverifikasi mutasi* only on a statement-line match. The checks run in this order:

1. **Status gate.** Reject "Diproses", "Pending", "Terjadwal" or "Gagal", and any future date.
2. **Beneficiary.** The beneficiary must equal the Koperasi account; a mismatch is a hard reject.
3. **Duplicates.** Reject a repeated perceptual hash or a repeated (reference, amount, date) tuple, and let one statement line settle only one claim unless finance splits it.
4. **Amount.** Exact match by default. Flag invoice + fee and invoice − withholding.
5. **Low confidence or tamper.** Low OCR confidence or tamper signals route to a human.

Statement credits are then matched by a ladder: a **VA hit**, an invoice number found in the narrative (fuzzy, truncation-tolerant), an OCR claim plus a statement line, a **unique amount (kode unik)**, sender-name alias plus amount (always human-confirmed), and finally the suspense queue ([BNI Collection Management](https://bni.co.id/en-us/corporate/solusi-wholesale/collection-management); [ION Network](https://blog.ionnetwork.co.id/?p=7789)). The strongest structural fix is a **static BNI eCollection VA per division**, because the payer is identified by the 16-digit number, not by free text. BI-FAST reportedly cannot pay a BNI VA ([Poliupg guide](https://portalukt2026.poliupg.ac.id/Tata%20Cara%20Pembayaran%20BNI%20Virtual%20Account%20(eCollection)%20General_New_ChargeToBiller.pdf)), but BNIDirect can. Because divisions pay through a centralised system, the head-office payment unit should also send **remittance advice** listing the invoices covered by each bulk transfer.

**Suspense proposal.** The Rp475 million is loaded at cutover as an **itemised list**, one line per credit with date, amount, raw sender text and bank reference, under its own account. It is **never netted or written off at cutover**, and it is aged in 0–30, 31–90 and over-90-day buckets. Unclaimed items go out to division finance contacts on a weekly list. Reclassifying an item needs two people, and a write-off needs a Pengurus or RAT decision. New suspense additions are reported separately from the legacy balance, so improvement is visible.

**What needs real input.** Bagian Penagihan should collect **20–50 real bukti transfer** across wondr, BNI Mobile, BNIDirect, ATM and other banks, with account numbers masked, and **one month of BNI statements** in every available export format. The BNI relationship manager must confirm VA availability and fees for the Koperasi, MT940/CSV export, narrative length per rail, and whether divisions can pay a VA through BNIDirect. The Koperasi must confirm who runs the centralised transfer system and whether it can send remittance advice. Pimpinan Unit and the accountant must set the suspense write-off policy, since no Koperasi-specific SAK EP guidance was found.

**Risks.** OCR rules written before samples exist will be wrong, because labels and reference formats are unverified. Kode unik produces "odd" invoice amounts that BNI division finance may reject. A refund paid out of suspense to the wrong division is the classic "kelebihan transfer" scam pattern ([IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/business/economy/waspadai-taktik-penipuan-bukti-transfer-palsu-ini-ciri-cirinya-00-gshdq-hkmpf3)), which is why the Pimpinan approval must remain.

### D06 — evidence is bookkeeping support and inherits the 10-year rule

**What the SOP answers.** The signed **Surat Jalan** is "the main proof" accompanying goods to the division and the basis for input and billing (SOP BAB III.1, V.1). OMI receipts are signed struk (SOP BAB II.1). Returns use a retur form (SOP BAB IV). Per the owner's brief, Surat Jalan are **handwritten and unnumbered** and orders arrive over **WhatsApp**, which means today's evidence chain has no sequence that could reveal a missing document.

**Proposed answer.** Accepted payment-proof formats should be **JPEG/PNG screenshots, PDF (BNIDirect or e-statement) and photographed ATM slips**. The system keeps the original bytes, a SHA-256 hash, upload metadata and the OCR output side by side, never only the extracted text. Thermal ATM slips need deskew and binarisation and will route to manual review more often. Surat Jalan become **system-numbered**, sequential and printed with the number. The signed copy is photographed or scanned back in and linked to its delivery record. A WhatsApp order is re-keyed by staff as an order record with the chat screenshot attached as evidence, until PICs order directly.

**Retention.** Payment proofs, statement matches, Surat Jalan, OMI struk and SPH, retur forms and opname sheets are **bookkeeping evidence**: 10 years from fiscal year-end, kept in Indonesia, and archived rather than purged (see D02). Under KUP Pasal 28(11), electronic results of bookkeeping processing fall under the same rule ([DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1805429/alasan-dokumen-dasar-pembukuan-wajib-disimpan-selama-10-tahun)). Rejected and duplicate uploads are transient and can be purged after 30 days.

**Manual exceptions.** Every OCR failure, low-confidence field or tamper flag goes to an exception queue owned by **Bagian Penagihan**. The resolver records a reason code. Overrides above a value threshold need Pimpinan Unit, and the person who uploaded a claim can never approve it.

**What needs real input.** The same sample corpus as D05 is needed. The cooperative must also supply **a scan of a current handwritten Surat Jalan and a retur form** so the numbered template mirrors what PICs already sign, and must decide who holds **legal-hold authority** (proposed: Bagian Akuntansi with Pimpinan Unit).

**Risks.** Evidence stored outside Indonesia sits awkwardly with the KUP "kept in Indonesia" wording. Whether a foreign-hosted primary with an Indonesian copy satisfies it was not resolved, so keep at least one Indonesian-resident copy. Receipt images also contain third-party personal data (payer names, partial account numbers), so masking and access restriction apply.

## D07: "free" works for a pilot, not for ten years of tax evidence

**What the SOP answers.** Nothing. The SOP predates the system.

**The gap, quantified.** The stack is a .NET API plus worker, a MongoDB replica set (needed for transactions), Auth0 with MFA, Tesseract/ffmpeg OCR currently built for Windows, and Next.js on Vercel. No free tier covers it:

| Component | Free option | Why it fails or is limited | Cheapest compliant step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Auth0 MFA | Free, 25,000 MAU | Plan table: "Pro MFA Factors — Free: Not included"; using them on Free risks a tenant block ([Auth0 Pricing](https://auth0.com/pricing); [Auth0 Community](https://community.auth0.com/t/clarification-on-pro-mfa-warning-while-using-totp-in-free-plan/189505)) | **Essentials $35/month** |
| MongoDB | Atlas M0 | 3-node replica set but **512 MB, 100 ops/s, no backups**, fixed 8.0, idle pause ([Atlas limits](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/atlas/reference/free-shared-limitations/)) | Flex $8–30/month (5 GB), or M10 ~$58/month with backups ([MongoDB Pricing](https://www.mongodb.com/pricing)); or self-host a single-node replica set on the VM |
| Compute | Oracle A1 Always Free | Cut to **2 OCPU/12 GB** June 2026 without notice; **Linux/ARM only**; Batam or Singapore, no Jakarta; idle reclamation ([OCI](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/FreeTier/resourceref.htm); [InfoQ](https://infoq.com/news/2026/07/oracle-cloud-free-tier-limits/)) | Indonesian VPS 2–4 vCPU/4 GB ~Rp105k–165k/month ([Biznet Gio](https://biznetgio.com/pricelist); [whtop IDCloudHost](https://www.whtop.com/id/plans/idcloudhost.com/148742)) |
| Compute (PaaS) | Render, Azure F1 | Render sleeps after 15 min with no persistent disk ([Render](https://render.com/docs/free)); F1 has 60 CPU-min/day | — |
| Windows | — | Azure Indonesia Central: App Service B1 Windows $54.75 vs Linux $12.41/month; Windows VM B2als v2 ~$38 vs Linux ~$31 ([Azure Retail Prices](https://prices.azure.com/api/retail/prices)) | Port to Linux, or pay the ~$5–40/month Windows premium |
| Backups/files | Cloudflare R2 10 GB, OCI Object Storage 20 GB | Free and adequate for nightly `mongodump --oplog` + evidence sync; R2 data likely outside Indonesia ([R2 Pricing](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/pricing/)) | Encrypt before upload |

**Proposed answer.** Use **two tiers**. For the **pilot only**, run Oracle Always Free A1 (or Atlas M0 plus any always-on compute) with nightly dumps to object storage. That requires a Linux/ARM build, and MFA either relaxed for the pilot or Essentials bought. For **production**, choose between two paths:

| Path | Setup | Total | In IDR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linux, Jakarta | Indonesian VPS (Biznet Gio or IDCloudHost, 4 GB) or Azure Indonesia Central Linux VM + self-hosted single-node replica set + R2/OCI backups + Auth0 Essentials | **~$41–70/month** | ~Rp0.7–1.15 million at an assumed, unverified Rp16,500/USD |
| Windows kept | Azure Windows VM in Jakarta, self-hosted Mongo, Auth0 Essentials | **~$75–90/month** | — |

Azure App Service Windows alone cannot host MongoDB or a persistent evidence disk. Either production path needs a **monthly restore test** into a scratch replica set, with time-to-restore recorded.

**Data residency.** KUP Pasal 28(11) says records are kept **in Indonesia**, and UU PDP Pasal 56 allows transfers abroad only with equal protection, binding safeguards or consent ([pasal.id](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-56)). Business records and evidence should therefore sit in a Jakarta data centre, with only identity data in Auth0, documented as a cross-border transfer in the privacy notice. Oracle Batam is in Indonesia but its capacity is unknown. PANDI requires NIB/NPWP/SK Kumham documents for a **.co.id** domain, which the Koperasi can supply ([PANDI](https://pandi.id/public/files/2025/5/rumusan-usulan-pengembangan-kebijakan-layanan-proxy-nama-domain-hasil-rapat-pleno-fms-1746966280.pdf)).

**What needs a real decision.** The owner or Pengurus must set a **monthly budget ceiling**. The Auth0 line alone, about Rp578k a month, likely exceeds the Linux compute cost. They must also accept or reject the **Linux port**, decide whether MFA is mandatory from day one, and name who pays and owns the cloud accounts. The accounts should belong to the Koperasi, not to a student developer.

**Risks.** Free tiers carry no SLA and change without notice; Oracle did so in 2026 ([InfoQ](https://infoq.com/news/2026/07/oracle-cloud-free-tier-limits/)). Some expire outright: AWS Free closes at six months, and Azure for Students lasts 12 months and only while the holder is a student ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/free/); [Azure for Students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-ca/free/students/)). A capstone hosted on a student credit will lose ten years of tax evidence when the student graduates. Running Auth0 MFA on the Free plan "for testing" and then leaving it in production risks a tenant block that locks out every approver.

## D08: pilot one division, then cut over on the 1st after a clean count

**What the SOP answers.** Nothing on migration. It does, however, show the month rhythm: deliveries peak at month start and end, and Penagihan, Pimpinan Unit and Akuntansi close in sequence at month-end (SOP BAB III.1, VI.2).

**Proposed answer.** Run a **division-scoped pilot**: one BNI division and its PIC order through the system for **2–4 weeks** while the others stay on WhatsApp, numbering Surat Jalan from day one. Then run a **parallel period of one full calendar month** for the processes being replaced (handwritten Surat Jalan, manual merge, manual matching, and Smart if retired), so the parallel covers **one month-end close and one Masa PPN**. Sources suggest 1–4 weeks of parallel running spanning a VAT period and a close, and warn that full dual entry is the costliest option ([Softomate](https://www.softomatesolutions.com/blog/odoo-implementation-timeline-uk/); [Acumatica](https://cdn.acumatica.com/media/2016/08/Acumatica-Going-live-with-your-new-ERP-system.pdf)). Follow with **4 weeks of hypercare** overlapping the first live month-end ([ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/glossary/go-live)). OMI is never "cut over", so its reconciliation is permanent rather than a temporary parallel run.

**Cutover checklist.** Go live on the **1st of a month**, with the opname count date equal to the cutover date, avoiding December–February (year-end close and RAT) and PPN filing days. The cutover loads four things:

1. Opening stock by count, with OMI on-hand variances recorded as reconciling items.
2. **Every open invoice individually**: number, faktur number, division, due date and PPN ([Ecosire](https://ecosire.com/es/blog/tally-to-odoo-migration-step-by-step-guide)).
3. The **itemised Rp475 million suspense list**, signed as-is by Akuntansi and Pimpinan Unit.
4. An opening list of handwritten Surat Jalan delivered but not yet invoiced.

Balance-sheet accounts must tie out with zero tolerance ([Ecosire 50-point](https://ecosire.com/blog/erp-go-live-checklist-launch-guide)). Sign-off is split by area: Kepala Toko signs stock and delivery continuity, the Pengolah Laporan and Akuntansi sign AR, suspense and the trial balance, Penagihan signs open invoices, and Pimpinan Unit signs go/no-go and accepted risks. Pengawas or an external accountant should review the pack ([Digital Adaption](https://digitaladaption.co.uk/blog/2026-06-05-erp-data-migration-checklist-sme/)). Rollback means reverting to WhatsApp and paper using **pre-printed numbered forms kept ready**, triggered by more than one business day of outage or failed opening reconciliation.

**People and training.** Use super users: Kepala Toko and one Staf Toko for operations, the Pengolah Laporan and one accounting staff for invoicing and close. Give them one-page Bahasa Indonesia job aids and short practice sessions during the pilot ([RSM](https://technologyblog.rsmus.com/insights/5-training-your-super-users-5-important-things-to-keep-in-mind)). An Indonesian swalayan ERP failed despite management support because training, competence and motivation were weak ([JAMA Polibatam](https://jurnal.polibatam.ac.id/index.php/JAMA/article/view/11704)). The **exit criterion** is that the Pengolah Laporan's backup has run a daily reconciliation and one month-end close unaided. Division PICs get a one-page notice and demo at least two weeks before go-live, including the mandatory payment-reference rule.

**Security before go-live.** Complete OWASP **ASVS 5.0 Level 1** across the app, with L2-grade authentication (MFA) for approver, finance and admin roles ([OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-asvs/)). Also require a tested restore, no shared logins, and test accounts disabled.

**What needs a real decision.** Pimpinan Unit must choose the **pilot division** and its PIC, the **cutover month**, the WhatsApp cut-off date, and the success metrics. Proposed metrics: 100% of pilot deliveries numbered, zero unexplained variance versus OMI for pilot SKUs, the share of credits auto-matched, new suspense additions at zero for the pilot division, and days to close versus baseline.

**Risks.** Few staff means super users get overloaded; protect 20–30% of their time ([ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/en-us/erp-training)). Cutting over in the middle of a PPN period splits faktur across two systems. Netting the legacy suspense at cutover would destroy the audit trail the cooperative needs to return funds to the right divisions.

## Conclusion

The eight gates turn out to be less about software than about **three signatures the cooperative has never had to give**. Pengurus must sign a jadwal retensi that replaces the 30-day purge for records. Pimpinan Unit must set the approval thresholds the SOP leaves blank, especially over the Staf Toko's unchecked OMI payments and the Kepala Toko's vendor payments. The owner must set a hosting budget that pays for MFA and Indonesian-resident storage. Once those exist, most technical choices follow directly: archive instead of purge, a Super Admin who cannot transact or destroy evidence, invoices derived from numbered deliveries, and settlement only from the bank statement.

The highest-leverage single action is not OCR at all. It is asking BNI for **per-division eCollection VAs and a statement export**, and asking the payer's central transfer unit for **remittance advice**. Together these attack the root cause of the Rp475 million suspense balance, un-narrated bulk transfers, which no amount of receipt OCR can fix. Until the cooperative hands over real samples (bukti transfer, a BNI statement, an OMI SPH, a handwritten Surat Jalan) and its tax adviser confirms PKP and BNI-pemungut status, every OCR rule and faktur mapping in this design remains a hypothesis to test in the pilot, not a specification.
