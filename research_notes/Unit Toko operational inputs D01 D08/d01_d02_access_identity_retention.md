Scope note: research run 2026-10-16. Legal text quoted from pasal.id (mirror of official text marked "Berlaku / Terverifikasi") and secondary law-firm/news sources; official JDIH copies were not all reachable (PP 71/2019 PDF on JDIH Kemenkeu is a scanned image, not text-searchable). ## (a) Role/permission model with segregation of duties (RBAC patterns, SoD matrix, maker-checker)

### Takeaway
Standard practice (ISO 27001:2022 A.5.3, COSO control activities, mainstream ERPs) is: roles grant permissions per document type and action; conflicting steps in each cycle (initiate / approve / execute / record / reconcile) are held by different people; where a small unit cannot staff full separation, compensating controls (activity logs, management review, supervisor sign-off) are explicitly accepted. The Unit Toko SOP roles map naturally to a purchase-to-pay, order-to-cash, cash, and record-to-report split, with Pimpinan Unit as the independent approver and Akuntansi as the independent recorder.

### Cited Findings
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A 5.3 (Segregation of duties) replaces 2013 control 6.1.2 and now lists activities that need segregation, including: (a) initiating, approving and executing a change; (b) requesting, approving and implementing access rights; (e) using and administering applications; (f) using applications and administering databases; (g) designing, auditing and assuring security controls — [ISMS.online A.5.3](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/5-3-segregation-of-duties-2022/)
- A.5.3 guidance: where separation is not possible, "particularly for small organisations with a limited number of employees, activity monitoring, audit trails, and management supervision can be used"; larger organisations can use automated tools to prevent conflicting roles being assigned — [ISMS.online A.5.3](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/5-3-segregation-of-duties-2022/)
- Purchasing SoD rule: the person initiating a purchase order should not approve it; the approver should not initiate payment; the payment initiator should not have custody of checks/payment instruments — [NJCPA, 2024](https://www.njcpa.org/learn/article/2024/09/23/why-segregation-of-duties-is-essential-for-internal-control)
- Cash SoD rule: no single individual should collect, deposit, record and reconcile cash receipts; another person reconciles deposit to GL, and a separate person reviews the reconciliation — [NJCPA, 2024](https://www.njcpa.org/learn/article/2024/09/23/why-segregation-of-duties-is-essential-for-internal-control)
- Management should build an SoD matrix listing responsibilities by department and individual and periodically monitor it; when staffing makes full separation impractical, compensating controls must be considered — [NJCPA, 2024](https://www.njcpa.org/learn/article/2024/09/23/why-segregation-of-duties-is-essential-for-internal-control); example public-sector SoD matrix template — [Georgia Dept. of Audits SOD Matrix (PDF)](https://www.audits2.ga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/SOD_Matrix.pdf)
- COSO places such separation under "control activities", one of its five internal-control components — [Solvexia SoD guide](https://www.solvexia.com/blog/segregation-of-duties-accounting)
- ERPNext pattern: users get Roles that carry pre-defined permissions per document type; "Role Profiles" bundle multiple roles as a template; users are added by the "System Manager"; there are "System Users" (staff with module access) vs "Website users" (customers/suppliers, portal only) — [ERPNext docs: Users and Permissions](https://docs.erpnext.com/docs/user/manual/en/users-and-permissions)
- Odoo pattern: app-specific Groups bundle access rights; changing another user's access rights requires the "Administration: Settings/Access Rights" right; "superuser mode" bypasses record rules and access rights and is restricted to Administration=Settings users; Odoo warns that misconfigured access rights can leave an "impotent admin" (nobody can change rights) — [Odoo 18 docs: Access rights](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/general/users/access_rights.html)
- OJK relevance: under UU 4/2023 (P2SK), cooperatives in the financial sector serving the public ("open loop") move to OJK supervision (POJK 47/2024); "closed loop" cooperatives serving only registered members stay under the Ministry of Cooperatives — [Kontan](https://keuangan.kontan.co.id/news/nilai-aset-21-koperasi-open-loop-yang-telah-dialihkan-ke-ojk-capai-rp-33912-miliar); [OJK POJK 47/2024 abstract](https://ojk.go.id/id/regulasi/Documents/Pages/POJK-47-Tahun-2024-Koperasi-di-Sektor-Jasa-Keuangan/Abstrak%20POJK%2047%20Tahun%202024%20Koperasi%20di%20Sektor%20Jasa%20Keuangan.pdf)
- Cooperative law: UU 25/1992 remains the base law (UU 17/2012 that replaced it was annulled by the Constitutional Court in 2014); a revision bill (RUU Perkoperasian, amendment to UU 25/1992) became a DPR initiative on 18 Nov 2025 and the Presidential letter was received in early 2026; no evidence found that it has been enacted as of Oct 2026 — [Investortrust, Nov 2025](https://investortrust.id/business/86236/ruu-perkoperasian-diharapkan-sah-sebelum-maret-2026); [Bisnis, Apr 2025](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250416/12/1869654/ruu-koperasi-ditargetkan-sah-bulan-ini-ksp-bisa-dijamin-jamkrida-cs)

### Inferences
- OJK/BI banking-grade rules do not directly bind a koperasi's retail shop unit (it is a trading unit, not a savings-loan/financial-sector activity); BNI's own vendor policies may, contractually. Treat banking practices (maker-checker, dual control) as good practice, not legal obligation.
- Suggested SoD conflict matrix for Unit Toko (rows = must not be the same person on the same document; "CC" = acceptable with compensating control):
  | Cycle | Step A | Step B | Rule |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Purchase (non-OMI) | Kepala Toko creates PO / negotiates vendor | Approves PO above threshold | Separate: Pimpinan Unit approves above threshold; below threshold CC (post-hoc review) |
  | Purchase | Vendor master create/edit (incl. bank account) | Approve PO or pay vendor | Separate; vendor-bank changes need second-person verification |
  | Receiving | Staf Toko records goods receipt | Same person approves/created the PO | Separate (Kepala Toko orders, Staf receives) |
  | Payables | Receive goods | Match invoice / release payment to supplier OMI | CC: Staf Toko pays OMI against its bill — require receipt-vs-bill match reviewed by Kepala Toko |
  | Sales (division order) | Kepala Toko receives order | Creates Surat Jalan / delivers | Different people (Staf Toko); PIC signature on delivery note as external confirmation |
  | Billing | Penagihan issues invoice/faktur pajak | Edit price/qty on the delivery or order record | Separate; invoice derived from signed Surat Jalan, not re-keyed |
  | Cash/receipts | Penagihan matches transfer to invoice | Approves refund of unidentified (suspense) funds | Separate: Pimpinan Unit approves refunds (already in SOP) |
  | POS cash | Staf Toko operates POS | Reconciles POS cash/daily report | Separate: Pengolah Laporan merges/reconciles; CC review by Kepala Toko |
  | Record-to-report | Akuntansi posts journals | Approves month-end report | Separate: Pimpinan Unit paraf (SOP) |
  | Stock | Staf Toko holds stock custody | Posts stock-opname adjustments | CC: opname counted by two people; adjustments approved by Kepala Toko/Pimpinan |
  | Reimbursement | Kepala Toko requests funds | Approves own request | Separate: Pimpinan Unit/Koperasi finance approves |
  | Access | Super Admin grants roles | Same person uses business role | Separate (see (b)) |
- Implement as: roles = bundles of (document type, action) permissions; a declarative SoD rule set checked (1) at grant time (block or require documented exception) and (2) at transaction time ("maker ≠ checker" on the same document id). Transaction-time check matters because in a small unit people legitimately hold multiple roles.
- Division PICs, couriers and walk-in customers should be external/portal identities (ERPNext "website user" analogue), never internal staff roles.

## (b) Super Admin governance (admin-cannot-transact, privileged access, break-glass)

### Takeaway
The product owner's design ("Super Admin controls users/roles/config but cannot do business work unless separately granted") is exactly what ISO 27001 A.5.3 (e/f: using vs administering applications) and A.8.2 (privileged access) recommend. Add: no self-grant, second-person approval for granting privileged or high-risk business roles, separate admin identity from any business identity, time-limited elevation, re-authentication, full logging, periodic review, and a sealed break-glass procedure.

### Cited Findings
- A.5.3 lists "using and administering applications" and "requesting, approving and implementing access rights" as activities to segregate — [ISMS.online A.5.3](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/5-3-segregation-of-duties-2022/)
- A.8.2 guidance points: keep a list of all users with privileged access; assign privileged rights on an event-by-event, minimum-need basis; keep a record of all grants with a straightforward authorisation process; privileged rights should have expiry dates; users should be explicitly informed when operating with privileged access; require re-authentication before using privileged rights where relevant; periodically audit privileged rights especially after organisational change (with A.5.18); consider a "break glass" procedure granting privileged rights within tightly controlled time windows — [ISMS.online A.8.2](https://www.isms.online/iso-27001/annex-a-2022/8-2-use-of-privileged-access-rights-2022/)
- A.8.2 links to A.8.15 for logging all privileged-account activity; administration of privileged application accounts typically owned by IT leadership — [ISMS.online A.8.2 checklist summary](https://isms.online/iso-27001/checklist/annex-a-8-2-checklist); [HighTable A.8.2](https://hightable.io/how-to-implement-iso-27001-annex-a-8-2)
- Break-glass practice: separate identity not reused for routine admin; narrowly scoped privileges for access recovery; split credentials (one person password, another MFA device); dual-control retrieval; activate only under predefined conditions; log and alert on every use and treat use as a security event with post-mortem; rotate secrets after every use; drill periodically — [Akeyless](https://docs.akeyless.io/docs/break-glass-access-best-practices); [Bitwarden](https://bitwarden.com/it-it/resources/how-to-set-up-break-glass-accounts.md); [Oracle A-Team](https://www.ateam-oracle.com/securing-oci-break-glass-access-best-practices)
- Odoo shows the risk of an unrestricted superuser: superuser mode bypasses record rules and access rights, so mature ERPs restrict and flag it — [Odoo 18 docs](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/general/users/access_rights.html)

### Inferences
- Recommended Super Admin rules:
  1. Super Admin permissions = identity & access admin + configuration + read-only audit view. Explicitly excluded: create/approve/pay/post on any business document, restore/purge of financial records, editing audit logs.
  2. Super Admin cannot grant roles to themself; grants of any business role to a Super Admin account, and grants of "approver"/"payment"/"posting" roles to anyone, require a second approver (e.g., Pimpinan Unit) — the two-person rule on access changes (A.5.3 b).
  3. A person who needs both should have two accounts (admin account + business account) so logs show which hat was worn; or, minimum, a distinct elevated session with re-auth (A.8.2 points 5–6).
  4. Minimum two Super Admins (avoid lock-out/"impotent admin") but small count; each named, MFA-mandatory.
  5. Break-glass account: sealed credentials split between two officers (e.g., Ketua Koperasi + Pimpinan Unit), use triggers an alert to all admins and Pimpinan, auto-expires, rotated after use, and still cannot transact.
  6. All admin actions written to append-only audit log not deletable by Super Admin (consistent with the project's existing provenance-seal work).
  7. Quarterly privileged-access review; semi-annual/annual review of all business roles (see (d)).
- Super Admin should also be unable to bypass the retention policy (no manual hard delete of financial documents) — makes (c) enforceable.

### Gaps
- No primary source found stating a specific Indonesian regulatory requirement for admin-cannot-transact in non-bank entities; it is a best-practice/ISO recommendation.
- Exact ISO 27002:2022 wording was taken via secondary summaries (standard text is paywalled).

## (c) Soft-delete / 30-day purge policy lawful in Indonesia (retention law)

### Takeaway
A blanket "soft delete then hard delete after 30 days" is NOT lawful for financial/tax records: UU KUP Pasal 28(11) and UU 8/1997 Pasal 11 require books, records and supporting documents (including electronically processed data) to be kept 10 years, in Indonesia. Apply 30-day purge only to non-record items (drafts never issued, rejected/duplicate uploads, cancelled-before-issue items, temp files). Issued documents should be "voided/cancelled" (kept, marked), and "deletion" of a record should mean hide-and-archive with retention clock, legal hold, and controlled destruction per a written retention schedule (jadwal retensi) adopted by a leadership decree. Personal data has the opposite pull (UU PDP: delete when no longer needed), resolved by retention-schedule periods and minimisation.

### Cited Findings
- UU KUP Pasal 28 ayat (11): books, records and documents that are the basis of bookkeeping, and other documents including results of data processing from bookkeeping done electronically or via online applications, must be kept for 10 years in Indonesia (at the place of business/domicile of the taxpayer); 10 years aligns with the limitation period for tax crime investigation; storage must consider security, suitability and reasonableness; Pasal 39 criminal sanctions for intentionally not keeping them where state revenue loss can arise — [DDTC News](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1805429/alasan-dokumen-dasar-pembukuan-wajib-disimpan-selama-10-tahun)
- UU 8/1997 (Dokumen Perusahaan) Pasal 11: records (Pasal 5: annual balance sheet, P&L, accounts, daily transaction journals, any writing about rights/obligations), bookkeeping evidence (Pasal 6) and financial-administration supporting data (Pasal 7) must be kept 10 years from end of the company's financial year; other documents' periods are set by their "nilai guna" (use value); periods are compiled into a retention schedule (jadwal retensi) set by decision of company leadership; keeping them does not remove their evidentiary function under limitation periods — [pasal.id UU 8/1997 Pasal 11](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-8-tahun-1997/pasal-11); [Hukumonline Klinik](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/jangka-waktu-penyimpanan-dokumen-perusahaan-lt646efc4036ca5)
- Hukumonline: financial-admin supporting data that is not part of bookkeeping evidence may have its period adjusted to company needs — [Hukumonline Klinik](https://www.hukumonline.com/klinik/a/jangka-waktu-penyimpanan-dokumen-perusahaan-lt646efc4036ca5)
- UU 27/2022 PDP Pasal 42(1): controller must stop processing when (a) retention period reached, (b) purpose achieved, or (c) data subject requests; (2) done "in accordance with laws and regulations" — [pasal.id Pasal 42](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-42)
- UU PDP Pasal 43(1): controller must delete personal data when (a) no longer needed for the purpose, (b) consent withdrawn, (c) subject requests, or (d) obtained/processed unlawfully; (2) deletion is carried out "in accordance with laws and regulations" — [pasal.id Pasal 43](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-43)
- UU PDP Pasal 44(1): controller must destroy (memusnahkan) personal data when (a) retention period expired and marked for destruction under the archive retention schedule (jadwal retensi arsip), (b) subject requests, (c) not related to resolving a legal case, and/or (d) unlawfully obtained — [pasal.id Pasal 44](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-44)
- PP 71/2019 (PSTE) includes deletion/destruction within "processing" of personal data and requires electronic system operators to delete irrelevant information under their control on request (Pasal 15, per secondary source) — [Rendra Topan](https://rendratopan.com/2019/11/12/proses-data-pribadi-dalam-penyelenggaraan-sistem-elektronik/); official text at [JDIH Komdigi](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/695/t/peraturan%2Bpemerintah%2Bnomor%2B71%2Btahun%2B2019)

### Inferences
- Proposed retention classes (to be adopted as Unit Toko jadwal retensi by Pengurus/Pimpinan decree, per UU 8/1997 Pasal 11(4)):
  | Item | Class | Treatment |
  |---|---|---|
  | Draft orders/POs/invoices never issued; rejected or duplicate uploads; abandoned carts; temp imports | Transient | Soft delete → 30-day restore window → automatic purge (OK) |
  | Issued invoices, faktur pajak, credit notes | Tax/bookkeeping evidence | No delete. Void/cancel with reason (keeps number sequence). Retain 10 yrs from end of fiscal year (KUP 28(11), UU 8/1997) |
  | Payment proofs (transfer slips), bank matching, suspense-fund records, refunds | Bookkeeping evidence | Retain 10 yrs; soft-delete = archive only |
  | Purchase orders, supplier bills (incl. OMI), goods receipts | Bookkeeping evidence | Retain 10 yrs |
  | Surat Jalan / signed delivery notes | Supporting evidence of sale (basis of invoice) | Retain 10 yrs (conservative: basis of bookkeeping) |
  | Journals, ledgers, financial statements, month-end report with paraf | Records (UU 8/1997 Pasal 5) | Retain 10 yrs |
  | Stock opname sheets and adjustments | Supporting data for inventory/COGS | Retain 10 yrs (conservative) |
  | POS daily reports, merged daily report | Bookkeeping evidence | Retain 10 yrs |
  | Audit logs / provenance seals for the above | "Results of data processing" of electronic bookkeeping (KUP 28(11)) | Retain at least as long as the record they describe |
  | User accounts of staff | Personal data + accountability anchor | Deactivate on leave (never hard-delete while referenced by retained records); minimise profile fields after leave; keep name/ID linkage for the life of referenced records |
  | Personal data not tied to records (phone, KTP image, photos, marketing) | Personal data | Delete when purpose ends / on request (PDP 43–44); short defined period |
- "Soft delete" for retained classes should mean: hidden from normal views, restorable by authorised role within 30 days, then moved to an archive tier (read-only, still searchable by Akuntansi/auditor) — not purged. Final destruction only after retention expiry, via a destruction list approved by Pimpinan, logged (berita acara pemusnahan), and skipped if a legal hold (tax audit, dispute) is set.
- Conflict resolution PDP vs retention: Pasal 42–44 each say processing/deletion/destruction is done "in accordance with laws and regulations", and Pasal 44(1)(a) ties destruction to the retention schedule — so a deletion request for personal data embedded in a tax record can be answered by restricting processing and deleting non-required fields, retaining the record until the 10-year period ends. Document this reasoning in the privacy notice.
- 10-year clock: from end of the financial year (UU 8/1997). Store in Indonesia per KUP 28(11) wording — relevant if the system is hosted on a foreign cloud; keep at least an Indonesian-resident archive/backup copy (consult tax adviser).

### Gaps
- UU 43/2009 Kearsipan and its PP 28/2012: not fetched; whether it binds a koperasi's commercial records (vs state/public archives) not confirmed. UU PDP Pasal 44 references "jadwal retensi arsip", so a retention schedule is expected either way.
- No specific retention period found for Surat Jalan or stock opname; 10 years is a conservative inference, not a cited rule.
- Faktur pajak / Coretax-era (PMK 81/2024) specific retention rules not researched; KUP 28(11) used as the governing rule.
- Cooperative-law-specific retention rule (UU 25/1992 or Permenkop on koperasi accounting) not found.
- PP 71/2019 audit-trail article could not be verified (PDF is scanned image); do not cite an article number for a log obligation.
- Whether "stored in Indonesia" in KUP 28(11) is satisfied by a foreign-hosted primary with Indonesian access was not resolved.

## (d) UU PDP requirements and staff identity verification (lawful basis, DPO, breach, rights, cross-border, NIK/Dukcapil, MFA, JML, access review)

### Takeaway
UU 27/2022 has been fully in force since 17 Oct 2024; its implementing regulation PP 33/2026 was set on 16 Jul 2026 and takes effect 16 Jan 2027 (6-month transition). The PDP supervisory agency (Lembaga/Badan PDP) is still not formed (Perpres pending; Komdigi supervises in the interim; agency expected to be effective ~2028). For Unit Toko: document lawful basis (contract/legal obligation for staff and buyer data), keep minimal identity data, breach notification 3x24 hours, assess DPO need (likely not mandatory at this scale but appoint a PIC), and treat foreign hosting as a cross-border transfer. Staff identity: verify against koperasi/BNI HR records rather than Dukcapil (Dukcapil verification requires PKS/MoU and is a paid, sector-limited service); use MFA, JML process, and periodic reviews.

### Cited Findings
- UU PDP: controllers had to comply within two years of enactment on 17 Oct 2022 (i.e., by 17 Oct 2024) — [Hukumonline summary via search; UU text at BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/229798/uu-no-27-)
- PP 33/2026 (implementing regulation of UU PDP): set 16 Jul 2026, effective 16 Jan 2027 (6 months after promulgation); 12 chapters, 225 articles covering classification (general vs specific data), processing bases, data-subject rights, controller/processor duties, cross-border transfer, DPO, DPIA, supervision, sanctions — [Meridian Hukum](https://meridianhukum.com/peraturan/pp-no-33-tahun-2026); [Katadata, 3 Sep 2026](https://katadata.co.id/finansial/makro/6a9979b7c5745/pp-pelindungan-data-pribadi-berlaku-januari-2027-ini-aturan-baru-data-anak); [Justisio, 22 Sep 2026](https://justisio.com/blog/pp-pdp-disahkan-jangan-sampai-kena-sanksi)
- PP 33/2026 Pasal 30(1): controller must have a processing basis before processing — [Meridian Hukum](https://meridianhukum.com/peraturan/pp-no-33-tahun-2026)
- Specific personal data (per PP 33/2026 reporting) includes health, biometric, genetic, criminal records, child data and personal financial data — [search summary of Katadata/Veritask](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)
- Breach notification: UU PDP Pasal 46 — written notice within 3x24 hours to data subjects and the Lembaga, containing data exposed, when/how, handling and recovery; public notice in certain cases — [pasal.id Pasal 46](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-46). PP 33/2026 Pasal 114 restates 3x24 h "since the failure is known with certainty, reasonably and properly" and adds the DPO/contact info; Pasal 115 public notice if public service disrupted/serious impact; Pasal 116–119 require documentation of every failure — [Veritask, 3 Sep 2026](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)
- DPO: UU PDP Pasal 53 requires a DPO for public-service processing, core activities needing large-scale regular and systematic monitoring, or core activities of large-scale processing of specific/crime-related data; Constitutional Court Decision 151/PUU-XXII/2024 reads the "dan" in Pasal 53(1)(b) as "dan/atau" — [pasal.id Pasal 53](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-53). PP 33/2026 Pasal 142–147: same triggers; appointment considers organisation structure/size; DPO must have access to top management, be independent and resourced — [Veritask](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)
- DPIA (PP 33/2026 Pasal 120–122) required before high-risk processing: specific data, large scale, new technology, automated decisions with legal/significant effect, systematic monitoring, matching/combining datasets — [Veritask](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)
- Cross-border: UU PDP Pasal 56 — transfer abroad allowed if destination has equal or higher protection; otherwise adequate and binding safeguards; otherwise data-subject consent — [pasal.id Pasal 56](https://pasal.id/peraturan/uu/uu-no-27-tahun-2022/pasal-56). PP 33/2026 Pasal 160–166: before transfer, map the transfer cycle, minimise data, assess legal instrument, assess risk to subjects, and inform subjects of purpose, safeguards, risks and mitigations — [Veritask](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)
- Sanctions (PP 33/2026 Pasal 184–185): written warning, temporary suspension, deletion/destruction, administrative fine up to 2% of annual revenue, scaled by impact, duration, subjects affected, business scale, cooperation (may be reduced to Rp0) — [Veritask](https://veritask.ai/id/artikel/pengaturan-teknis-pelindungan-data-pribadi-dan-kewajiban-pengendali-serta-prosesor-akhirnya-terbit-lewat-pp-33-2026)

### Inferences
- Lawful basis for Unit Toko: staff data — performance of employment/assignment and legal obligation; division PIC data (name, BNI work email/phone, signature on Surat Jalan) — contract performance with the division; walk-in customer — usually no personal data needed (cash/QR anonymous); tax identity (NPWP/NIK on faktur) — legal obligation.
- DPO: a small koperasi shop processing staff and B2B contact data is unlikely to meet Pasal 53 "large scale" triggers, but appointing a named privacy PIC (e.g., in the koperasi secretariat) is cheap and supports the 3x24h breach workflow. Verify thresholds once Lembaga issues technical rules.
- Hosting abroad (e.g., US cloud): treat as cross-border transfer: record a transfer assessment, use the provider's binding contractual safeguards, inform staff/PIC in the privacy notice; prefer an Indonesian (Jakarta) region where available — also eases KUP 28(11) "stored in Indonesia".
- Staff identity verification design:
  1. Source of truth = koperasi staff roster (and, for BNI-assigned staff, BNI employee number confirmed by the koperasi HR/secretariat). Onboarding requires an HR/Pimpinan-approved request referencing roster ID — no self-registration for internal roles.
  2. Do NOT store KTP images or full NIK for login; if needed for tax/HR, keep in HR records, mask in UI (show last 4), restrict to HR role. Dukcapil API is impractical (PKS, fees, sector focus) and disproportionate.
  3. Authentication: unique named accounts (no shared POS logins); MFA (TOTP/passkey) mandatory for Super Admin, approvers, payment/billing, accounting; at least for any remote access. Short session timeout on shared POS terminals; PIN-based quick switch tied to named user.
  4. Joiner/Mover/Leaver: joiner = roster-linked request + Pimpinan approval + role from template; mover = remove old roles before adding new (avoid accumulation that breaks SoD); leaver = disable same day as exit (account retained, not deleted, because audit records reference it), revoke sessions/tokens.
  5. Access reviews: privileged (Super Admin, break-glass) quarterly; business roles semi-annually and on any org change; dormant accounts auto-disabled after e.g. 60–90 days inactivity (cadences are common practice; not prescribed by ISO text found).
  6. External identities (division PICs, couriers): invite-only accounts linked to the division/courier record, verified via BNI work email domain; minimal fields.
