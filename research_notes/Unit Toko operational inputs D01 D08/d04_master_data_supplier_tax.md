## 1. What is OMI, and how do partners order, receive, pay and return?

### Takeaway
"OMI" means **Outlet Mitra Indogrosir**. It is not "Outlet Mitra Indomaret". It is a franchise/partnership program run by PT Inti Cakrawala Citra (Indogrosir, the wholesale arm of the Indomaret group). It explicitly targets cooperatives (koperasi) at companies and government agencies, which fits the Unit Toko case. Public sources describe the commercial model: own store name, supply guaranteed by Indogrosir, loaned software, planogram, training. **No public source documents the operational details**: DDO, SPH, the koli/struk receiving procedure, payment terms, per-item return windows or data exports. These live in the franchise contract and OMI SOPs. For the system design, treat the user's field observations as the source of truth.

### Cited Findings
- OMI = Outlet Mitra Indogrosir, a minimarket development program in franchise form under PT Inti Cakrawala Citra (Indogrosir), which has existed since 2001. Partners may use their own store name. — [detikFinance, Nov 2022](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-6391805/mau-buka-indomaret-tapi-pakai-nama-sendiri-begini-cara-dan-biayanya)
- The 2001 partnership program adopted a franchise model in 2003, which let Indogrosir "support sales development at cooperatives, at companies and government agencies". OMI claims more than 600 stores in 152 cities/regencies across 10 provinces and cooperation with more than 250 koperasi. — [waralabagram.com (franchise directory, 2025)](https://waralabagram.com/franchise-omi/)
- The investment package includes equipment and merchandise (including the signboard), **loan of software and support for the franchise term**, training per OMI SOP, "belanja hemat" promotion support, operational supervision, and store opening promotion. Payback is about 24–36 months. Coverage follows Indogrosir store areas (Jabodetabek, Bandung, Surabaya, Yogyakarta, Medan, Palembang, Semarang, Pekanbaru). — [detikFinance](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-6391805/mau-buka-indomaret-tapi-pakai-nama-sendiri-begini-cara-dan-biayanya)
- Store types and investment (2025 directory): R-8 (30–40 m², about Rp190 million), R-15 (40–50 m², about Rp250 million), R-29 (>50 m², about Rp300 million). The package includes "loan of software integrated with minimarket operations", a modern POS with integrated sales recording, stock management by Indogrosir "to avoid overstock", and guidance on **price setting** and planogram. — [waralabagram.com](https://waralabagram.com/franchise-omi/)
- Older or contradictory investment figures: about Rp135–150 million (2022) and about Rp160 million (Tempo) versus Rp190 million (Hops.id, waralabagram). — [detik](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-6391805/mau-buka-indomaret-tapi-pakai-nama-sendiri-begini-cara-dan-biayanya); [Tempo](https://www.tempo.co/ekonomi/ingin-buka-franchise-outliine-mitra-indogrosir-siapkan-dana-rp-160-juta-dan-lahan-untuk-disurvei-1160404); [Hops.id](https://www.hops.id/unik/29412684369/ingin-tau-harga-franchise-minimarket-yang-bisa-pakai-nama-sendiri-yuk-intip-harga-syarat-dan-keuntungan-franchise-omi-mitra-indogrosir)
- OMI partners may sell goods sourced outside Indogrosir. This matches the Unit Toko's separate MCM/Mayora merchandise and the Pasar Kering emergency buys. After setup, the business is fully owned by the investor, while Indogrosir keeps monitoring it. — [waralabagram](https://waralabagram.com/franchise-omi/) (via search summary); [Tempo](https://www.tempo.co/ekonomi/profil-omi-dari-indogrosir-bantu-kembangkan-warung-jadi-minimarket-ada-yang-omzetnya-tembus-rp-20-juta-per-hari--1160521)
- An academic case study (skripsi, IAIN Kudus) of an OMI store reports that orders are placed via a **"PB" (Pembelian Barang)** document. Fast-moving or promoted items are often out of stock when ordered, because Indogrosir also supplies Indomaret and OMI gets lower priority. — [IAIN Kudus repository, Bab IV](http://repository.iainkudus.ac.id/6490/7/7.%20Bab%20IV.pdf) (via search summary; the PDF text was not extractable)
- Indogrosir also runs a separate "Mitra Indogrosir" PPOB app (pulsa, token listrik, bill payments) for partners. — [mitra.indogrosir.co.id](https://mitra.indogrosir.co.id/)
- Official OMI site cited by press: omifranchise.co.id. It was unreachable during this research. — [detik](https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-6391805/mau-buka-indomaret-tapi-pakai-nama-sendiri-begini-cara-dan-biayanya)

### Inferences
- In the data model, name the supplier **"Indogrosir (PT Inti Cakrawala Citra) – OMI program"**, not "Indomaret". Faktur pajak masukan will be issued by PT Inti Cakrawala Citra's NPWP, so the supplier master record should hold that legal entity and NPWP.
- The user's terms (auto-restock Mon/Thu, koli, DDO, SPH, per-item return windows) are contract/SOP vocabulary. Model them as configurable supplier policy data, not hard-coded rules:
  - `return_window_days_before_expiry` per SKU (for example Coca-Cola 90 days, SGM 180, Lactogrow 270, Chiki 30, Panadol 270)
  - delivery calendar (Mon/Thu)
  - koli as a delivery container with a seal/ID
  - DDO as a special-order document
  - SPH as a monthly billing statement to reconcile against receipts and returns
- Because OMI loans the POS software, the OMI system is the system of record for OMI-sourced SKU codes (PLU) and costs. Any capstone system should **import or reconcile** with it (CSV/print exports) rather than replace it. Assume no public API.
- The "PB" order document seen in the thesis is probably the store-side order. The user's "DDO" may be the Indogrosir-side distribution list. The two could be variants of the same flow. Unverified.

### Gaps
- No public documentation found on DDO, SPH, payment terms (tempo days), minimum order, margins, koli sealing/receiving procedure, struk/tanda terima format, return windows or what data exports the OMI back office provides. These are likely contract-confidential. Obtain an SPH sample, a DDO email, a delivery struk and a return slip from the Unit Toko.
- Whether OMI item codes are Indogrosir PLUs, GTINs or both is unknown.

## 2. GS1 / GTIN rules for packaging levels, and internal codes

### Takeaway
GTIN-8/12/13 identify consumer units scanned at POS. GTIN-14 identifies trade/case levels and is not for POS. Store every GTIN in a normalized 14-digit form with a mod-10 check digit. Only the brand owner, through a GS1 Indonesia membership, may assign GTINs. A retailer should store vendor GTINs and never invent them. For items without barcodes, use internal PLUs or GS1 restricted-circulation numbers (prefix 02 / 20–29) that are valid only inside the store.

### Cited Findings
- GTINs are 8, 12, 13 or 14 digits. Each combines a GS1 Company Prefix, an Item Reference and a calculated check digit. GTIN-14 adds an Indicator Digit. Barcode carriers: GTIN-8 uses EAN-8, GTIN-12 uses UPC-A/UPC-E, GTIN-13 uses EAN-13, and GTIN-14 uses ITF-14/GS1-128. GTIN-14 is "used for wholesale shipments, not retail point of sale". — [Wikipedia: GTIN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Trade_Item_Number)
- Shorter GTINs are right-justified and left-padded with zeros into the 14-digit field. For GTIN-14, indicator digits 1–8 mark a packaging level and 9 marks a variable-measure item. There is no global consensus on which digit means which level, and no meaning should be built into it. — [Wikipedia: GTIN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Trade_Item_Number)
- GTIN-14 identifies homogeneous groupings (cases or cartons of identical units). It is derived from the inner unit's GTIN-12/13 by adding an indicator digit and recalculating the check digit, and it is not meant to be scanned at POS. — [GS1 Canada: What is GTIN-14](https://gs1ca.org/documents/standards/GS1CA-What-is-GTIN-14-and-How-to-Use-it-EN.pdf); [GS1 Sweden: types of GTIN](https://gs1.se/en/?p=63808)
- GS1 Indonesia is the only organization authorized by GS1 Global to handle barcode numbering in Indonesia. Membership is per company, not per product. Fees are tiered by paid-up capital/company size. A successful member receives an allocation of 1,000 numbers. Documents requested include NPWP, deed, business registration, ID and, for food, BPOM/P-IRT permits. The source quotes fees using a USD rate of Rp14,300, so the figures are dated. — [UKMIndonesia.id](https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/inilah-prosedur-dan-biaya-pendaftaran-barcode-di-indonesia); [UKMIndonesia.id: barcode GS1](https://ukmindonesia.id/baca-deskripsi-posts/barcode-gs1)
- Prefix 899 is Indonesia's GS1 prefix. It indicates where the company is registered, not the country of manufacture. — [luatminhkhue.vn (secondary)](https://luatminhkhue.vn/ma-vach-899-cua-nuoc-nao.aspx)

### Inferences
- Master data design:
  - SKU (selling unit) has 0..n GTINs, because a product can carry several barcodes across repackaging or promotions.
  - Each packaging level (unit, inner/pack, case/dus/karton) is a row with its own GTIN (often a GTIN-14 for the case), `contains_qty` of the child level, and a flag for whether it is sellable at POS.
  - Validate the length (8/12/13/14) and the mod-10 check digit. Store as a 14-digit zero-padded string, never as a number, so leading zeros are kept.
- "Dus 330" (presumably a Le Minerale 330 ml case) should be a packaging level whose case GTIN is distinct from the bottle GTIN. Unit Toko should not assign GTINs to vendor goods.
- Items with no barcode (loose goods, own-repacked sembako, merchandise without a code): assign internal PLU codes. If they need to be printed as barcodes, use the GS1 restricted-circulation range (prefix 02 or 20–29), which must not leave the store. This is standard GS1 General Specifications practice, but this research did not verify it from a primary GS1 page (gs1.org returned 403). Mark it as to be confirmed.
- The cooperative needs GS1 Indonesia membership only if it brands its own products (for example repacked rice sold to BNI divisions). Not needed otherwise.

### Gaps
- Current (2026) GS1 Indonesia fee schedule and the gs1id.org website were not retrieved.
- GS1 restricted-circulation prefix rules were not confirmed from a primary GS1 source in this session.

## 3. HET (Harga Eceran Tertinggi): products with a legal retail price cap in 2026

### Takeaway
The main legal retail caps that matter to a small shop are **MinyaKita** (government-program cooking oil, Rp15,700/L under Permendag 18/2024; retailers must sell at or below HET and be registered in SIMIRAH) and **beras** (medium and premium rice, set by Bapanas per zone). Sugar's HET is uncertain: a 2024 relaxation set Rp17,500/kg, and no confirmed 2026 regulation was found. LPG 3 kg HET is set by regional governments and applies to official pangkalan, which a shop would normally not be. All figures have changed several times since 2024, so the system should store HET as **dated, zone-specific reference data with a source reference**, not as constants.

### Cited Findings
- MinyaKita HET was raised from Rp14,000 to **Rp15,700/litre** in August 2024 under **Permendag 18/2024** (on packaged palm cooking oil and governance of minyak goreng rakyat). — [Bisnis.com, Aug 2024](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20240817/12/1791749/tok-harga-minyakita-naik-jadi-rp15700-per-liter-usai-hut-ri)
- Under Permendag 18/2024, distribution runs producer → D1 → D2 → retailer, and retailers must sell at or below HET. A "pengecer" is defined as a business selling minyak goreng rakyat to the public **and registered in SIMIRAH**. Kemendag targets unregistered retailers selling above HET and warned of criminal and fine sanctions. Distributors get administrative sanctions. — [Bisnis.com, Jan 2025](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250113/12/1831175/kemendag-endus-pengecer-nakal-jual-minyakita-di-atas-het-tak-terdaftar-di-sistem); [Bisnis.com, Dec 2024](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20241213/12/1824100/minyakita-mahal-kemendag-sanksi-distributor-yang-lakukan-pelanggaran)
- Market prices for MinyaKita remained above HET through 2025 into January 2026 (for example a Rp17,327/L national average in June 2025). No 2026 change to the Rp15,700 HET was found. — [Bisnis.com, Jun 2025](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250611/12/1883988/harga-pangan-hari-ini-rabu-116-beras-minyakita-masih-lampau-het); [Bisnis.com, 7 Jan 2026](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260107/12/1942252/harga-pangan-hari-ini-7-januari-harga-beras-turun-minyakita-masih-mahal)
- Rice HET is zone-based (Bapanas). Premium rice: zone 1 Rp14,900, zone 2 Rp15,400, zone 3 Rp15,800 per kg (2025). Medium rice: raised to **Rp13,500/kg** (national) around 22 Aug 2025; January 2026 reporting cites a medium range of Rp13,500–15,500/kg across zones. SPHP (Bulog stabilization rice) HET is Rp12,500–13,500/kg. The earlier 2023 Perbadan was Rp10,900–11,800 (medium) and Rp13,900–14,800 (premium). — [Bisnis.com, 7 Aug 2025](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20250807/12/1900144/harga-pangan-hari-ini-7-agustus-harga-beras-mahal-di-semua-zonasi); [Bisnis.com, 7 Jan 2026](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260107/12/1942252/harga-pangan-hari-ini-7-januari-harga-beras-turun-minyakita-masih-mahal); [Bisnis.com, 5 Jan 2026](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260105/12/1941536/harga-pangan-hari-ini-5-januari-harga-beras-daging-ayam-kompak-turun). **Contradiction:** some 2025 articles still cite medium Rp12,500; the August 2025 revision supersedes it.
- Sugar: the HET was Rp12,500/kg (Permendag 7/2020). A 2024 Bapanas relaxation set a consumer price of Rp17,500/kg, to be formalized via a Perbadan. Market averages were about Rp18,000–18,200/kg in 2025. Cane farmers (APTRI) have lobbied to abolish the consumer HET. No confirmed 2026 sugar HET regulation was found. — [Bisnis.com, May 2024](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20240530/12/1769666/aturan-relaksasi-berakhir-besok-bapanas-siapkan-harga-gula-baru); [suarasurabaya.net](https://www.suarasurabaya.net/ekonomibisnis/2024/bapanas-sebut-relaksasi-harga-gula-di-konsumen-berlanjut-hingga-30-juni); [Antara Jogja](https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/835248/wamenko-pangan-usulan-penghapusan-het-gula-perlu-pembahasan)

### Inferences
- The `HetRule` reference table should hold: product category or SKU, unit (L/kg), zone (Jakarta is zone 1 for rice under Bapanas zoning: Jawa, Lampung, Sumsel, Bali, NTB, Sulawesi), cap amount, effective_from/effective_to, issuing agency (Kemendag/Bapanas/Pemda) and a regulation reference. The resale-price workflow should block or warn when the sell price is above the active cap.
- HET-capped items are thin- or negative-margin when sourced at street price. Cost-plus pricing may therefore be impossible for MinyaKita/rice bought at Pasar Pagi/Indogrosir ("Pasar Kering"). The system should flag "cost > HET" rather than auto-mark-up.
- Selling MinyaKita formally may require SIMIRAH registration as a pengecer. Ask whether Unit Toko stocks MinyaKita via OMI.
- Most branded goods (Coca-Cola, SGM, Panadol, Chiki) have **no** legal HET. Their printed "HET" or suggested retail price is a manufacturer RRP, not a legal cap. The field should distinguish `legal_het` from `manufacturer_rrp`.

### Gaps
- Current (Oct 2026) Bapanas Perbadan/Kepbadan numbers for rice and sugar, and any 2026 revision of the MinyaKita HET, were not verified from primary text.
- LPG 3 kg HET (regional, ESDM/Pemda) was not researched in depth. A shop that is not a pangkalan normally cannot legally resell subsidized LPG at HET. Verify if relevant.

## 4. PPN (VAT): rate, computation, exemptions, PKP status, Coretax faktur rules

### Takeaway
In 2026 PPN remains **12% nominal** under the UU HPP. For non-luxury goods it is computed on **DPP nilai lain = 11/12 × harga jual** (PMK 131/2024, supplemented by PMK 11/2025), so the effective rate is **11%**. Coretax faktur use **transaction code 04** for this. Basic staples listed in PP 49/2022 (beras, gabah, jagung, sagu, kedelai, garam, daging, telur, susu segar, buah, sayur, and plain white cane sugar) are **BKP that are exempt from PPN** (PPN dibebaskan, code 08). Most packaged branded goods (minyak goreng, mie instan, susu formula, snacks, drinks, medicines) are taxable at the effective 11%. A business must register as PKP only above **Rp4.8 billion** annual gross turnover (PMK 197/2013), and a koperasi is treated like any other business. Since 1 Jan 2025, faktur are created in **Coretax** (PMK 81/2024).

### Cited Findings
- PMK 131/2024 (in force 1 Jan 2025): luxury goods (PPnBM objects) pay 12% × full price. **Non-luxury goods/services pay 12% × DPP nilai lain of 11/12 × selling price**, an effective 11%. — [pajak.com / TaxPrime simulation](https://www.pajak.com/pajak/tanya-pak-jaka/pmk-131-2024-nilai-ppn-terutang-tetap-sama-taxprime-simulasikan-contoh-penghitungannya/amp/); [DDTC: tarif PPN 2025](https://news.ddtc.co.id/review/konsultasi/1807964/tarif-ppn-di-2025-jadinya-11-atau-12-begini-ketentuan-terbarunya)
- DJP: deliveries using DPP 11/12 must be invoiced with faktur **code 04**. — [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1807950/ppn-dihitung-dengan-dpp-1112-dari-harga-jual-djp-kode-fakturnya-04)
- DJP (Hestu Yoga Saksama): in Coretax the DPP 11/12 is keyed in ("11/12 × Rp10 juta = Rp9 juta sekian"), and the system applies 12%, producing effectively 11% of price. DJP discussed a transition of about 3 months for retail PKPs that issue faktur from their own systems. — [DDTC: Faktur pajaknya sudah via Coretax](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1808011/ppn-12-dihitung-dengan-dpp-1112-faktur-pajaknya-sudah-via-coretax)
- PMK 11/2025 (effective 4 Feb 2025) consolidated the other DPP-nilai-lain and besaran-tertentu rules so that the effective 11% applies to those regimes too. — [pajak.go.id SP-4/2025](https://pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-02/SP-4%20PEMERINTAH%20TERBITKAN%20ATURAN%20DPP%20NILAI%20LAIN%20DAN%20BESARAN%20TERTENTU%20PPN.pdf); [tirto.id](https://tirto.id/isi-pmk-11-tahun-2025-dan-berapa-tarif-ppn-terbaru-g8e8)
- Faktur codes per PER-11/PJ/2025 (secondary): 04 is the common code for non-luxury; 01 now applies essentially to luxury BKP; code 10 is reported as new for "other-rate" deliveries. **Sources disagree on code 10's exact scope.** — [online-pajak: kode faktur 2026](https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-efaktur-ppn/kode-faktur-pajak/); [Akuntansi Mandiri, May 2026](https://www.akuntansimandiri.com/2026/05/cara-membuat-faktur-pajak-di-coretax.html); [Ortax](https://ortax.org/membuat-faktur-pajak-dpp-nilai-lain-pada-aplikasi-coretax)
- PP 49/2022 (effective 12 Dec 2022): staples "sangat dibutuhkan rakyat banyak" (beras, gabah, jagung, sagu, kedelai, garam, daging, telur, susu, buah-buahan, sayur-sayuran) are BKP with **PPN exempted** (Art. 16B UU PPN), no SKB needed. Gula konsumsi (white crystal cane sugar without flavour or colour additives) is also exempt. The PKP must still issue a faktur (code 08) and report it. — [DDTC: beras BKP dibebaskan](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1801517/ingat-beras-masih-masuk-barang-kena-pajak-yang-dibebaskan-dari-ppn); [Pajakku](https://pajakku.com/artikel/pp-492022-atur-fasilitas-ppn-dibebaskan-dan-tidak-dipungut-cek-poin-pentingnya-disini); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/terbitkan-pp-49-2022--pemerintah-pertahankan-kemudahan-ppn-lt639ac623297a5)
- PKP threshold: gross turnover above **Rp4.8 billion/year** (PMK 197/PMK.03/2013) requires PKP registration. Below it, registration is voluntary. PMK 81/2024 Art. 60 keeps the small-business exception. A floated cut to Rp3.6 billion is **not** official. — [pajak.go.id](https://pajak.go.id/id/berita/pajak-sinjai-jelaskan-relaksasi-batas-waktunya-pengajuan-pkp); [IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/pengusaha-wajib-daftar-pkp-kecuali-kategori-ini/); [Hukumonline](https://www.hukumonline.com/berita/a/batasan-omzet-pengusaha-kecil-wajib-ppn-dinaikkan-lt52c66e310cd53/)
- PMK 81/2024 (Coretax regulation, effective 1 Jan 2025): Art. 385(1) lists required faktur contents (faktur code, serial number, creation date, signature, among others). Art. 387(1) requires PKP to upload faktur via the Taxpayer Portal or an integrated channel. Art. 375(1) says input VAT is credited against output VAT in the same tax period. — [DDTC: Coretax provisions in PMK 81/2024](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1806618/isi-bab-ketentuan-teknis-pelaksanaan-coretax-djp-dalam-pmk-812024); [pajak.go.id: pengkreditan pajak masukan 2025](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/artikel/pengkreditan-pajak-masukan-1-januari-2025-begini-penjelasannya)
- Coretax faktur can carry a 6-digit goods/services code per line item. — [DDTC](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1807234/coretax-pkp-bisa-cantumkan-kode-barangjasa-6-digit-ke-faktur-pajak)
- Returns: a VAT return for taxable goods is documented with an electronic **nota retur** under PMK 81/2024 (Arts. 286–290, replacing PMK 65/2010). — search summary of [jurnal.id](https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/pencatatan-retur-penjualan-otomatis/) (secondary; article numbers not verified)

### Inferences
- **Tax flags in SKU master data** (one per SKU):
  - `ppn_treatment` ∈ {TAXABLE_NONLUXURY (code 04, DPP 11/12), EXEMPT_PP49 (code 08), NON_BKP}
  - a 6-digit Coretax goods code
  - Typical OMI sembako: rice, eggs, salt and plain gula pasir are exempt. Minyak goreng, mie, susu kemasan/formula, kopi, snacks, drinks, Panadol and merchandise are taxable.
- **Computation**: for a price P excluding VAT, DPP = P × 11/12 and PPN = 12% × DPP = 11% × P. For tax-inclusive retail prices, PPN = P_incl × 11/111. Store DPP, the nilai-lain DPP and PPN per invoice line, and round per Coretax.
- **Input VAT from OMI (faktur arriving about 2 weeks later)**: this is crediting input VAT. Under PMK 81/2024 it is normally credited in the same period as the faktur date; late crediting within 3 months is a long-standing rule but not verified here. The system should track a `pending_faktur_masukan` state per OMI purchase/SPH and match on receipt by supplier NPWP, faktur number and DPP. Input VAT is only useful if the koperasi is PKP.
- **Is Unit Toko a PKP?** The koperasi as a legal entity (not the unit) holds the NPWP and PKP status. Because the billing section issues faktur pajak to BNI divisions, the koperasi is evidently already a PKP, so its whole turnover, including the shop, is in scope. A unit cannot separately be PKP. Confirm with the koperasi.
- **Sales to BNI divisions**: BNI is a BUMN. Certain SOEs are appointed VAT collectors (pemungut PPN, code 03), and if so they withhold the PPN rather than pay it to the koperasi. Verify whether BNI acts as pemungut for these purchases. This affects invoice and faktur code and cash expected. Not researched here.
- **Walk-in retail sales**: retail PKPs may issue simplified faktur (struk/receipt without buyer identity), which are reported in aggregate ("digunggung"). Long-standing practice; not re-verified under PMK 81/2024 in this session.
- **Faktur timing**: the long-standing rule is that a faktur is created at delivery of goods, or at receipt of payment if payment comes first (advance payment requires a faktur uang muka). A monthly "faktur gabungan" for deliveries to the same buyer within a month is allowed. Neither rule was verified in PMK 81/2024 text. Treat both as likely-unchanged but to be confirmed. For the system: link each Surat Jalan (delivery date) to the invoice and faktur, and alert when a delivery has no faktur by month-end.

### Gaps

## 5. Koperasi-specific rules relevant to shop records

### Takeaway
Koperasi accounting moved from SAK ETAP to **SAK EP (Entitas Privat), effective 1 Jan 2025**. Kemenkop policy (Permenkop UKM 2/2024; one source cites 2/2025) requires separating **member vs non-member transactions**, per-unit (and consolidated) reporting, and related-party disclosure. SHU from member transactions is distributed partly as "jasa anggota" in proportion to each member's transactions. Non-member profit is not distributed to members as jasa. The shop must therefore record buyer member status (and member ID) per sale.

### Cited Findings
- DSAK IAI made SAK EP effective 1 Jan 2025, replacing SAK ETAP. Kemenkop said its older 2015 accounting regulations (No. 12/13/14/Per/M.KUKM/IX/2015) no longer fit. SAK ETAP did not provide consolidation for multi-unit koperasi or separation of member/non-member revenue; the new policy addresses both. — [Kemenkop/IAI presentation on Permenkop 2/2024](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Materi_Permenkop%202%20thn%202024_Kemenkop%20dan%20UKM.pdf); [DDTC: peraturan baru kebijakan akuntansi koperasi](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1801567/peraturan-baru-kebijakan-akuntansi-koperasi-baca-di-sini)
- SAK EP is mandatory for savings-and-loan koperasi and recommended for real-sector koperasi (one source). Another source cites Permenkop 2/2025 as mandating SAK EP. **The regulation number conflicts between sources.** — [IAI/Kemenkop materials](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Materi_Permenkop%202%20thn%202024_Kemenkop%20dan%20UKM.pdf) (via search summary)
- UU 25/1992 Art. 45: SHU from member transactions is allocated to reserves, jasa anggota, management and other funds. SHU from non-member transactions goes to reserves and other funds, not jasa anggota. Jasa anggota requires member transaction records across all units. — [tirto.id: SHU](https://tirto.id/mengenal-sisa-hasil-usaha-shu-cara-pembagian-dan-contohnya-gnKJ); research summarized from [IKOPIN journal](https://journal.ikopin.ac.id/index.php/ecoopsday/article/view/4694/3722)
- Non-member transactions at koperasi typically occur in units such as canteen and shop. — [jurnal.fe.unram.ac.id (study)](https://jurnal.fe.unram.ac.id/index.php/prakbis/article/download/3356/1432/13763) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- The sales header needs: `customer_type` ∈ {MEMBER, NON_MEMBER, INSTITUTION (BNI division)}, `member_id` when MEMBER, and `payment_type` ∈ {CASH, CREDIT (payroll deduction/potong gaji), VOUCHER}. Credit sales to members create member receivables, which are usually settled via payroll deduction in employee koperasi. That practice is common but not sourced here.
- Unit Toko should produce unit-level revenue/COGS/margin reports that feed koperasi consolidation, with member/non-member splits.
- Whether a BNI division counts as a "member" transaction (divisions are not members; employees are) affects SHU and should be decided with the koperasi's AD/ART.

### Gaps

- UU 25/1992 status. Note that the 2020 Cipta Kerja law and later koperasi bills amended parts of it. Whether a new UU Perkoperasian was enacted by 2026 was not checked.
- PPh treatment (for example PP 55/2022 final 0.5% for gross turnover at or below Rp4.8 billion, and its time limits for koperasi) was not researched. It is likely irrelevant if the koperasi's turnover is above the threshold.
