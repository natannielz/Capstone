# Order handling, warehouse and shipping staff workspaces: patterns from Indonesian e-commerce, WMS, B2B procurement and logistics tools (for Unit Toko)

Scope note: back-office (seller/staff) screens only. Sources researched 21 Oct 2026. Primary sources are the platforms' own help/edu centres (Shopee Seller Education Hub, Tokopedia Care, TikTok Shop/Tokopedia Seller University, INAPROC/e-Katalog v6, PaDi UMKM, Ginee, Jubelio, Accurate, Odoo, Lalamove, MileApp). Two B2B procurement marketplaces turned out to be the closest analogues to Unit Toko's BNI-branch flow: **INAPROC Katalog Elektronik v6** (government buyer, PPK inspection, digital BAST) and **PaDi UMKM** (Telkom-run marketplace for BUMN buyers; BNI is a BUMN; B2B flow = PO, then shipping, then signed BAST, then invoice).

## 1. Marketplace seller order queues (Shopee "Pesanan Saya", Tokopedia, TikTok Shop): tabs, sub-filters, batch actions, SLA countdowns, order detail

### Takeaway
All three marketplaces arrange the seller queue as **status tabs that follow the parcel's lifecycle** (to pay, to ship, shipped, done, cancel/return). Inside the "to ship" tab they split **"needs processing"** from **"processed, waiting for courier"**. Every ship-side screen carries a **deadline** (Shopee "Batas Pengiriman", Tokopedia "Batas Respon", TikTok "Ship within 24 hours"), with urgency buckets (Terlambat / <24 jam / >24 jam) and sort-by-deadline. Bulk work happens on a separate mass-shipping page that has filters, document generation (label, pick list, pack list, manifest) and spreadsheet upload for couriers that are not integrated. Tokopedia dropped the explicit "Terima Pesanan" (accept) step in Sept 2024, so arranging shipment now counts as accepting. B2B procurement platforms (e-Katalog v6, PaDi UMKM) still require an explicit **Terima / Tolak** with a deadline.

### Cited Findings
**Shopee: tabs and statuses**
- Seller-side statuses in Pesanan Saya: **Belum Bayar** (checked out, awaiting payment), **Perlu Dikirim** (paid or COD, to be processed and shipped), **Dikirim** (handed to courier), **Selesai** (received and confirmed by buyer), **Pengembalian/Pembatalan**, and **Pengiriman Gagal** (courier failed, e.g. COD refused or wrong address, parcel returned to seller). — [Shopee Edu: Mengenal Status Pesanan Saya (14-08-2025)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/3844)
- To arrange shipping: Pesanan Saya > tab **Perlu Dikirim** > filter **Perlu Diproses**. Once shipping is arranged, the order stays in **Perlu Dikirim > Telah Diproses** until the parcel is handed to the courier, then it moves to **Dikirim** with status "Sudah Dikirim". Shopee separates "mengatur pengiriman" (choosing Pick up or Antar ke counter) from "mengirimkan pesanan" (physical handover), and both must happen before the deadline. Since 22 Oct 2025 the store address cannot be changed while arranging shipment. — [Shopee Edu: Perbedaan antara mengatur pengiriman dan mengirimkan pesanan](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16208)
- After handover, the status change from Dikemas to Dikirim "takes 1x24 jam". Integrated couriers use **Resi Otomatis** (tracking number printed on the label). Non-integrated couriers such as "Jasa Kirim Toko" (the store's own courier) use **Resi Manual**: the printed label has no tracking number, and the seller must enter the courier's resi afterwards before the status becomes Dikirim. Shopee advises printing 2 copies of the label (one stuck on the parcel, one kept as proof) and enforcing "satu resi untuk satu nomor pesanan". — [Shopee Edu: Cara Cetak Resi Shopee (13-02-2026)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7092)

**Shopee: mass shipping page (Pengiriman Massal)**
- Filters on the mass-shipping page: **Batas Pengiriman** (options: Semua, Terlambat, Kurang dari 24 jam, Lebih dari 24 jam), **Jasa Kirim**, **Status Pesanan** (shown only when some orders need shipping re-arranged after a failed pickup), **Tipe Pesanan** (1 produk / beberapa produk / Pesanan Gabungan), **Pre-Order**, **Waktu Pesanan Siap Dikirim**, and **Urutkan** (by ready-to-ship date, ship deadline, or product ID). — [Shopee Edu: Mengatur Pengiriman Massal](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7089)
- Shopee's own throughput tips: do single-product orders first, sort by Batas Pengiriman "Tercepat ke Terlama", and load more orders per page. Mass pickup asks for pickup date/time, a note for the courier, and confirmation of the pickup address. For non-integrated couriers the flow is: download the order list, fill resi numbers in Excel, upload, confirm, and the status becomes "Dikirim". — [Shopee Edu 7089](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7089)
- Four mass shipping documents: (1) **Label Pengiriman**; (2) **Daftar Pesanan per Produk**, a consolidated pick list of everything to take from the warehouse; (3) **Daftar Pesanan**, a per-order item list used to check packing; (4) **Dokumen Manifes**, the seller-to-courier handover list signed by both parties at pickup. Manifest fields: Nama Penjual, No. HP Penjual, Alamat Pengirim, Jasa Kirim, Tanggal Pengemasan, Jumlah Paket, Daftar pesanan. A manifest holds at most 200 orders (50 per page). Orders are added with "+ Pilih Pesanan" or by typing/scanning the resi into "Scan atau masukkan no. resi". The document filter includes **Status Dokumen Dicetak**. — [Shopee Edu: Jenis & Download Dokumen Pengiriman Massal (16-10-2025)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7090)

**Shopee: SLA, late shipment, cancellation**
- **Masa Pengemasan** examples (non-pre-order): Reguler/Next Day orders before 12.00 WIB must ship the same working day by 23.59; orders after 12.00 ship the next working day by 23.59. Same Day orders before 14.00 ship the same day by 16.59. Instant: arrange within 1 hour (45 min for Instant Prioritas). **Jasa Kirim Toko**: next working day by 23.59. Sunday, national holidays and courier non-operating days are excluded. **Saturday counts** from 1 Mar 2026 for Star/Star+ sellers and from 1 May 2026 for Shopee Mall. — [Shopee Edu: Memahami Masa Pengemasan dan Batas Waktu Pengiriman (16-10-2026)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7093). This conflicts with an older buyer help-centre article that excludes Saturday: [help.shopee.co.id 136118](https://help.shopee.co.id/portal/4/article/136118-[Pengemasan-Pesanan]-Berapa-lama-pesanan-saya-dikemas-oleh-Penjual). Treat the older one as superseded.
- A multi-product order's deadline follows the product with the longest packing period. Orders shipped after the deadline count toward **Tingkat Keterlambatan Pengiriman**: ≥10% over 7 days gives 1 penalty point, and ≥10% plus ≥50 late orders gives 2. Sellers can appeal courier-caused lateness within 14 days via Kesehatan Toko. — [Shopee Edu: FAQ Batas Waktu Pengiriman](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16209); [FAQ Masa Pengemasan](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/20231)
- Auto-cancellation happens if the seller does not arrange shipment by the deadline, or arranges it but the parcel is not dropped off or picked up within 2 days after the deadline (Reguler) or 1 day (Next Day/Same Day/Instant). — [Shopee Edu 7093](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7093)
- Seller-initiated cancellation offers **Batalkan Sebagian** (partial: not allowed if more than 50% of quantity/value, or after "Atur Pengiriman" for non-instant shipping) or **Pembatalan Semua Pesanan**. Both affect "Tingkat Pesanan Tidak Terselesaikan". Buyer cancellation requests are **Diterima/Ditolak** by the seller. — [Shopee Edu: Mengelola dan Merespons Pembatalan Pesanan (30-06-2026)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/23750). For a non-instant buyer cancellation the seller has 1x24 jam to respond, after which the system auto-cancels within the next 1x24 jam. — [Shopee Help: jenis pembatalan pesanan](https://help.shopee.co.id/portal/4/article/87564-[Pembatalan-Pesanan]-Apa-saja-jenis-pembatalan-pesanan-di-Shopee?previousPage=other+articles)

**Tokopedia (now operated on the TikTok/Tokopedia Seller Center)**
- From **10 Sep 2024**, sellers process new orders directly with **Request Pick Up** or by entering the resi (AWB), without clicking **Terima Pesanan**. A new order has status **Siap Dikirim** in tab **Siap Dikirim**. Once responded to, it shows **Menunggu Pick Up** or **Menunggu Status Resi** in tab **Dalam Pengiriman**. Other list statuses: Sedang Dikirim, **Resi Diubah**, **Produk Dikembalikan**, **Pesanan Tiba**. The platform defines three separate timers: **Batas respon** (deadline for resi/pickup request), **Batal otomatis** if the seller never confirms, and **Batal otomatis** if confirmed but the courier never picked up. — [Tokopedia Care: Batas Waktu Respon Pesanan dan Konfirmasi Pengiriman](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/batas-waktu-respon-pesanan-dan-konfirmasi-pengiriman)
- Older rules (before 10 Sep 2024, still applied to third-party-managed accounts): Terima Pesanan within 1x24 jam and Konfirmasi Pengiriman within 2x24 jam for Reguler/Kargo/Ekonomi, with courier-specific cut-offs for Next Day/Same Day/Instant. Weekends and national holidays are excluded. — [same Tokopedia Care article](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/batas-waktu-respon-pesanan-dan-konfirmasi-pengiriman)
- **Perpanjang Waktu Pesanan**: the seller picks a time and reason (max 3 working days), the buyer must approve, and the remaining response deadline shows on the right. If the buyer rejects, the order auto-cancels without a performance penalty. — [Tokopedia Care: Perpanjang Waktu Pesanan](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/penjual-ingin-perpanjang-batas-waktu-proses-pesanan)
- **Tolak Pesanan** for out-of-stock should be done while the order is still new or unprocessed. Cancelling after accepting or confirming shipment costs reputation points. — [Tokopedia Care: Menolak Pesanan Karena Stok Habis](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/bagaimana-cara-menolak-pesanan). Tokopedia's T&C still describe the seller options "menerima, menerima sebagian, atau menolak", where partial acceptance requires buyer consent. — [Tokopedia Terms](https://www.tokopedia.com/terms)

**TikTok Shop / Tokopedia Seller Center**
- Seller Center app status filter: All order, **Awaiting shipment**, **Awaiting collection**, In transit, Delivered, Unpaid, **On hold**, Completed, Cancelled. Urgency filters: All urgent task, **Ship within 24 hours or less**, **Auto-cancelling within 24 hours or less**, **Shipping overdue**, **Cancellation requested**, **Abnormal packages**, **Return/refund requested**. The order card shows status, status detail, order ID, price and order info. — [TikTok Shop by Tokopedia Seller University: Managing Orders on Seller Center App](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8228106950166273&lang=en)
- Older TikTok Seller App guide (Indonesian): the home summary shows counts for **Menunggu pengiriman**, **Menunggu pickup** and **Retur pending**, each linking into Kelola Pesanan. Tab rules from that guide: Belum Dibayar (auto-cancel if unpaid after 48 hours); Menunggu Diproses (seller should process within 72 hours: print the Daftar Pengambilan pick list, click **Siapkan Kiriman** to schedule pickup within 48 hours, print the label); Menunggu Pickup (courier collects within 24 hours); Sedang Dikirim; Terkirim; Selesai (6 days after delivery); Dibatalkan (seller has 48 hours to answer a buyer cancel request or it is auto-approved, rejecting requires a reason, and the buyer can appeal). — [Seller University: TikTok Seller App](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10007452&lang=id-ID). The guide is undated; a third-party 2024 guide instead says "Atur Pengiriman" must happen within 3 working days, so the exact SLA is uncertain. — [Stickler (2024)](https://stickler.live/id/2024/02/14/opsi-pengiriman-toko-tiktok/)
- TikTok generates A6 or A5 PDF labels and can also print packing list or pick list. Labels can be reprinted under Kelola Pesanan > Label Pengiriman. For seller-shipped orders there are bulk upload templates: shipping info (ID Pesanan, ID SKU, Nama Produk, Variasi, Jumlah, Nama Penyedia Pengiriman, ID Pelacakan) and delivery status (Hasil Pengiriman, Alasan Pengiriman Gagal). — [Stickler (2024)](https://stickler.live/id/2024/02/14/opsi-pengiriman-toko-tiktok/) (third-party source)
- **Gabungkan Pesanan**: when the seller clicks "Atur Pengiriman", Seller Center suggests merging eligible orders into one parcel. A merged parcel can be changed until it reaches 'Menunggu Pengambilan', and there is a filter "Digabungkan dengan pesanan lain". — [Seller University: Menggabungkan Pesanan (2025-03-27)](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753823779079937&lang=id-ID)

**B2B procurement marketplaces (closest to Unit Toko)**
- **e-Katalog v6 (INAPROC)**: a new order appears under Transaksi > Pesanan with status **"Menunggu Konfirmasi Penyedia"**. The penyedia (supplier) clicks **Terima Pesanan** or **Lihat Detail** and has 3 working days, after which the order auto-cancels. The detail page shows Nomor pesanan, Tanggal dibuat, **Tenggat waktu untuk merespon**, Ringkasan pesanan, Informasi pengiriman and Informasi pembeli. Accepting leads into signing the **Surat Pesanan** (purchase order) via PrivyID with an OTP. — [INAPROC: Menerima Pesanan (2025-07-31)](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8176727583247-Menerima-Pesanan)
- **PaDi UMKM**: a red count badge marks new orders. Tabs: **Pesanan Baru** (buttons **Tolak Pesanan** with a reason, **Terima Pesanan**, **Nanti Saja**), then **Diproses** (Kirim Pesanan: choose courier), **Siap Dikirim** (print label; for an own courier enter nama kurir, no. HP kurir, tanggal and waktu pengiriman), **Dalam Pengiriman** (Lacak for a third-party courier, **Upload Bukti Penerimaan** for an own courier) and **Selesai**. Billing sits under Tagihan > Pesanan Diterima > Unggah Faktur Pajak. — [PaDi UMKM: Apa yang Harus Dilakukan Setelah Proses Transaksi Berhasil (2023)](https://info.padiumkm.id/media/apa-yang-harus-dilakukan-setelah-proses-transaksi-berhasil). Orders auto-cancel if the seller does not respond within 4x24 jam. — [PaDi UMKM Syarat dan Ketentuan](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan)

### Inferences
- **Peninjauan pesanan (/ordering/reviews)** should follow the B2B pattern (e-Katalog/PaDi), not current Tokopedia: an explicit **Terima / Tolak (alasan wajib) / Terima sebagian** decision per shop-channel suborder, with a visible **tenggat respon** countdown and a home count badge. Partial acceptance should require a buyer-visible reason, which mirrors Tokopedia's "menerima sebagian" with buyer consent and Shopee's "Batalkan Sebagian" ≤50% rule.
- Unit Toko's fulfillment pages map onto the marketplace split as follows. *Persiapan kiriman* is Perlu Dikirim > Perlu Diproses. *Penugasan kurir* and *Keberangkatan kiriman* are Telah Diproses / Menunggu Pick Up. *Kiriman* is Dikirim/Dalam Pengiriman. *Penerimaan kiriman* is Pesanan Tiba to Selesai. Each page should show a **deadline column with urgency chips** ("Terlambat", "< 24 jam", "> 24 jam") and **default sort by deadline ascending**.
- Unit Toko's own couriers correspond to Shopee "Jasa Kirim Toko" and e-Katalog "Kurir Penyedia". These channels need a manual resi or delivery-note number, courier name, vehicle number and phone, plus an own-courier proof upload (the PaDi "Upload Bukti Penerimaan" pattern).
- A "Status dokumen dicetak" filter and a separate "processed but not handed over" state prevent double printing and lost parcels between packing and dispatch.

### Gaps
- Could not fetch an authoritative description of Shopee's order *detail* page layout or the exact wording of the per-row deadline text. The "kirim sebelum" countdown label was not verified in sources.
- The Indonesian tab labels of the merged Tokopedia/TikTok Seller Center desktop "Kelola Pesanan" (2025–2026) were not confirmed. Only the English app filter list and an older Indonesian TikTok app guide were found.
- No public docs were found on keyboard shortcuts in Shopee/Tokopedia/TikTok seller centres.

## 2. Indonesian WMS / omnichannel tools (Jubelio WMS, Ginee, Accurate, Odoo): picking lists, packing verification by scan, pick-pack-ship statuses, multiple warehouses, stock opname

### Takeaway
Indonesian WMS tools model outbound work as **explicit stage queues**: To Be Picked, then Picking, then To Be Packed, then Waiting for Outbound, then Shipped. Picking runs on a handheld or phone (PDA): scan the order or resi, then the app guides the picker to the rack with SKU, location and quantity. The step must **block on mismatch** ("Product Quantity Not Match", "Scanned SKU is not match"). Pick lists are **consolidated by SKU and location** across orders (batch/wave picking). Packing is a separate **scan verification** step that can auto-print the label, and handover is documented with a **shipping manifest**. Stock opname everywhere follows **create count task, count (savable, resumable), see selisih, generate penyesuaian**. Ginee locks counted SKUs during the count, and Odoo assigns counts to users with scheduled dates and asks for confirmation if stock moved since the count.

### Cited Findings
**Jubelio WMS**
- The Jubelio WMS feature list includes warehouse location management by lantai/baris/kolom/rak, picking-packing-shipping in one flow, **Batch, Cluster & Zone Picking** (by SKU, order number or rack code), FEFO, a **Shipping Manifest** described as "Bukti serah terima paket pesanan saat penyerahan pesanan dari gudang ke kurir", putaway recommendation, inbound-to-picking-rack transfer recommendations, a WMS mobile app (putaway, picking, packing, stock opname) and a **Laporan Performa Karyawan**. The pain points it lists include "dobel kirim pesanan" and not knowing how many orders are tertunda/terproses/terkirim in real time. — [Jubelio: Sistem gudang (WMS)](https://jubelio.com/produk/omnichannel/gudang/)
- Mobile scanning flow (2021 article): scan the invoice, the app guides the picker to the right rack, and items are re-scanned at packing to confirm the right goods. — [Jubelio blog (2021)](https://jubelio.com/wms-jubelio-fitur-terbaru-untuk-pebisnis-kelola-gudang-mereka/)
- Batch picking: group orders, total each SKU, pick once per rack into a troli/container, **sort back per order at a "meja sortir" or staging area**, then pack and print the label. — [Jubelio: Batch Picking (2026-03-03)](https://jubelio.com/solusi/batch-picking-adalah/)
- Jubelio Enterprise adds **Wave Picking**, **Verifikasi Packing** ("Verifikasi barang hasil picking dan proses pengemasan pesanan sebelum disiapkan untuk pengiriman"), Shipping Manifest, and **Exception Management** covering gagal pickup, gagal kirim, paket tertahan, keterlambatan SLA and retur, plus Claim Management for lost or damaged parcels. — [Jubelio Enterprise](https://jubelio.com/enterprise/)
- Three order-processing modes (2021): Picklist method, Label Pengiriman method, and "Buat Jadi Selesai" (mark done directly). Jubelio claims 10–30 minutes per batch. — [Jubelio: Cara Cepat dan Tepat untuk Proses Pesanan (2021)](https://jubelio.com/proses-pesanan/). Vendor claim: "kurang dari 5 menit/pesanan" for Paxel clients. — [Jubelio WMS page](https://jubelio.com/produk/omnichannel/gudang/) (marketing claim, unverified)

**Ginee (OMS + WMS + PDA)**
- OMS order flow tabs: **Menunggu Diproses** (accept or arrange shipment, multi-channel and multi-store at once), **Menunggu Dicetak** (print label, daftar ambil, packing list, invoice), **Menunggu Dikumpulkan** (reprint or move to shipped) and **Sudah Dikirim**. If arranging shipment fails, the order goes to a **Gagal** list with the reason shown. Orders are auto-routed to warehouse lists by rule: Tanpa Gudang (SKU not mapped), Gudang Sendiri, Gudang Resmi Channel, Gudang Pihak Ketiga. — [Ginee: Cara Memproses Pesanan](https://ginee.com/id/help/cara-memproses-pesanan/)
- WMS order statuses: **Tertunda** (waiting for the admin to dispatch the task), **Sedang Dikirim**, **Selesai**, **Abnormal** (e.g. the customer changes the order and it must be flagged), **Dibatalkan**, Semua. These pages are for "staff senior di gudang". — [Ginee: Panduan Manual Ginee WMS](https://ginee.com/id/help/panduan-manual-ginee-wms/)
- PDA Picking: scan the outbound order number or logistics number, and the app shows SKU name, **location** and quantity. Scans beyond the required quantity are not counted, a mismatch shows "Product Quantity Not Match", and picking only completes when quantities match. "Finish Picking" jumps to the next SKU, then offers "Continue to the next picking task". — [Ginee: WMS-PDA Picking](https://ginee.com/id/help/wms-pda-picking/)
- PDA **Package Review** (pack verification): scan the outbound or logistics number, then scan SKUs. A wrong SKU gives "Scanned SKU is not match with current review SKU", a quantity mismatch blocks completion, and the review status turns green in WMS when done. — [Ginee: WMS-PDA Package Review](https://ginee.com/id/help/wms-pda-package-review/)
- **Pick Bin**: barcode-labelled totes, mass-created up to 100. A bin is bound 1:1 to an outbound list during picking, so the packer scans the bin barcode to pull up the order. Outbound list statuses: **To Be Picked, Picking, To Be Packed, Waiting for Outbound**. **WMS Scan and Pack** can auto-print the shipping label after each pack ("Print the Shipping Label after packing"), and the list shows "Label Printed: Yes/No". — [Ginee: Pick Bin Management (2024-03-22)](https://ginee.com/id/help/ginee-wms-pda-pick-bin-management/)
- Manual outbound: put the cursor in the SKU/barcode box and scan, each scan adds +1. "Outbound Semua" fills all quantities when actual equals planned. The exported **Daftar Ambil** aggregates by SKU and location (e.g. SKUA at rack 001 with quantities 1 and 2 becomes one line, qty 3). — [Ginee: Manajemen Daftar Outbound](https://ginee.com/id/help/fitur-manajemen-daftar-outbound/)
- **Stock opname** statuses: **Menunggu Stock Opname, Sedang Dicek, Selesai, Invalid**. List columns: auto-generated Nomor Stock Opname, Gudang, **Jumlah MSKU / Sudah Dihitung / Perbedaan Jumlah**, Tipe (Berdasarkan Gudang / Berdasarkan MSKU), Pembuat, Waktu Pembuatan/Update. The count can be saved and resumed ("Simpan", then "lanjut stock opname") and the count list can be printed. **SKUs under count are locked for inbound/outbound**, while sales-order reservations are unaffected. After finishing, differences (kurang/lebih) are turned into a **penyesuaian stok** via "buat daftar penyesuaian stok", which appears in stock in/out records. — [Ginee: Cara Melakukan Stock Opname di Sistem ERP (2022)](https://ginee.com/id/help/cara-melakukan-stock-opname-di-sistem-erp/)

**Accurate Online**
- **Perintah Stok Opname** fields: Tanggal SPK, Tanggal Mulai, **Penanggung Jawab** (PIC), **Dikerjakan Oleh** (user email), Keterangan, **Gudang**, **Kategori Barang**, Pemasok (optional). Then **Hasil Stok Opname**: pick the Perintah Opname number and enter counted quantities. Then a **Proses Opname** tab auto-creates the **Penyesuaian Persediaan**, posted to an "Akun Perkiraan Beban Selisih Stok". — [Accurate Help: Cara Mencatat Proses Stok Opname (2026-07-28)](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/persediaan/hasil-stok-opname/mencatat-proses-stok-opname/); [Accurate Help: Mengenal Fitur Perintah Stok Opname (2026-07-21)](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/persediaan/perintah-stok-opname/mengenal-fitur-perintah-stok-opname)
- Items are excluded from the opname if they fall outside the chosen gudang/kategori/pemasok, have zero stock and no history, are inactive, or are non-inventory types. — [Accurate Help: Penyebab Total Barang Stok Opname Berbeda](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/persediaan/stok-opname/stok-opname-tidak-sama-daftar-barang/)
- Penyesuaian Persediaan has type **Penambahan / Pengurangan**, and there is a separate value-only adjustment. — [szetoaccurate.com (2023, partner blog)](https://www.szetoaccurate.com/penyesuaian-persediaan-di-accurate-online/). A third-party blog recommends an approval step and freezing sales/purchase input during opname. — [aplikasiumkm.com](https://aplikasiumkm.com/total-stok-opmane-tidak-sama-dengan-barang-dan-jasa/) (low reliability, not confirmed in official docs)

**Odoo 17 Inventory**
- **Physical Inventory** columns: Location, Product, Lot/Serial, UoM, **Counted Quantity**, **Difference** (green if counted > on hand, red if less), plus optional Inventory Frequency (Days), Last Count Date, Available/On Hand Qty, Accounting Date, **Scheduled Date**, **User** (assigned counter). A recorded count is "not yet applied" until **Apply**, which prompts for an Inventory Reason and writes a stock move for traceability. **Request a Count** asks for Inventory Date, User, Accounting Date and Count mode (**Leave Empty**, i.e. blind count, or **Set Current Value**). Count sheets print to PDF. If stock moved between count and apply, Odoo asks for confirmation. In the **Barcode app, users only see counts assigned to them and scheduled for today or earlier**. — [Odoo 17 docs: Inventory adjustments](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/count_products.html)
- Odoo marks a quant "Quantity has been moved since last count" (`is_outdated`) and restricts the assignee to stock users. — [odoo/odoo commit ffc9e94 (2024)](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/commit/ffc9e94ba309ad0e26979902573f4a99aa4a4534). Manager approval of adjustments is not standard and needs a third-party module (Request for Approval, then Approve/Reject). — [Odoo Apps: Inventory Stock Adjustments](https://apps.odoo.com/apps/modules/17.0/inventory_stock_adjustments)

### Inferences
- **Persiapan kiriman** should be one page with stage tabs (Perlu diambil, Sedang diambil, Perlu dikemas, Siap berangkat) and per-tab counts. It should have a scan box that accepts an order number, delivery-note number or tote barcode. Picking shows *rak/lokasi, SKU, qty* line by line. Packing is a **scan-to-verify** step that refuses wrong SKUs or excess quantity and optionally auto-prints the label or surat jalan on completion.
- For the low order volume of a koperasi, consolidated **Daftar ambil per produk** (Shopee "Daftar Pesanan per Produk", Ginee Daftar Ambil by SKU and location) plus a per-order packing check (Shopee "Daftar Pesanan") is probably enough. Batch/wave picking with sorting tables is overkill unless order volume grows.
- An "Abnormal / Bermasalah" bucket (Ginee) with a reason is a useful sink for orders blocked mid-flow (stock shortfall after reservation, address change, substitution pending). It fits *Persiapan kiriman* and *Keluhan barang*.
- **Hitung barang / Rekonsiliasi jumlah fisik** should copy the opname lifecycle: *Menunggu dihitung, Sedang dihitung, Selesai, Dibatalkan*. Fields: nomor, gudang, cakupan (seluruh gudang / kategori / SKU tertentu), penanggung jawab, petugas hitung, tanggal. Progress shows **Jumlah SKU / Sudah dihitung / Ada selisih**. Offer a **blind count option** (Odoo "Leave Empty"), save and resume, **lock or flag SKUs being counted** (Ginee), and warn if stock moved since the count (Odoo). The step from selisih to penyesuaian should be a separate, approvable step (Accurate "Proses Opname").
- Show **staff performance** (picked/packed per person, time per order), as Jubelio advertises. It supports the "Pekerjaan saya" home.

### Gaps
- No public Jubelio step-by-step doc (edu.jubelio.com) was retrieved for the exact packing screen fields and status names. Jubelio statuses are inferred from feature pages.
- No official Accurate doc confirming a built-in approval workflow for stock opname was found. Only a low-reliability blog mentions approval.
- Multi-warehouse allocation rules beyond Ginee's routing lists (e.g. split shipments across warehouses) were not researched in depth.

## 3. B2B delivery documents (surat jalan, delivery order, packing list, BAST, tanda terima) and how proof of delivery is captured

### Takeaway
Indonesian B2B deliveries chain **PO/Surat Pesanan, then Delivery Order (internal release instruction), then picking/packing, then Surat Jalan (travels with goods; signed by pengirim, pengemudi/kurir and penerima; 2–3 rangkap), then POD/tanda terima, then BAST (formal handover after inspection; basis for invoicing)**. Government and BUMN procurement platforms have digitised the BAST. e-Katalog v6 auto-generates it from transaction data after the buyer's **Pemeriksaan Tepat Guna** (fitness-for-use check covering function, quantity, timeliness), the buyer enters the institution's BAST number, and both sides sign digitally. PaDi UMKM makes BAST upload and verification the trigger for invoicing. POD capture has converged on **photo + e-signature + recipient name + timestamp/GPS**, with photo-quality rules (Lalamove) or AI checks (MileApp/JNE).

### Cited Findings
**Document roles and sequence**
- DO vs surat jalan vs POD: the DO authorises preparing or releasing goods ("before goods are released"), the surat jalan records what is travelling and to where, and the POD confirms receipt. Domestic flow: "Pesanan → Delivery Order → Picking dan Packing → Surat Jalan → Pengiriman → Proof of Delivery". The DO is often internal (admin, warehouse, dispatch). A signed surat jalan can serve as POD only if the parties' procedure says so. Typical POD elements: nama penerima, tanggal & waktu, tanda tangan, **stempel perusahaan**, foto barang, lokasi, carrier digital status, **catatan kerusakan atau kekurangan**. — [muat.com: Delivery Order Adalah](https://muat.com/delivery-order/)
- The packing list gives per-box/pallet contents (quantity, weight, dimensions, type) so receivers can check without opening everything, and the picking list guides warehouse staff. — [forwarder.ai (2026-05-20)](https://forwarder.ai/blog/jenis-dokumen-logistik/). Deliveree: land shipments use surat jalan, invoice, packing list and resi, and the surat jalan "wajib ditandatangani oleh pengirim, supir, dan penerima". — [Deliveree: 10 Jenis Dokumen Pengiriman (2025-12-27)](https://www.deliveree.com/id/bisnis/jenis-dokumen-pengiriman-barang-wajib/)

**Surat jalan fields**
- Kledo template: No. Dokumen, Tanggal; pengirim (perusahaan, alamat, telepon, email); penerima (nama, perusahaan, alamat, telepon); Nomor Referensi (BOL / No. Pesanan / Resi); Carrier/Ekspedisi; Nomor Kendaraan; Petugas/Driver; item table (No, Deskripsi, Jumlah, Satuan, Berat, Catatan) with Total Jumlah and Total Berat; delivery instructions; **three signature blocks (Pengirim / Pengantar-Carrier / Penerima) each with Nama and Tanggal**; the statement "Barang telah diterima dalam kondisi baik dan sesuai jumlah"; optional company stamp; print 2–3 rangkap (pengirim, penerima, arsip). — [Kledo: Contoh Surat Jalan Barang (2025-12-02)](https://kledo.com/blog/contoh-surat-jalan-barang/)
- Mandatory components: nomor & tanggal, identities, item details (nama, jumlah, satuan, **kondisi saat dikirim**), **reference numbers (PO, invoice/faktur)**, and signature fields for pengirim, kurir, penerima. Distributors add the customer PO number, sales/courier name and route. — [OnlinePajak (2024-02-21)](https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-efaktur-ppn/contoh-surat-jalan-barang/)
- B2B variant columns: Nomor Surat Jalan (e.g. "SJ-GDG/2026/09/045"), Tanggal, gudang asal, perusahaan tujuan, **Nomor PO/DO terkait**, items per SKU (kode, nama, jumlah, satuan, berat), **Jumlah Total Koli/Pallet**, **Nama Pengemudi dan Nomor Kendaraan**, both signatures, **Catatan Selisih** (if physical count does not match the document). Mismatched recipient identity is "penyebab tertahan paling umum di lapangan". — [api.co.id (2026-09-04)](https://api.co.id/blog/surat-jalan-pengiriman-barang/)

**BAST (Berita Acara Serah Terima)**
- Government BAST template (Kemenkeu PMK 265/2014 annex): Kop surat; Nomor; "Pada hari ini … tanggal … tahun …"; tempat; PIHAK PERTAMA (Nama, NIP, Pangkat/Gol, Jabatan) and PIHAK KEDUA; articles: Pasal 1 handover of goods with value in figures and words and an attached list, Pasal 2 responsibility transfers on signing, Pasal 3 made in **2 rangkap bermeterai**. — [Kemenkeu JDIH: Format BAST](https://jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/api/download/fulltext/2014/265~PMK.05~2014PerLamp.pdf) (dated 2014; the legal-template style is still the reference)
- e-Katalog v6 BAST (real 2026 example): No Referensi Dokumen, Tanggal BAST Dibuat (with time), **No. Surat Pesanan**, **No. BAST** (institution numbering), Pemesan = PIHAK KEDUA (unit, penanggung jawab, jabatan e.g. PPK, NPWP, alamat), Penyedia = PIHAK PERTAMA. **Ringkasan Pengiriman per Termin**: Nama Penerima, Permintaan Tiba, **Pesanan Tiba** (timestamp), **Selesai Pemeriksaan**, Alamat Pengiriman, Kurir ("Kurir Penyedia"), Berat, Biaya Pengiriman, product table. Clauses: all goods delivered per Surat Pesanan; delivery dates; 100% delivered and inspected; "**BAST ini menjadi acuan utama … dalam melakukan penagihan**" and valid "**tanpa memerlukan tanda tangan basah**"; buyer must pay; responsibility transfers. — [Kesbangpol Banten: Paket Pengadaan Selesai 2026 (PDF)](https://kesbangpol.bantenprov.go.id/storage/kesbangpol/files/866/Upload/Tahun%202026/Paket%20Pengadaan%20Telah%20Selesai%202026.pdf)
- e-Katalog v6 receipt flow (buyer/PPK): open Daftar Pesanan, then **Konfirmasi Pengiriman/Pesanan**, then pop-up **"Pemeriksaan Tepat Guna"**: choose the delivery stage with status "Telah Tiba", enter the **tanggal selesai pemeriksaan**, tick the statement that function, quantity and arrival time are as agreed, and optionally attach an inspection document. Status becomes "**Sudah Diperiksa**". Then **Buat BAST**: choose the termin, enter the BAST number ("Ubah Nomor BAST" allowed until signed), review, sign digitally (BSrE passphrase), and the BAST goes to the penyedia to countersign (sub-status "Menunggu Konfirmasi Penyedia"). With termin payments, a **BAPP** is issued per termin and the BAST only at the last termin. PPK is "sangat disarankan" to video the unboxing to support insurance or compensation claims. — [INAPROC: Panduan PPK – Membuat & Mengelola Pesanan (2026-08-22)](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8167729525263-Panduan-PPK-Membuat-Mengelola-Pesanan); [INAPROC: cara menerima pesanan v6 (2024)](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/7629851252111-Bagaimana-cara-menerima-pesanan-pada-Katalog-Elektronik-versi-6)
- e-Katalog v6 supplier-side shipping (Kurir Penyedia) requires updating each stage. **Sedang Dikemas**: confirm via a "Pilih Pengiriman" pop-up showing tanggal permintaan tiba, alamat, qty per product. **Dalam Pengiriman**: enter Nomor Resi, plus optional Nomor Kendaraan and Nama Kurir. **Telah Tiba**: Tanggal Diterima is auto-filled and locked, Jam Diterima is auto-filled but editable within the shipped-to-now window, **Foto Bukti Penerimaan** is optional. For multi-location or multi-stage orders the supplier picks the "Tahap Pengiriman", and order statuses become **Persiapan Pengiriman, Dikirim Sebagian, Tiba Sebagian**. — [INAPROC: Memproses Pengiriman (2025-08-03)](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8177194599183-Memproses-Pengiriman). The manual also says to record a packing video for insurance and to print the label before sending. — [Katalog Elektronik Manual Book Penyedia 2024 (Biro PBJ Kaltim)](https://biropbj.kaltimprov.go.id/storage/file/MANUAL%20BOOK%20-%20KATALOG%20ELEKTRONIK%20%20PENYEDIA%202024.pdf)
- e-Katalog T&C: the supplier must pack and mark parcels and include an identity document for the goods. Own couriers must give the buyer a "tanda bukti pengiriman". The buyer must inspect promptly, and both sign the BAST (or BAPP for termin) digitally. Risk of loss or damage with a Kurir Penyedia stays with the supplier, and late delivery can incur **denda keterlambatan**. — [INAPROC: S&K Katalog Elektronik v6 (Versi 1.5, 2025-11-28)](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/14461422000143-Syarat-dan-Ketentuan-Katalog-Elektronik-Versi-6-Versi-1-5)
- PaDi UMKM B2B flow: the buyer issues a **Purchase Order**, the seller ships to the PO address, **seller and buyer sign the BAST**, the seller uploads it, PaDi verifies it, and **if valid PaDi issues the Invoice** and the buyer pays. If invalid, the seller is notified to re-upload. In PaDi's T&C, BAST is defined as the document showing the buyer has received the product. — [PaDi UMKM Syarat dan Ketentuan](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan). The PaDi BAST document states it is valid without wet signatures. — [PaDi UMKM BAST document page](https://padiumkm.id/document/bast/INV-)

**Proof-of-delivery capture**
- Lalamove: a business account enables **Tanda Terima Digital (E-signature)** ("Aktifkan tanda tangan digital") and POD photos. A valid POD photo shows the goods clearly in good light during unloading, plus a **location indicator** (house/unit number, lift sticker with address, customer door, or company logo for non-residential sites). Invalid: blurry, zoomed, unrelated, **selfies**, covered camera. Drivers must **not photograph faces or home interiors**. The status "**Pengiriman Gagal**" means "Driver tidak upload foto bukti pengiriman". The driver can upload a signature *or* a POD photo, and the photos are viewable under the Drop Off location in order details. — [Lalamove FAQ ID](https://www.lalamove.com/id/faq)
- Lalamove add-on "**Perjalanan Pulang Pergi**" exists for deliveries where the driver must bring back documents such as a **signed surat jalan**. Business accounts allow multiple operators and give access to delivery reports. — [Lalamove Business ID](https://www.lalamove.com/id/business). Driver SOP: photograph items at pickup, **count items unloaded at drop-off**, photograph the handover before completing the order, and confirm the recipient identity or ask the name of a substitute recipient. — [Lalamove: Prosedur Menjalankan Pesanan](https://www.lalamove.com/id/driver/prosedur-menjalankan-pesanan)
- MileApp (own-fleet/courier task app used by JNE): the admin bulk-loads tasks and **auto-dispatches** them by region and flow (delivery, pickup, handover). The courier taps "start", geolocation and time are recorded, recipient contact is one tap away, and e-POD is a **photo + on-screen signature stored with location and time**. — [MileApp × JNE case study (2026-05-05)](https://mile.app/id/case-studies/kolaborasi-mileapp-dan-jne-untuk-perkuat-logistik-indonesia). JNE's ~24,000 couriers had many dark, blurry or irrelevant POD photos. Image recognition that only accepts photos with a person or building and no blur raised valid e-POD from "<~20%" to "~83%". — [MileApp: Meningkatkan Kualitas POD JNE (2026-05-05)](https://mile.app/id/case-studies/meningkatkan-kualitas-proof-of-delivery)
- Lalamove defines a POD as covering sender/recipient info, goods (type, quantity, condition) and recipient signature. Digital forms include e-signature, photo, and an **OTP confirmed by the recipient**. — [Lalamove blog: Pentingnya Dokumen Bukti Pengiriman (2024-11-28)](https://www.lalamove.com/id/blog/driver/pentingnya-bukti-pengiriman-barang/)

### Inferences
- **Keberangkatan kiriman** should generate a **Surat Jalan** per shipment. Fields: nomor SJ, tanggal, Unit Toko as pengirim, cabang BNI as penerima (nama cabang, alamat, PIC, telepon), nomor pesanan/suborder and PO/approval reference, item table (kode, nama, jumlah, satuan), **jumlah koli**, kurir/pengemudi, nomor kendaraan, and three signature blocks (Pengirim, Pengantar, Penerima) with a **catatan kondisi/selisih** box. Batch dispatch should also produce a **manifest/daftar serah terima** for the courier (Shopee manifest, Jubelio Shipping Manifest).
- **Penugasan kurir** should mirror e-Katalog's two courier modes. *Kurir sendiri* records kurir name, phone, vehicle number, and manual resi/SJ number. *Pihak ketiga* records ekspedisi and resi or pickup request. Optionally use MileApp-style auto-assignment by area.
- **Penerimaan kiriman (branch receipt)** should be a two-step BAST like e-Katalog's Pemeriksaan Tepat Guna. Step 1: the branch checks quantity per line, condition and timeliness, and records *diterima / kurang / rusak* per item with optional photo or video. Step 2: an auto-generated digital **BAST** where the branch can enter its own BAST number, signed electronically by the branch PIC, then countersigned by Unit Toko. Partial deliveries become "Tiba sebagian" with BAPP-like partial receipts, and the BAST is issued when complete. Per PaDi UMKM, the signed BAST can be the trigger for invoicing/tagihan.
- **Kiriman (tracking)** should store POD artefacts: photo(s), recipient name, signature, timestamp, GPS. Apply Lalamove's photo rules (goods + location marker, no faces) and block completion without a POD for own couriers (Lalamove's "Pengiriman Gagal" if no POD photo).

### Gaps
- No BNI-specific internal procurement or receipt document format was found. Whether BNI branches require a wet-signed, stamped (stempel cabang) or bermeterai BAST is unknown and should be confirmed with the client.
- Deliveree and JNE/J&T/SiCepat business POD documentation was not retrieved in detail. Only the Lalamove and MileApp/JNE e-POD material is cited.
- No source was found on the legal acceptance of digital tanda tangan for koperasi-to-BUMN BAST outside e-Katalog/PaDi. e-Katalog uses BSrE/PrivyID certified e-signatures.

## 4. Complaint / return flows (Shopee Pengembalian Barang/Dana, Tokopedia Komplain/Pusat Resolusi, TikTok, B2B)

### Takeaway
Marketplace complaint handling is **deadline-driven and evidence-driven**. Each request shows a countdown in an "Aksi" column. The seller picks from a fixed menu of resolutions: refund only, return + refund, return + replacement, reject/appeal. The seller can escalate to platform CS ("Minta Bantuan", "Ajukan Banding"), and the evidence required depends on the reason (photos/video of packing, resi, the condition of the returned item). Returns have their own clock: the buyer must ship within 5 days (Shopee), and the seller has 2 working days after receiving the return to dispute. A "Prioritas" filter shows requests due in under 1–2 days. Platforms push sellers to record **packing videos/CCTV** as dispute insurance, and e-Katalog pushes buyers to record unboxing.

### Cited Findings
**Shopee**
- The seller works on the **Pengembalian/Pembatalan** page. Tabs/filters: Semua, **Dana Dikembalikan ke Pembeli** with filter "Tinjau Pengembalian Dana Disetujui Shopee", **Pengembalian** with filter **Perlu Validasi**, and **Banding Diajukan** (appealed requests). The deadline shows in the **Aksi** column. An appeal requires a reason and evidence uploaded via "Upload Bukti" following "Pedoman". The "Ajukan Banding" button is hidden while the return is in transit (**grey-out period**) or after the deadline. — [Shopee Edu: Cara Mengajukan & Memeriksa Hasil Banding (2026-08-09)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/24725)
- Evidence matrix (appeal reason, then required evidence). "Saya telah mengirim produk dalam kondisi baik": photo/video of the item working before shipping. "Barang rusak saat pengiriman": photo (mandatory) showing resi, quantity, condition before shipping. "Barang pengembalian rusak / tidak lengkap / berbeda / sudah dipakai": photos of return packaging and resi, the damaged parts, inner and outer packaging. Fake-product claims: distributor certificate or trademark proof. Packing video helps for high-value items. — [Shopee Edu 24725](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/24725)
- Appeal windows: **7 hari kerja** after grey-out ends if the return has not been received; **2 hari kerja** after receiving the returned item; **3 hari kerja** for refund-only after the buyer's request is accepted. Working days include Saturday and exclude Sunday and holidays. Filter **Prioritas** shows requests due within <1 or <2 days. — [Shopee Edu: Ketentuan Pengajuan Banding (13-08-2026)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16761)
- During Shopee's review the seller can submit evidence ("Kirim Bukti") or **request an early return**. The buyer then has **5 calendar days** to ship to the seller's return address, otherwise the request is cancelled and payment is released to the seller. — [Shopee Edu: Merespons Pengajuan Pengembalian (2025-12-26)](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/24020)
- For "barang tidak sampai / tidak lengkap" the seller needs to do nothing because Shopee processes the claim. Missing the evidence deadline means Shopee decides on available evidence. Most appeals resolve in 2 working days. Seller compensation (if the appeal is valid) is capped at the refund minus fees. Sellers can attach chat screenshots of buyer agreements. — [Shopee Edu: FAQ pengembalian barang/dana](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/17055/faq-pengembalian-barang-dan-dana)

**Tokopedia (Seller Center)**
- Since **Dec 2024**, complaints can only be answered via Seller Center or the Seller Center app, under the menu **Pesanan Dikomplain**, in the **Pusat Resolusi**. Seller actions: accept the buyer's proposed solution (Pengembalian Dana) or **Minta Bantuan** (Tokopedia CS mediates). For a shipping-cost dispute, attach the physical resi. For "tidak lengkap", attach photos of the full product before shipping. — [Tokopedia Care: Menanggapi Komplain Pengembalian Dana](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/cara-menanggapi-komplain-pengembalian-dana)
- Return flows. **Retur dan Pengembalian Dana**: the seller enters the return address ("Masukkan Alamat"), tracks the buyer's return resi via **Lacak**, and confirms receipt, after which the refund is released. **Retur dan Penggantian Produk**: after receiving the return, the seller ships a replacement and enters its resi ("Masukkan No. Resi"), and the buyer closes the complaint when it arrives. Problems with the returned item go to "Minta Bantuan" or "Lainnya". — [Tokopedia Care: Menanggapi Komplain Retur](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/cara-menanggapi-komplain-retur-dan-pengembalian-dana)
- Seller responses: **Pilih solusi** (accept or propose) or **Tolak pengajuan**. Tolak is not available for "pesanan belum diterima" complaints, where the seller instead chooses "Minta Tokopedia Investigasi Kiriman". A **countdown** appears on the complaint page. Tokopedia advises packing documentation (complete photos, phone video or **CCTV at the packing station**). — [Tokopedia Care: FAQ Pusat Resolusi Penjual](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/faq-pusat-resolusi-seputar-penjual)
- Buyers must file "Produk yang Diterima Bermasalah" within **2x24 jam** of "terkirim" status. From **9 May 2025**, if the buyer does not confirm within **6 calendar days**, funds go to the seller. — [Tokopedia Care: Barang Sudah Diterima Namun Produk Berkendala](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/barang-sudah-diterima-namun-produk-berkendala)

**TikTok Shop / e-Katalog / PaDi**
- The TikTok Seller App home shows a **Retur pending** count, and Terkirim/Selesai orders carry return sub-statuses: Pengembalian Sedang Diproses / Berhasil / Ditutup. — [Seller University: TikTok Seller App](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10007452&lang=id-ID)
- e-Katalog v6: with a Kurir Penyedia the supplier bears damage or loss risk and may be fined. Disputes are handled by chat first, then the Pusat Bantuan. BAST amendments go through the Pusat Bantuan. — [INAPROC S&K v6](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/14461422000143-Syarat-dan-Ketentuan-Katalog-Elektronik-Versi-6-Versi-1-5). PaDi UMKM: buyer–seller disputes are settled outside the platform, PaDi supplies transaction history and has final say on unresolved cases, and claims after receipt confirmation are the buyer's responsibility. — [PaDi UMKM S&K](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan)

### Inferences
- **Keluhan barang (/fulfillment/cases)** should be a queue with tabs by stage, for example *Perlu ditanggapi, Menunggu barang kembali, Barang retur diterima (perlu dicek), Pengganti dikirim, Selesai*. Each row needs a **"Batas respon" countdown** and a **Prioritas** filter (< 1 hari, < 2 hari).
- Resolution options should be a fixed menu adapted to B2B: *Kirim kekurangan* (short shipment), *Tukar barang / substitusi*, *Retur + kredit/potong tagihan*, *Tolak dengan bukti*. Required evidence should depend on the reason: photos at receipt from the branch, packing photos and surat jalan copy from the store, and the signed BAST line notes.
- Because the BAST is signed after inspection, most "keluhan barang" should be raised **at receipt** (per-line kurang/rusak in Penerimaan kiriman), which automatically opens a case linked to the BAST. Post-BAST complaints should require stronger evidence or a time window, like Tokopedia's 2x24 jam and PaDi's "claims after confirmation are the buyer's responsibility".
- Encourage packing photos (or a photo per koli) at *Persiapan kiriman* as cheap dispute evidence. Both Shopee and Tokopedia tell sellers this is what wins disputes.

### Gaps
- TikTok Shop Indonesia's current seller return-response deadlines were not confirmed. Only cancellation response (48 h) appeared, in an undated guide.
- No source describes how B2B buyers in Indonesia handle substitutions (barang pengganti) formally, e.g. whether a substitution requires an addendum to the PO/Surat Pesanan. e-Katalog uses "Addendum" for changed arrival dates, and its applicability to item substitution was not verified.

## 5. What makes these queues fast for staff (scan flows, counts per tab, aging/priority indicators, printing, bulk actions)

### Takeaway
Speed comes from a few repeated patterns: **counts on every tab and on the home summary**, **urgency buckets and sort-by-deadline** as defaults, a **separate list of failed or needs-redo items with the reason**, **scan-first input** (scanner focus in a text box, each scan +1, scan the resi to add to the manifest, scan the tote to open the order), **hard blocking on mismatch** instead of warnings, **auto-print on completion**, a **printed/not-printed status**, and **spreadsheet round-trips** for bulk resi entry. Mobile PDA/phone apps handle floor work (pick, pack, count), and the web handles admin tasks.

### Cited Findings
- Count summaries: the TikTok Seller App home shows counts for Menunggu pengiriman / Menunggu pickup / Retur pending linking to the queue — [Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10007452&lang=id-ID). PaDi UMKM shows a red number badge next to "Pesanan" — [PaDi UMKM](https://info.padiumkm.id/media/apa-yang-harus-dilakukan-setelah-proses-transaksi-berhasil).
- Urgency filters: TikTok/Tokopedia "Ship within 24 hours or less", "Auto-cancelling within 24 hours or less", "Shipping overdue", "Abnormal packages" — [Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8228106950166273&lang=en). Shopee "Batas Pengiriman: Terlambat / <24 jam / >24 jam" and sort "Tercepat ke Terlama" — [Shopee Edu 7089](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7089). Shopee returns "Prioritas" filter (<1 or <2 days) — [Shopee Edu 16761](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16761).
- Redo/failure lists: Shopee's Status Pesanan filter appears only when orders need re-arranging after a failed pickup — [Shopee Edu 7089](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7089). Ginee moves failed shipment arrangements to a "Gagal" list with the failure reason — [Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/cara-memproses-pesanan/). ### Inferences
- **Pekerjaan saya** should show a card per queue with *count + overdue count + due-today count*, as in the TikTok home summary, linking into a pre-filtered queue. Odoo's "assigned to me and due today or earlier" rule is a good default filter for count tasks and picking tasks.
- Every staff queue page should get **counts on tabs**, an **urgency chip column** (Terlambat / Hari ini / Besok / Nanti), a **default sort by deadline**, and a **"Perlu diulang / Bermasalah" tab with a reason column**.
- Put a **persistent scan/search box** at the top of Persiapan kiriman, Keberangkatan kiriman and Hitung barang. It should accept order number, SJ number, resi or SKU barcode, autofocus after each action, and have Enter-to-confirm. Mismatches block with a clear Indonesian error (e.g. "SKU tidak sesuai dengan pesanan", "Jumlah melebihi pesanan").
- Treat printing as state: show "SJ dicetak / Label dicetak" flags, offer bulk print from a selection, optionally auto-print on pack completion, and keep a reprint action in later stages.
- For a small koperasi with own couriers, a simple courier mobile view (task list, then navigate/call, then POD photo + signature + recipient name) gives most of the MileApp value without route optimisation.

### Gaps
- No official documentation of keyboard shortcuts (non-scanner) in Shopee/Tokopedia/TikTok/Ginee/Jubelio web consoles was found. Keyboard-flow recommendations above are inferences from the scanner-focus patterns.
- No quantitative studies (throughput or error rates) beyond vendor claims (Jubelio "<5 menit/pesanan") and MileApp's POD-validity numbers (~20% to ~83%) were found.
