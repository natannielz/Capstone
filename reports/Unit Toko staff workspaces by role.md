# Turn Unit Toko's staff pages into document queues

Every reference system studied here (Shopee Seller Centre, the merged Tokopedia/TikTok Seller Center, Jubelio, Ginee, Accurate, Mekari Jurnal, Odoo, SAP, Amazon Business, Coupa, INAPROC e-Katalog and PaDi UMKM) gives each back-office role the same three surfaces, and Unit Toko should do the same: a **work queue split into lifecycle tabs** with counts, a deadline column and urgency sorting; a **detail page for one dated document**, such as a price proposal, order, surat jalan, invoice or purchase order, rather than a freely editable record; and a **checker view** that names the proposer, shows old against new, and disables "Setujui" for the person who proposed the change. Unit Toko's BNI flow resembles consumer marketplaces far less than it resembles the B2B procurement platforms INAPROC e-Katalog v6 and PaDi UMKM, which keep an explicit Terima/Tolak with a response deadline and make a digitally signed BAST, issued after the buyer inspects the goods, the trigger for billing. Indonesian law touches only a handful of screens, but where it applies it changes the arithmetic. BNI is a BUMN pemungut, so on any payment above Rp10 juta it withholds the PPN and 1.5% PPh 22, and a **Rp22.200.000 invoice arrives as a Rp19.700.000 transfer**. The Faktur Pajak now carries a **17-digit number that DJP assigns** in Coretax and must be uploaded by the 20th of the following month, a wrong 22-digit branch **NITKU cannot be fixed with a pengganti** and forces cancel-and-reissue, and **e-Meterai Rp10.000** belongs on a kuitansi stating more than Rp5 juta received, not on the tagihan, surat jalan or BAST. Document numbering, terbilang, surat jalan signature blocks and tukar faktur bundles are custom, and the queue and review patterns are best practice. Build what touches money and tax first (wapu-aware invoices and payment matching, a Faktur Pajak status with an upload deadline, and buyer tax data checked by a second person), then a "Pekerjaan saya" home with real counts, a dated price-proposal document, and a BAST-at-receipt flow that hands fulfilment over to billing.

## Law shapes a handful of screens; custom and best practice shape the rest

### Three tiers of obligation, kept apart on every screen

This report sorts every recommendation into one of three tiers. **Legal** means a statute or regulation with a sanction: the PPN computation, Faktur Pajak format and timing, buyer tax identifiers, BUMN collection of PPN and PPh 22, bea meterai, the national time zones and the koperasi financial statement set. **Custom** means what Indonesian buyers, accountants and warehouse staff expect although no law requires it: document number formats, Roman-numeral months, terbilang, surat jalan signature blocks, BAST layout, tukar faktur bundles, and EYD V Rupiah notation, which is an official spelling standard but carries no sanction for private software. **Best practice** covers the interaction patterns that proven tools share, such as lifecycle tabs, counts, diffs, per-line approval and visible four-eyes cues.

No source in this research makes maker/checker itself a legal duty for a koperasi. It is Unit Toko's own control, and it matches both Accurate's "minimal dua pengguna" approval condition ([akuntansionline.co.id](https://akuntansionline.co.id/fitur-penyetuju-transaksi-di-accurate-online/)) and the separate Maker, Approver and Releaser users of BNI's own BNIdirect ([BNI](https://www.bni.co.id/en-us/home/bnidirect)). BNI branch staff therefore already know the four-eyes model.

### Every role gets a queue, a document and a checker view

Indonesian seller tools all organise work **by lifecycle state, not by category**, and each tab allows only its own actions. Tokopedia's integrated Seller Center offers Activate, Edit and Delete on deactivated products and nothing at all on frozen ones ([Tokopedia Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10000818&lang=en)), while PaDi UMKM's order tabs run from Pesanan Baru through Diproses, Siap Dikirim and Dalam Pengiriman to Selesai, with a red count badge on new orders ([PaDi UMKM](https://info.padiumkm.id/media/apa-yang-harus-dilakukan-setelah-proses-transaksi-berhasil)). Three further patterns recur. A "needs fixing" tab shows the reason: Shopee's "Perlu Tindakan" tab gives every blocked product a reason, a suggested fix, a deadline and a fix button ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/1741/Memeriksa-dan-Memperbaiki-Produk-yang-Diblokir)), and Ginee moves failed shipment arrangements into a "Gagal" list with the reason attached ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/cara-memproses-pesanan/)). Deadlines appear as urgency buckets, as in Shopee's mass-shipping filter by Batas Pengiriman into Terlambat, under 24 hours and over 24 hours ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7089)). And two status axes stay apart: Accurate separates document state (draf, diajukan, disetujui, ditolak) from fulfilment state (Menunggu Diproses, Sebagian diproses, Terproses), and closes a document with a stated reason instead of deleting it ([Solusi Akuntansi Indonesia](https://solusiakuntansiindonesia.com/menutup-pesanan-pembelian-pada-accurate-online/)). For Unit Toko this becomes one queue contract shared by every role page: lifecycle tabs that each carry a count, a "Perlu tindakan" tab with a reason column, a deadline column with Terlambat / Hari ini / Besok / Nanti chips and a default sort by deadline, and two status chips wherever a document both waits for approval and gets fulfilled.

Mature tools treat a change as a document, not as a field edit. Accurate changes prices through a "Penyesuaian Harga Jual" form with a start date and lines of new prices ([account.co.id](https://account.co.id/membuat-penyesuaian-harga-jual/)), and Akeneo routes a contributor's draft to an owner who approves or rejects each change separately and can comment on each one ([Akeneo](https://help.akeneo.com/serenity-collaborative-work/review-product-proposals)). Four-eyes enforcement shows up in the interface, not only on the server. Odoo's "Exclusive Approval" blocks a user who approved one step from approving another step on the same record ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/19.0/applications/studio/approval_rules.html)), and Google Workspace keeps "Requests submitted", which the requester can cancel, apart from the requests the user may approve ([Google](https://support.google.com/a/answer/13790448?hl=en)). Every Unit Toko checker screen should therefore carry four cues: the proposer by name and role ("Diajukan oleh: Budi (Pemeriksa harga), 21 Okt 09.12 WIB"); the change shown as old against new rather than only the new value; a disabled "Setujui" button with the sentence "Anda yang mengajukan perubahan ini" when the viewer is the proposer; and a "Menunggu persetujuan" badge on the live record so that nobody files a second proposal.

### Pekerjaan saya should count work, not chart it

Staff homes in the reference tools open with **counts of things to do, each linked to a pre-filtered queue**: Shopee's To Do List of orders to ship and pending refunds ([LianLian Global](https://global.lianlianpay.com/article_platform_shopee/40-79774.html)), Coupa's homepage "To Do's" ([GitLab Handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/business-technology/enterprise-applications/guides/coupa-guide/)), and the TikTok seller app's counts for awaiting shipment, awaiting pickup and pending returns ([Tokopedia Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10007452&lang=id-ID)). Urgency comes from age and deadlines. RapidApproval shows each approval type's queue count and oldest request, turning red once the request passes its reminder threshold ([RapidApproval](https://appexchange.salesforce.com/partners/servlet/servlet.FileDownload?file=00P3A00000YSbOKUA1)); Shopee's return dashboard filters by "Due in 1 day / Due in 2 days" and shows days left in an Actions column ([Shopee PH](https://seller.shopee.ph/edu/article/20188)); Linear's Priority tab separates notifications that need action from FYI updates ([Linear](https://linear.app/docs/inbox)); and Odoo's barcode app shows a counter only the counts assigned to them and due today or earlier ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/count_products.html)). NN/g calls this an operational dashboard for time-sensitive tasks, where bars and plain numbers beat gauges and pies ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dashboards-preattentive/)).

Unit Toko's `/workspace` page should therefore show one card per work type the user's grants allow, such as "Usulan harga menunggu tinjauan · 4 · tertua 2 hari · 1 lewat batas". Cards are ordered by overdue count and then by age, never alphabetically, and zero-count cards collapse into a single "Semua beres" line. Counts respect the user's scope and include items received by delegation, with a banner such as "termasuk 2 dari delegasi untuk Sari, s/d 30 Okt". FYI items sit in a second tab and may be snoozed; items with a deadline cannot be. Shared desks need an explicit claim ("Ambil", "Ditangani oleh Rina") so that two checkers do not open the same proof. All of this is best practice; no rule governs it.

## Catalog and pricing clerk: a price change is a dated document, not a field edit

The clerk works across Katalog toko (`/administration/catalog`), Harga kemasan (`/catalog/prices`), Katalog publik (`/catalog/public`), and Aturan buka kemasan with its execution log (`/inventory/pack-conversions`, `/inventory/pack-conversion-executions`), the routes and labels in the app's navigation config (local codebase observation, `frontend/src/modules/identity/identity-workflow.ts`). **No legal requirement in this research governs these screens.** Contract prices are a matter of contract with BNI, so everything below is custom or best practice.

**The list should be a lifecycle queue with one search box and a named completeness checklist.** Shopee's 2025 product list finds a product by name, parent SKU, variant code or product ID from a single box and flags low stock in the stock column ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/20515)). Two marketplace habits matter most for Unit Toko. Shopee **underlines a price that has a running or upcoming promotion** and shows the promo price and its dates on hover ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/26134)), a cheap way to show that a price proposal is pending for a SKU without adding a column. Shopee's listing-quality view also lists **named problems that can be filtered and fixed**, such as fewer than three photos, too few attributes or a name outside 25–100 characters ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/6915)), instead of a vague score. Katalog toko should therefore use the tabs Semua · Tayang · Draf · Menunggu persetujuan · Perlu tindakan · Diarsipkan, with a Kelengkapan filter for Tanpa foto, Foto belum lolos pemindaian, Kemasan belum lengkap, Tanpa harga disetujui and Belum di katalog mana pun. Rows in Perlu tindakan carry the reviewer's reason and a "Usulkan ulang" button. Editing opens in a non-modal side panel, which NN/g recommends so that the clerk can copy values from neighbouring rows ([NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/data-tables/)). Bulk actions state the selection scope ("12 dipilih dari 340") and preview the change before confirming ([HashiCorp Helios](https://helios.hashicorp.design/patterns/table-multi-select); [BASIS](https://design.basis.com/patterns/bulk-editing)). Bulk price and package edits should use the template round trip Indonesian sellers already know: download only the selected rows, edit only the highlighted columns, upload, and download any failed rows with an "Alasan gagal" column ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/6953)).

**Packaging needs an explicit unit hierarchy, which no Indonesian marketplace offers.** The marketplace models fall short in opposite directions. Shopee allows at most two variation types, each with its own price and stock ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16726)), and counts its minimum purchase across all variations of a product, so buyers can mix variants to reach it ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/13251)). Shopify B2B instead sets increment, minimum and maximum **per variant**, and variants cannot be combined to reach a minimum ([Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/catalogs/quantity-pricing)). Accurate's multi-satuan model supplies the right vocabulary: up to five unit levels with the smallest first, a conversion ratio per level such as 1 Lusin = 12 PCS, and one default price per unit ([penjualanonline.id](https://penjualanonline.id/cara-mengatur-default-harga-jual-multi-satuan-di-accurate-online/)). The package editor should state each rule as a sentence, "1 Karton = 24 Botol · minimum 2 karton · kelipatan 1", and say plainly whether minimum and increment apply per SKU or across the family, because the two platforms differ and buyers notice. The isi (contents per pack) should drive a price per base unit, which reviewers need when they judge a change. Aturan buka kemasan should use the same smallest-first sentences and the same draft → ajukan → tinjau flow.

**Harga kemasan should become a proposal document with a dated effect, a comparison view and per-line decisions.** Accurate's price-change form already sets this pattern for Indonesian bookkeepers. Its header holds the price category and a "Mulai Berlaku" date, its Excel import reports success and failure counts before saving ([Accurate](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/penjualan/penyesuaian-harga-diskon/impor-penyesuaian-harga-diskon)), approval can be switched on for exactly this transaction type ([Accurate](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/pengaturan/preferensi/cara-mengaktifkan-pengaturan-persetujuan/)), and that approval can carry a minimum-value threshold and a one, two or all approvers condition ([akuntansionline.co.id](https://akuntansionline.co.id/fitur-penyetuju-transaksi-di-accurate-online/)). SAP Ariba's catalog comparison separately lists items whose **price changed by more than a chosen percentage** ([SAP Ariba, older help](https://ep.tce.adquira.com/AribaBuyer/ariba/resource/en_US/help/content/admin/catalog/catalog_workspace/compare_versions.htm)), and Akeneo lets the reviewer decide each change, sending a rejected change back into the maker's draft rather than discarding it ([Akeneo](https://help.akeneo.com/serenity-collaborative-work/review-product-proposals)). The Unit Toko proposal should hold a header with catalog, Mulai berlaku and alasan, and lines showing SKU, kemasan, harga lama, harga baru, Δ Rp, Δ % and harga per satuan dasar. The reviewer gets a queue showing pengusul, tanggal diajukan, jumlah baris and largest Δ %, a "perubahan > X %" filter, and Setujui/Tolak per line with a note alongside "Setujui semua". Approved prices should become **new dated price rows**, never overwrites, as the Odoo community recommends ([Odoo forum](https://www.odoo.com/forum/help-1/best-practice-for-changing-product-sales-prices-in-odoo-18-295322)); the same thread notes that Odoo has no native old-to-new variance report for auditors. Each SKU then gains a "Riwayat harga" tab with harga, berlaku sejak, pengusul, penyetuju and alasan. Shopee locks price fields while a promotion runs ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/5431)) and Jubelio advises creating a new promotion rather than editing a running one ([Jubelio](https://edu.jubelio.com/kelas-online/courses/jubelio-online-training/pelajaran/cara-membuat-promosi-harga-coret/)); the equivalent rule here is **one open proposal per SKU**.

**Katalog publik is a per-catalog publication flag whose effect should be visible before approval.** Shopify B2B publishes each product per catalog with a TRUE/FALSE flag, and when several catalogs apply the lowest price wins ([Shopify](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/catalogs)). Amazon Business shows business prices only to registered business customers ([Sell on Amazon](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-b2b-prices)). Unit Toko's page already states that guests see catalogs **without prices** and that a second person must approve (local codebase observation). The improvement is to list, inside the proposal, exactly which products will become visible or hidden, and to add a "Lihat sebagai tamu" preview like Tokopedia's side preview of the product page ([Tokopedia Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=10000818&lang=en)). Photo rules can follow the marketplace consensus of 1:1 squares of at least 600×600 px, at most 5 MB, at most nine images and a solid, preferably white background on the main image ([Tokopedia Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7065663894374161&lang=id-ID)), with the first photo as cover ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16726)). Each photo also needs a chip from Unit Toko's virus scan (Dipindai, Lolos, or Ditolak with the reason). No marketplace documents a scan-state UI, so this part is Unit Toko's own design, and a photo that has not passed the scan should keep the product out of any public catalog.

**What to leave out matters as much.** Shopee's "spam harga" market-price auto-block and Amazon's competitor price recommendations ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16726); [Sell on Amazon](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-b2b-prices)) do not fit contract prices to BNI, which should be checked against the last approved price rather than the market. Performance tags, ad nudges, gamified missions, opaque 0–100 quality scores and batch limits sized for 50,000 products add noise. Tokopedia's old inline price cell, which applied on Enter, bypasses the checker and should become an "Usulkan harga" action on the row. Wholesale tiers are unnecessary today; if Unit Toko ever adds them, it should enforce Shopee Grosir's rule that each of up to five tiers is cheaper than the last ([Shopee SG](https://seller.shopee.sg/edu/article/14209/what-is-the-wholesale-feature)) at the moment the tier is entered.

## Order desk and warehouse: copy e-Katalog and PaDi, not Shopee

The shop-side order pages are Permintaan masuk (`/ordering/requests/incoming`), Peninjauan pesanan (`/ordering/reviews`, which serves both branch spending approval and shop acceptance) and Penggantian barang (`/ordering/substitutions`). The fulfilment pages are Persiapan kiriman (`/fulfillment/preparations`), Penugasan kurir (`/fulfillment/courier-assignments`), Keberangkatan kiriman (`/fulfillment/dispatches`), Kiriman (`/fulfillment/shipments`), Penerimaan kiriman (`/fulfillment/receive`), Keluhan barang (`/fulfillment/cases`), and the counting pages Hitung barang (`/inventory/counts`) and Rekonsiliasi jumlah fisik (`/inventory/source-reconciliations`). The obligations here are custom and contractual rather than statutory, with one exception: once a Faktur Pajak exists, returns enter the Coretax nota retur flow.

**Acceptance should be explicit and time-boxed, as on B2B platforms.** Tokopedia removed the separate "Terima Pesanan" step on **10 September 2024**, so arranging pickup or entering a resi now counts as accepting ([Tokopedia Care](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/batas-waktu-respon-pesanan-dan-konfirmasi-pengiriman)). The B2B platforms kept the step. In e-Katalog v6 a new order sits at "Menunggu Konfirmasi Penyedia" with a visible **response deadline of three working days**, after which it cancels automatically ([INAPROC](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8176727583247-Menerima-Pesanan)); PaDi UMKM offers Tolak Pesanan with a reason, Terima Pesanan or Nanti Saja ([PaDi UMKM](https://info.padiumkm.id/media/apa-yang-harus-dilakukan-setelah-proses-transaksi-berhasil)) and cancels after **4×24 jam** without a response ([PaDi UMKM S&K](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan)). Partial acceptance is customary but bounded: Tokopedia's terms allow "menerima sebagian" only with buyer consent ([Tokopedia](https://www.tokopedia.com/terms)), and Shopee forbids a partial cancellation above 50% of quantity or value ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/23750)). Tokopedia also lets a seller request a deadline extension of up to three working days, which the buyer must approve ([Tokopedia Care](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/penjual-ingin-perpanjang-batas-waktu-proses-pesanan)). The shop-acceptance view of Peninjauan pesanan should therefore offer Terima, Tolak with a required reason, and Terima sebagian with a reason the buyer can see, under a "Tenggat respon" countdown and beside an "Ajukan perpanjangan" request to the branch. Branch-approval items and shop-acceptance items have different columns and different actors, so they belong in **separate saved views** of the same page. Permintaan masuk maps onto Mbizmarket's RFQ step, where the seller answers a buyer request and may adjust payment terms, delivery estimate and taxes before the PO ([Mbizmarket](https://www.mbizmarket.co.id/cara-transaksi)).

**Fulfilment pages map onto the marketplace stages, and each needs a deadline column.**

| Unit Toko page | Marketplace equivalent |
|---|---|
| Persiapan kiriman | Shopee "Perlu Dikirim > Perlu Diproses" |
| Penugasan kurir and Keberangkatan kiriman | "Telah Diproses / Menunggu Pick Up" |
| Kiriman | "Dikirim" |
| Penerimaan kiriman | "Pesanan Tiba" to "Selesai" |

Shopee separates arranging shipment from physical handover and requires both before the deadline ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16208)). Its packing windows show how precisely the SLA has to be defined: regular orders placed before 12.00 ship the same working day by 23.59, orders using the store's own courier ship by 23.59 the next working day, and Sundays and holidays do not count ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7093)). Unit Toko should set its own working-day SLA per order type against a holiday calendar, show the resulting deadline as Terlambat / Hari ini / Besok / Nanti chips on every fulfilment page, and sort by it. Ginee's warehouse view keeps an "Abnormal" bucket for orders blocked mid-flow ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/panduan-manual-ginee-wms/)); Unit Toko needs a matching "Bermasalah" tab with a reason column for cases such as a stock shortfall after reservation or a pending substitution.

**Persiapan kiriman should be a scan-driven, blocking flow.** Ginee's outbound list moves through To Be Picked, Picking, To Be Packed and Waiting for Outbound ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/ginee-wms-pda-pick-bin-management/)), its exported pick list merges quantities for the same SKU and rack into one line ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/fitur-manajemen-daftar-outbound/)), and its pack verification **refuses to complete** when a scanned SKU or quantity does not match ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/wms-pda-package-review/)). Shopee bundles four mass-shipping documents (label, consolidated pick list, per-order packing list and courier manifest) and filters orders by whether their documents have been printed ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7090)). At a koperasi's volume, a consolidated "Daftar ambil per produk" plus a per-order packing check is enough; the batch and wave picking with sorting tables that Jubelio sells ([Jubelio](https://jubelio.com/solusi/batch-picking-adalah/)) is overkill until volume grows. The page should have stage tabs for Perlu diambil, Sedang diambil, Perlu dikemas and Siap berangkat; a persistent scan box that accepts an order number, surat jalan number or SKU barcode; blocking errors such as "SKU tidak sesuai dengan pesanan"; "SJ dicetak" and "Label dicetak" flags; and an optional photo per koli. Shopee's appeal evidence centres on proof of the goods' condition before shipping ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/24725)), and Tokopedia recommends CCTV at the packing station ([Tokopedia Care](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/faq-pusat-resolusi-seputar-penjual)), so packing photos are the cheapest dispute insurance available.

**Penugasan kurir and Keberangkatan kiriman should produce a surat jalan and a manifest.** e-Katalog's own-courier flow ("Kurir Penyedia") records the resi, vehicle number and courier name at "Dalam Pengiriman", then auto-fills the received date at "Telah Tiba" with an optional receipt photo ([INAPROC](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8177194599183-Memproses-Pengiriman)). Unit Toko's own couriers fit that model, while third-party couriers need only the courier name and a resi. The surat jalan is custom, not law, but its fields have converged. Indonesian templates give it a number and date, sender and recipient blocks, the customer's PO reference, an item table with code, name, quantity and unit, total koli, driver name and vehicle plate, a box for shortfalls or differences, and **three signature blocks for Pengirim, Pengantar and Penerima**, printed in two or three copies ([Kledo](https://kledo.com/blog/contoh-surat-jalan-barang/); [api.co.id](https://api.co.id/blog/surat-jalan-pengiriman-barang/)). Deliveree states that sender, driver and recipient must all sign it ([Deliveree](https://www.deliveree.com/id/bisnis/jenis-dokumen-pengiriman-barang-wajib/)). Batch dispatch also needs a handover manifest; Shopee's holds up to 200 orders and adds a parcel when its resi is scanned ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/7090)).

**Kiriman and Penerimaan kiriman should end in proof of delivery and a BAST.** Proof-of-delivery capture has converged on photo, signature, recipient name, time and location ([MileApp](https://mile.app/id/case-studies/kolaborasi-mileapp-dan-jne-untuk-perkuat-logistik-indonesia)), and photo rules are what make it work. Lalamove requires the goods plus a location marker and bans selfies and faces ([Lalamove](https://www.lalamove.com/id/faq)), and JNE raised valid e-POD photos from **under about 20% to about 83%** once image checks rejected blurry or irrelevant shots ([MileApp](https://mile.app/id/case-studies/meningkatkan-kualitas-proof-of-delivery)). The decisive pattern is e-Katalog's two-step receipt. The buyer first runs a **"Pemeriksaan Tepat Guna"** on a stage marked "Telah Tiba", recording the inspection date, confirming that function, quantity and timing match, and optionally attaching a document. The buyer then creates a BAST, may enter the institution's own BAST number until signing, signs it digitally, and sends it to the supplier to countersign ([INAPROC](https://bantuan.inaproc.id/hc/id-id/articles/8167729525263-Panduan-PPK-Membuat-Mengelola-Pesanan)). A real 2026 e-Katalog BAST states that it is "acuan utama … dalam melakukan penagihan" and is valid without a wet signature ([Kesbangpol Banten](https://kesbangpol.bantenprov.go.id/storage/kesbangpol/files/866/Upload/Tahun%202026/Paket%20Pengadaan%20Telah%20Selesai%202026.pdf)), and PaDi UMKM issues the invoice only after the BAST is uploaded and verified ([PaDi UMKM S&K](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan)). Unit Toko's Penerimaan kiriman already confirms receipt by scanning the courier's QR code (local codebase observation). It should add a per-line check of diterima, kurang or rusak with a photo, then generate a BAST with a field for the branch's own BAST number, and make that BAST the document that releases billing. Partial deliveries show "Tiba sebagian" until the last part arrives. Whether BNI branches require a wet-signed, stamped or bermeterai BAST, and whether an uncertified e-signature satisfies them outside e-Katalog's certified BSrE/PrivyID signing, was not resolved in the sources and must be confirmed with BNI.

**Keluhan barang should be deadline-driven, and most complaints should start at receipt.** Marketplace complaint queues show a countdown in an Actions column, filter "Prioritas" requests due in under one or two days ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/16761)), and ask for evidence that depends on the reason ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/24725)). Tokopedia gives buyers **2×24 jam** after delivery to report a problem ([Tokopedia Care](https://www.tokopedia.com/help/article/barang-sudah-diterima-namun-produk-berkendala)), and PaDi makes claims after receipt confirmation the buyer's responsibility ([PaDi UMKM S&K](https://padiumkm.id/syarat-dan-ketentuan)). Unit Toko should open a case automatically from any "kurang" or "rusak" line in the BAST check and require stronger evidence for complaints raised after the BAST is signed. Its resolution menu should be B2B-specific: Kirim kekurangan, Tukar/substitusi through Penggantian barang, Retur dengan nota kredit or potong tagihan, and Tolak dengan bukti. The legal step applies when a Faktur Pajak was already issued: the return then runs through Coretax, where the buyer creates an electronic nota retur dated on the return day and the seller approves or rejects it under "Retur Pajak Keluaran" ([DJP Coretaxpedia](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/retur-barang)), and the case page must show that status.

**Hitung barang should follow the stock-opname lifecycle and post adjustments separately.** Ginee's counts move through Menunggu Stock Opname, Sedang Dicek, Selesai and Invalid, show SKUs counted against SKUs with a difference, and **lock SKUs under count for inbound and outbound** ([Ginee](https://ginee.com/id/help/cara-melakukan-stock-opname-di-sistem-erp/)). Odoo supports a blind count ("Leave Empty") and asks for confirmation if stock moved since the count ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/17.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/inventory/warehouses_storage/inventory_management/count_products.html)), and Accurate turns counted differences into a separate inventory adjustment posted to a stock-difference expense account ([Accurate](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/persediaan/hasil-stok-opname/mencatat-proses-stok-opname/)). Unit Toko should count blind by default, lock or flag SKUs under count, and require approval to turn a variance into an adjustment.

## Finance: BNI's withholding makes every large invoice arrive short

### The tax rules that change the numbers

**PPN on ordinary goods is 12% of an 11/12 base.** PMK 131/2024 limits the full 12% rate to luxury goods; everything else pays 12% × DPP nilai lain, where DPP nilai lain is 11/12 of the price, an effective 11% ([MUC Consulting](https://muc.co.id/id/article/resmi-berlaku-tarif-ppn-untuk-barang-mewah-12-non-barang-mewah-11)). **BNI collects that PPN itself on larger payments.** PMK 8/2021 was revoked by the Coretax omnibus PMK 81/2024 ([JDIH BPK](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/160062/pmk-no-8pmk032021)), whose Pasal 292 names BUMN as pemungut and whose Pasal 294 exempts payments of **at most Rp10 juta including PPN** that are not split from a larger transaction ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1818728/kondisi-yang-bikin-pkp-setor-sendiri-ppn-saat-bertransaksi-dengan-bumn)). **BNI also collects PPh 22**: under PMK 51/2025 a BUMN collects **1.5% of the price excluding PPN**, except on payments of at most Rp10 juta excluding PPN ([Ortax](https://ortax.org/ketentuan-pemungutan-pph-pasal-22-oleh-bumn)). Because the two thresholds use different bases, the system must evaluate them separately for every payment. The arithmetic below is computed from the cited rules.

| | Rp20.000.000 sale | Rp9.200.000 sale |
|---|---|---|
| DPP nilai lain (11/12) | Rp18.333.333 | Rp8.433.333 |
| PPN (12% of DPP) | Rp2.200.000 | Rp1.012.000 |
| Invoice total | Rp22.200.000 | Rp10.212.000 |
| PPN collected by BNI | Yes: total is above Rp10 juta incl. PPN | Yes: total is above Rp10 juta incl. PPN |
| PPh 22 collected by BNI | Rp300.000 (1.5% of the price) | None: price is at most Rp10 juta excl. PPN |
| **Transfer Unit Toko should expect** | **Rp19.700.000** | **Rp9.200.000** |

If the system does not expect these net amounts, every BNI payment will look like an underpayment.

**Coretax made the Faktur Pajak stricter.** PER-11/PJ/2025 sets the code plus serial number at **17 digits**: two for the transaction code, two for the status code and a 13-digit serial made of a two-digit year and an eleven-digit DJP sequence. DJP assigns the number automatically on approval, so e-NoFa number requests no longer exist ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1811127/per-11pj2025-pertegas-ketentuan-kode-dan-nomor-seri-faktur-pajak)), and the upload deadline moved to the **20th of the following month** ([Pajakku](https://pajakku.com/artikel/per-11pj2025-pokok-perubahan-faktur-pajak-dalam-coretax)); an older DJP article that cites the 15th is superseded. The buyer identifier is BNI's single 16-digit NPWP, and each receiving branch is a place of business with a **22-digit NITKU**, the NPWP plus a six-digit sequence ([Ortax](https://ortax.org/penggunaan-nitku-sebagai-pengganti-npwp-cabang)). Coretax requires the 22-digit NITKU whenever the buyer is identified by NPWP ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/review/konsultasi-coretax/1808368/muncul-error-nitku-pembeli-tidak-valid-apa-yang-harus-dilakukan)), and a wrong buyer NITKU **cannot be corrected with a faktur pengganti**; the faktur must be cancelled and a new one issued ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1811670/keliru-cantumkan-nitku-pembeli-tak-bisa-bikin-faktur-pajak-pengganti)). That makes the branch's tax data the riskiest data in the whole finance flow.

The transaction code is the main open tax question. PER-11/PJ/2025 assigns code **03** to deliveries to pemungut other than government agencies, such as a BUMN, and code **04** to DPP nilai lain ([Pajakku](https://pajakku.com/artikel/per-11pj2025-pokok-perubahan-faktur-pajak-dalam-coretax)). DJP's priority rule puts a collector code ahead of 04, as it puts the government-pemungut code 02 ahead of 04 ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1808116/ramai-bahas-kode-faktur-simak-lagi-hierarki-penggunaannya)). BNI payments above the threshold therefore most likely take 03 with the 11/12 base ticked, and smaller ones take 04, an inference that a tax adviser must confirm.

**Timing and form are also legal.** The faktur is due **at delivery**, or at payment if payment comes first, and a staged delivery needs one at each termin payment; a monthly faktur gabungan per buyer is permitted ([Online Pajak](https://www.online-pajak.com/tentang-efaktur-ppn/kapan-faktur-pajak-diterbitkan/)), and each termin payment gets its own faktur ([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/bayar-bertahap-begini-cara-isi-faktur-pajak-termin-sesuai-per-11-pj-2025/)). A faktur missing the statutory minimum contents (seller and buyer identity, goods, price, PPN, code and serial, signer) fails the formal test, and the buyer cannot credit it ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1804950/faktur-pajak-harus-penuhi-syarat-formal-dan-material-seperti-apa)). The BAST date should therefore set the default faktur date, and the invoice list should warn when a delivered order has no faktur in its delivery month.

Bea meterai is narrower than habit suggests. UU 10/2020 charges **Rp10.000** on documents that state **receipt of money above Rp5.000.000**, exempts documents for shipping and receiving goods, and imposes a **100% sanction** when an unpaid meterai is settled later ([UU 10/2020](https://pajakku.com/tax-guide/uu/10-tahun-2020)). DJP confirms that an invoice owes meterai only when it serves as proof of payment ([DJP](https://pajak.go.id/en/node/49921)). The kuitansi must therefore be a separate document flagged for e-Meterai above Rp5 juta, and the tagihan PDF must never carry a "LUNAS" stamp. Corporate buyers often demand a stamped invoice anyway as a billing custom; Monotaro requires one at Rp5 juta or more in its tukar faktur bundle ([Monotaro](https://www.monotaro.id/panduan-tukar-faktur-dan-pembayaran)). BNI's own checklist was not found and should be requested.

Three facts stay uncertain and should be confirmed before the logic is hard-coded. Unit Toko's PKP status comes first, because a koperasi below the **Rp4,8 miliar** turnover threshold issues no Faktur Pajak at all ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1813945/omzet-lebihi-rp48-m-pemungutan-ppn-dilaksanakan-tahun-berikutnya)). BNI's continued pemungut status comes second: as of 15 October 2026 DJP was still drafting the regulation on taxing BUMN restructuring under Danantara, and no source says BNI lost the status ([DDTCNews](https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1823309/djp-pp-pajak-untuk-restrukturisasi-bumn-danantara-masih-digodok)). The 03-versus-04 code choice is the third.

### What the finance pages should show

**Tagihan (`/billing/invoices`)** should have tabs for Draf, Menunggu persetujuan, Belum dibayar, Dibayar sebagian, Lunas and Dibatalkan, each with a count and a Rupiah sum. "Terlambat" should be a computed overlay rather than a stored state, and a "Faktur pajak belum terbit" filter should carry a "Batas unggah 20 {bulan}" countdown. The columns should show the branch with the last six digits of its NITKU, the due date with "lewat N hari", the total, "Dipungut BNI", **"Diharapkan diterima"**, the received and remaining amounts, and a separate Faktur Pajak status that runs Belum dibuat → Draf Coretax → Disetujui (NSFP) → Diganti → Dibatalkan. An aging strip above the table (belum jatuh tempo, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, over 90 days) grouped by branch makes collection follow how BNI pays, and bulk WhatsApp or email reminders follow Kledo and Paper.id ([Kledo](https://kledo.com/en/invoices/)). Wapu adds a state Indonesian SME tools rarely show, **"Lunas (netto pungutan)"**, which waits for BNI's withholding slip. The invoice detail and print should show the full calculation (harga jual → DPP 11/12 → PPN → total → minus PPN collected by BNI → minus PPh 22 → jumlah ditransfer) together with the chosen transaction code and the reason for it. The print should carry the commercial fields Indonesian practice expects but no law fixes, namely invoice number, date, due date, payment terms, PO number and delivery reference ([Jurnal](https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/arti-faktur-penjualan-kredit-dan-komponen-pentingnya/)), plus the BNI PO/SPK number, the SJ and BAST numbers, terbilang, and the receiving account with the instruction "cantumkan nomor tagihan di berita transfer".

**Persetujuan penerbitan tagihan (`/billing/invoice-releases`)** should enforce maker ≠ checker and present a pass/fail checklist: a 16-digit NPWP, a 22-digit NITKU that matches the delivery branch, a transaction code consistent with both thresholds, a DPP equal to 11/12 of the price, the PO and BAST attached, an active receiving account, and totals recomputed on the server. A void needs a reason and a second approver, and once a faktur is approved the void stays blocked until the Coretax cancellation or retur is recorded. Accurate already ships approval per document type, including for tukar faktur ([Accurate](https://accurate.id/release-notes-aol/1004194/)).

**Bukti pembayaran and Pemeriksaan pembayaran (`/billing/payments`, `/billing/payment-reviews`)** must treat a screenshot as a pre-screen only, because OJK warns that **AI-forged transfer proofs** circulate ([Metro TV News](https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/N4EC4Jqr-hati-hati-pemalsuan-bukti-transfer-pakai-ai-ini-yang-harus-dilakukan)). Read-out fields (amount, date, reference and destination account) should be compared with the *expected net*, and duplicates caught by file hash and by a repeated bank reference, amount and date. The allocation grid must handle one transfer across several invoices, partial payments, an overpayment parked as "Kelebihan bayar", and pre-filled rows for "PPN dipungut BNI" and "PPh 22 (menunggu bukti potong)". Approval requires a matched bank-mutation line or a second-approver override.

**Mutasi bank and Penerimaan bank (`/billing/bank-statements`, `/billing/bank-receipts`)** should follow the shared Indonesian reconciliation model of a statement import or direct feed, match suggestions on equal amount and date, one-to-many matching, transactions created from unmatched lines, and bank rules for recurring items ([Jurnal](https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/PenerapanFiturCashlink)). Jurnal's direct feeds include BNI, though some bank links fetch only 30 days ([Jurnal](https://www.jurnal.id/id/blog/direct-feeds-bni-jurnal)), and Accurate added match-many in October 2024 ([Accurate](https://accurate.id/release-notes-aol/1012083/)). The statement header shows statement balance, book balance and difference, and "Tutup rekonsiliasi periode" is enabled only when the difference is zero. **Rekening penerimaan (`/billing/receiving-accounts`)** already routes changes through an approved bank channel (local codebase observation); it should also show the account holder name, which must equal the koperasi's legal name, count the open invoices that use the account, and warn before deactivating one that appears on unpaid invoices. **Aturan komersial (`/ordering/commercial-policies`)** is where the tax switches belong (buyer is a BUMN pemungut, Unit Toko is PKP, default transaction code, payment terms and BNI's tukar faktur document list), and changing them should go through the same proposal-and-review flow as prices.

**Buku akuntansi and Laporan akun dan jurnal (`/accounting/books`, `/reporting/accounts`)** carry a regulatory naming duty. Permenkop UKM 2/2024 requires koperasi to use an IAI standard, with SAK EP replacing SAK ETAP from January 2025 ([Kemenkop slides via IAI](https://web.iaiglobal.or.id/assets/files/file_publikasi/Materi_Permenkop%202%20thn%202024_Kemenkop%20dan%20UKM.pdf)). The statement set is Laporan Posisi Keuangan, **Laporan Perhitungan Hasil Usaha** rather than "Laba Rugi", Laporan Perubahan Ekuitas, Laporan Arus Kas and CALK, and it must separate member from non-member revenue. Two accounts, "PPN dipungut oleh pemungut" and "PPh 22 dibayar dimuka", let wapu payments close receivables correctly, and a monthly period lock should follow a zero reconciliation difference and a check that approved fakturs equal the PPN Keluaran ledger.

## Procurement and receiving: one case page should tie eighteen queues together

Unit Toko splits its own purchasing across eighteen pages. Needs and ordering live in Kebutuhan pembelian (`/inventory/replenishment`), Permintaan pengadaan (`/procurement/requests`) and Pesanan pembelian (`/procurement/purchase-orders`). Supplier advances live in Otorisasi uang muka (`/procurement/funding`), Bukti uang muka pemasok (`/procurement/disbursements`) and Verifikasi debit pemasok (`/procurement/settlements`). Receiving and inspection run through Penerimaan pengadaan (`/procurement/receiving`), Pemeriksaan barang pengadaan (`/procurement/inspections`) and Penerimaan mutu barang (`/procurement/quality-acceptances`), and held stock through Penerimaan, Penilaian and Pelepasan stok ditahan (`/procurement/stock-receipts`, `/procurement/stock-valuations`, `/inventory/stock-releases`). Documents, payables and claims sit in Dokumen pemasok dan pencocokan (`/procurement/supplier-documents`), Utang pemasok (`/procurement/supplier-payables`), Klaim pemasok (`/procurement/supplier-claims`) and Pengembalian uang muka (`/procurement/advance-returns`), and supplier master data in Pemasok dan kode sumber (`/procurement/suppliers`) and Rekening pemasok (`/procurement/supplier-banks`). This fine split is a strength for segregation of duties and a weakness for anyone trying to follow one purchase. The law enters only at the input-tax and nota retur steps, and only if Unit Toko is PKP.

**Requests and orders need two status axes, remaining quantities and closing with a reason.** Accurate's PO statuses are "Menunggu Diproses", "Sebagian diproses" and "Terproses", only unprocessed POs can be deleted ([Ultima Solusindo](https://ultimasolusindo.com/menghapus-transaksi-pesanan-pembelian-secara-massal/)), and approval runs sequentially by value band ([doniirvan.id](https://www.doniirvan.id/accurate-online/fitur-accurate-online/pembelian/)). Mekari Jurnal's purchase request has a required "Urgensi" field (Rendah / Sedang / Tinggi) and notifies procurement staff on submission ([Mekari Jurnal](https://help-center.jurnal.id/hc/id/articles/17245524160537-Bagaimana-Cara-Mengelola-Permintaan-Pembelian-pada-Jurnal)), and one request can become several POs that offer only unprocessed quantities and block anything above the remainder ([Mekari Jurnal](https://help-center.jurnal.id/hc/id/articles/23999859514009-Bagaimana-Cara-Mengelola-Pemesanan-Pembelian-dari-Menu-Permintaan-Pembelian)). Accurate also shows the failure mode to avoid: two draft invoices consuming the same PO line, which surfaces only at approval as "terdeteksi terproses dua kali" ([Ultima Solusindo](https://ultimasolusindo.com/error-barang-abc-atas-pesanan-xyz-terdeteksi-terproses-dua-kali-mohon-cek-transaksi-yang-memproses-pesanan-tersebut-saat-menyetujui-transaksi-faktur-pembelian/)). SAP blocks goods receipts and invoices against an unapproved PO ([Espresso Tutorials](https://books.espresso-tutorials.com/invoice-verification-and-the-gr-ir-in-sap-s-4hana)). Unit Toko should show a Persetujuan chip and a Pemenuhan chip on every request and PO, show ordered, received, billed and remaining quantities on each line, reserve the quantities that drafts consume, add a "Dikirim ke pemasok" step, and replace delete with "Tutup dengan alasan" once anything downstream exists. Odoo's official Indonesian strings ("Menunggu Tagihan", "Sepenuhnya Ditagih", "Jml Diterima") provide ready-made vocabulary ([Odoo id.po](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/odoo/odoo/17.0/addons/purchase/i18n/id.po)).

**Receiving, inspection and held stock should mirror Indonesian SOPs and SAP's stock types.** A typical Indonesian warehouse SOP checks goods against the supplier's surat jalan, counts koli with photo or video, runs an incoming QC check, separates rejected from accepted goods, and only then stores what was accepted ([Logistics Plus Indonesia](https://logisticsplus.co.id/prosedur-sop-penerimaan-barang-di-gudang-warehouse/)). BULOG's SOP forbids receiving without a valid PO or SPK ([BULOG SOP](https://studylibid.com/doc/4331317/prosedur-penerimaan-dan-pengeluaran-barang)). Accurate's receipt stores the supplier's surat jalan number as "No Terima #" for later matching ([Solusi Akuntansi Indonesia](https://solusiakuntansiindonesia.com/penerimaan-barang-dari-pesanan-pembelian/)), keeps a short-delivered PO open, and is commonly configured so that warehouse roles cannot see purchase prices ([penjualanonline.id](https://penjualanonline.id/cara-membuat-penerimaan-barang-dari-pesanan-di-accurate-online/)). The Berita Acara Pemeriksaan Barang template supplies the inspection layout: the contract or SPK basis, the inspection team, a per-line Sesuai / Tidak Sesuai table, and one of three conclusions, "dapat diterima", "perlu perbaikan/pelengkapan/penggantian oleh Penyedia" or "perlu pemeriksaan ulang" ([LPSK](https://e-ppid.lpsk.go.id/storage/content-files/BERITA%20ACARA%20PEMERIKSAAN%20BARANG.pdf)). SAP keeps quality-inspection and blocked stock unavailable until a release decision ([SAP Help](https://help.sap.com/doc/a6a8c7536e8e2a4be10000000a174cb4/700_SFIN3E%20006/en-US/299bc7536e8e2a4be10000000a174cb4.html)), values conditionally received goods only at release, and lists overdue blocked stock by days since posting ([SAP Learning](https://learning.sap.com/courses/inventory-management-and-physical-inventory-in-sap-s-4hana/posting-a-goods-receipt-with-reference)). Unit Toko's pages already keep inspected goods quarantined and rely on original video (local codebase observation). They should add the BAPB layout, an aging column ("hari sejak diterima") on held stock, and a Penerimaan pengadaan form that requires the supplier's SJ number, vehicle plate and koli count.

**Matching needs a tax panel and a tukar faktur checklist alongside the three quantities.** Odoo's three-way match gives each bill a compact verdict of Yes, No or Exception on whether it should be paid ([Cybrosys](https://www.cybrosys.com/odoo/odoo-books/v17/purchase/3way-matching/)). Indonesian corporate buyers add document checks: PT SMART requires one invoice per PO, buyer name and NPWP exactly as on the PO, separate DPP and PPN and down payments shown on the final invoice, and it counts the **due date from receipt of complete documents** ([PT SMART](https://www.smart-tbk.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Prosedur-dan-Persyaratan-Penagihan.pdf)). Monotaro's tukar faktur bundle consists of a tanda terima, a stamped invoice, a signed GRN or surat jalan, a PO copy and the faktur pajak, which must carry the GRN date ([Monotaro](https://www.monotaro.id/panduan-tukar-faktur-dan-pembayaran)). In Coretax, suppliers' output fakturs arrive prepopulated in the buyer's Pajak Masukan list with a "Dilaporkan oleh Penjual" column, but crediting no longer depends on the seller's reporting under PER-11/PJ/2025 Pasal 122(3) ([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/per-11-pj-2025-tegaskan-hak-pkp-kreditkan-pajak-masukan-tanpa-tunggu-pelaporan-penjual/)), and input tax can be credited up to three periods later ([DJP KT-08/2025](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/2025-02/KT-08_2025%20Pengkreditan%20Pajak%20Masukan%20Pasca-Implementasi%20Coretax...pdf)). Dokumen pemasok dan pencocokan should therefore combine a Dipesan / Diterima (lolos mutu) / Ditagih grid with variance chips, a tax panel with the NSFP, the faktur date against the receipt date, DPP/PPN and the Coretax status, and the tukar faktur checklist. Utang pemasok should start its due date from "tanggal dokumen lengkap diterima" and show a "menunggu dokumen" state.

**Advances, payments and claims follow Accurate's lifecycle.** Accurate's **Uang Muka Pembelian** records the supplier's down-payment invoice number and a taxable flag, is paid through "Proses → Pembayaran", and is applied to the final invoice by "Ambil → Uang Muka" ([Accurate](https://help.accurate.id/product/accurate-online/fitur-aol/pembelian/uang-muka-pembelian/membuat-uang-muka-pembelian/)), while a dedicated "Retur Pembayaran Uang Muka" handles refunds ([trainingaccurate.com](https://trainingaccurate.com/blog/retur-uang-muka-pembelian-accurate-5/)). Payment is split into a Perintah Pembayaran that approves and a later Transfer Pemasok that records execution, which Accurate presents as separating the person who approves from the person who executes ([doniirvan.id](https://www.doniirvan.id/accurate-online/fitur-accurate-online/pembelian/)). Accurate also keeps **Klaim Pemasok** for replacement goods apart from Retur Pembelian for financial returns ([doniirvan.id](https://www.doniirvan.id/accurate-online/fitur-accurate-online/pembelian/)). The distinction matters legally, because only goods returned for credit need a Coretax nota retur, which the buyer (here Unit Toko) creates on the day of return ([DJP Coretaxpedia](https://www.pajak.go.id/coretaxpedia/retur-barang)). The final supplier invoice should show "total − uang muka terpakai = sisa tagihan".

**Supplier master data and bank accounts carry the fraud risk.** Indonesian vendor registration asks for the deed and Kemenkumham decree, an NIB with KBLI, a 16-digit NPWP, an SPPKP or non-PKP statement, and a bank book cover, statement or bank reference letter ([Bank Indonesia](https://www.bi.go.id/id/layanan/lelang-jasa-barang/Documents/Persyaratan-Dokumen-Registrasi-Rekanan-Badan-Usaha.pdf); [Pengadaan.com](https://helpdesk.pengadaan.com/portal/id/kb/articles/faq-syarat-kelengkapan-dokumen-vendor-non-perorangan)). PETRONAS makes a vendor ineligible when any document has fewer than **90 days** of validity left ([PETRONAS PePS](https://peps.petronas.com/FAQ.aspx)). PT SMART requires a stamped, signed statement for any bank-account change and an account name that matches the PO ([PT SMART](https://www.smart-tbk.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Prosedur-dan-Persyaratan-Penagihan.pdf)). Bank name-inquiry APIs return the holder name: BRI's distinguishes closed and dormant accounts ([BRIAPI](https://developers.bri.co.id/id/docs/api-docs-account-name-validation)), and Flip resolves some inquiries asynchronously ([Flip](https://docs.flip.id/docs/api/account-inquiry/)). Rekening pemasok should therefore require evidence, an inquiry result with a timestamp and a "Menunggu hasil cek rekening" state, a name match against the legal name, a signed change letter and a different checker, with old and new accounts side by side and no payment to a new account until it is approved.

**The single biggest improvement is a case page.** SAP's Document Flow shows all preceding and following documents with their status from any document ([SAP Help](https://help.sap.com/doc/b183ce53118d4308e10000000a174cb4/700_SFIN3E%20006/en-US/b4dfb65334e6b54ce10000000a174cb4.html)), SAP Business One's Relationship Map draws PO → receipt → invoice → payment ([SAP Help](https://help.sap.com/doc/011000358700000787192011e/8.82/en-US/RelationshipMaps_881.pdf)), and Odoo puts smart buttons on the PO ([Odoo](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/18.0/applications/inventory_and_mrp/purchase/manage_deals/manage.html)). No Indonesian ERP found offers a single cross-stage timeline, since Accurate and Jurnal work per document, so the gap is an opportunity. A "Kasus pengadaan" page keyed by PO should show a horizontal stepper running Permintaan → Pesanan → Uang muka → Penerimaan → Pemeriksaan → Mutu → Stok ditahan → Dokumen & pencocokan → Utang → Pembayaran/Klaim. Each step shows its status, quantity progress such as "Diterima 80/100, lolos mutu 75, ditahan 5", amount progress, and who made and who checked it, while steps that do not apply are greyed "Tidak berlaku". A "Langkah berikutnya" panel offers only the actions the viewer's role may take at that step. All eighteen queue pages remain, but every row links to the case.

## Branch approvers and administrators: make the four-eyes rule visible

The branch spending approver works in Peninjauan pesanan (`/ordering/reviews`) and maintains rules in Persetujuan belanja (`/ordering/spending-policies`); the names are easy to confuse, because "Persetujuan belanja" edits rules rather than approving orders. Administrators work in Akun dan undangan (`/administration/accounts`), Akses dan keanggotaan (`/administration/access`), Delegasi sementara (`/administration/delegations`), Organisasi dan unit with Alamat organisasi (`/administration/organizations`), Riwayat perubahan (`/administration/history`) and Keterkaitan akun (`/administration/person-reviews`). No legal rule governs these screens, so the recommendations are best practice, with one regulatory link: **Organisasi dan unit holds the branch NITKU and address**, so edits there need the same checker as an invoice.

**The approval queue should show why approval was needed and how long the request has left.** Amazon Business sets the standard. Approvers check the shipping address, payment method and items; they can **reject single items while approving the rest** but must reject the whole order when the address or payment method is wrong; and a pending cart holds its prices for **seven days**, with a reminder within three ([Amazon Business](https://business.amazon.com/en/learn/approving-requests)). Amazon's checkout carries coding fields such as PO number, cost center and "Comments for approver", and its button reads "Submit order for approval" ([Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=201633090)). Coupa returns a rejected requisition to Draft for the requester to fix and resubmit ([GitLab Handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/business-technology/enterprise-applications/guides/coupa-guide/)). A ServiceNow practitioner shows why the reject reason must be required on every path: the list's right-click "Reject" skipped the reason prompt that the form enforced ([David McDonald](https://davidmac.pro/posts/2025-05-16-approval-reject-reason/)). Talenta and RapidApproval allow bulk decisions with one shared comment ([Talenta](https://help-center.talenta.co/hc/en-us/articles/9170113068185-How-to-Manage-Delegation-on-the-Inbox-Menu)), and BNI's own approvers already process items in "My Task → Pending Task" **with a token** ([AHU Online](https://panduan.ahu.go.id/doku.php?id=cara_pembayaran_simpadhu_pnbp)). The branch-approval view of Peninjauan pesanan should therefore list Cabang/divisi, Pemesan, No. pesanan, Total, Sisa anggaran setelah pesanan, Aturan yang berlaku (for example "Melebihi Rp5 jt per pesanan"), Diajukan and Kedaluwarsa dalam, oldest first. The detail page offers Setujui, Tolak with a required reason, and Tolak sebagian per line, which is unavailable when the address is wrong. Step-up verification applies once per decision, or once per bulk batch with the count and total shown, and bulk approval skips the approver's own items and high-value items. Notification emails and WhatsApp messages carry only a deep link to sign-in. Sprout redirects logged-out email approvers to the login page so forwarded emails cannot approve ([Sprout](https://guide.sprout.ph/system-behavior-email-approvals-and-session-authentication)), whereas a Lawson forum thread records a forwarded email approving under a generic system account ([LawsonGuru](https://lawsonguru.com/forums/integration/ipa/process-flow-10-approval-from-forwarded-email)).

**Spending rules should be ordered rows with a fallback and plain-language reasons.** Coupa climbs people's approval limits until one covers the amount, falls back to an "ultimate approver", and treats self-approval as an explicit setting ([Coupa](https://get.coupa.com/rs/950-OLU-185/images/Approvals.pdf)); its chains come in blocking and warning types and carry a description shown in notifications ([Coupa docs](https://docs.coupa.com/en/developer-documentation/coupa-core-flat-files-csv/flat-file-csv-import/approval-chain-import)). Talenta evaluates approval lines from the top, most specific first, and its "Auto Ascend" forwards to the next level when an approver seat is empty ([Talenta](https://help-center.talenta.co/hc/en-us/articles/11371231766425-How-to-Set-or-Change-Approval)). Coupa also advises making budgets visible ([Coupa](https://www.coupa.com/blog/how-speed-requisition-order-time-shorter-approval-chains/)). Persetujuan belanja should therefore present each rule as a row combining a scope (organisation, wilayah, cabang or divisi), a condition (total above X, category in a set, or monthly budget remaining below Y), an approver and a priority, sorted "paling spesifik di atas" with a fallback approver at the bottom. Each rule is either blocking ("Pesanan tidak dapat diajukan") or a warning ("Perlu persetujuan"), and its reason text appears to both buyer and approver. "Pemesan tidak boleh menyetujui pesanannya sendiri" should be a system invariant rather than a setting; when the requester is the only eligible approver, the order routes upward and the requester is told why.

**Delegation must be dated, scoped and parallel.** SAP's planned substitution rejects a rule without an end date ([SAP](https://help.sap.com/doc/fioritech/1.0/en-US/attachments/3362178363/Managing_Substitutions_in_My_Inbox_2.0.pdf)), Coupa requires a reason and a delegate of equal or higher level ([GitLab Handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/business-technology/enterprise-applications/guides/coupa-guide/)), and Talenta restricts delegates by branch and runs delegation as a **parallel layer**, in which the main approver and the delegate both receive the item and the first decision counts ([Talenta](https://help-center.talenta.co/hc/en-us/articles/11388182960793-How-to-Create-and-Manage-Delegation-Settings)). Delegasi sementara should therefore require Dari, Kepada, Mulai and Selesai with no open-ended delegation, a scope, a reason and an eligibility filter, and record "Disetujui oleh X atas nama Y" in history. Banners appear in both users' Pekerjaan saya, and creating a delegation requires step-up verification.

**Access should be granted through role templates with a scope, an end date and a second admin.** Shopee's Sub Akun ships six default roles (Admin, Merchandiser, Penyelesaian Pesanan, Keuangan, Customer Service and KOL Livestreamer) ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/8572)), with the stated aim of stopping staff from changing prices or exporting confidential data without the owner knowing ([Shopee](https://seller.shopee.co.id/edu/article/13907)). Tokopedia's Seller Center splits permissions into "Hanya melihat" and "Melihat & Mengedit", requires invitees to activate within **120 jam**, and says in its FAQ that permissions come only through roles ([Tokopedia Seller University](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837736040679169&lang=id-ID)). A Tokopedia course contradicts that FAQ by describing per-employee edits ([Tokopedia course](https://seller-id.tokopedia.com/university/course?content_id=3221470063675152&learning_id=6992591971338001)); roles-only is the cleaner choice for audit. Google Workspace previews a role's privileges and scopes it to an organisational unit ([Google](https://support.google.com/a/answer/9807615?hl=en)) and requires a second admin for sensitive changes ([Google](https://support.google.com/a/answer/13790448?hl=en)). Entra access reviews offer Approve, Deny or Don't know with a reason and suggest revoking users with no sign-in in 30 days ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/id-governance/perform-access-review)). Unit Toko should ship named role templates for buying organisations (Pemesan cabang, Penyetuju belanja, Admin organisasi) and for the koperasi (Petugas gudang, Pemeriksa harga, Pemeriksa rilis faktur, Keuangan, Admin koperasi) with a "Lihat izin" matrix. The scope picker should default to the narrowest unit the inviter controls and state its coverage, as in "berlaku untuk 12 cabang di Wilayah Jakarta". Invitations should track Diundang, Aktif, Kedaluwarsa and Dinonaktifkan with an activation deadline and an optional "Berlaku sampai" date, approver-capable and admin roles should need a second admin, and a periodic "Tinjau akses" should close the loop.

**Riwayat perubahan should show who acted, on whose behalf, and what changed.** Entra's audit detail shows actor, target, correlation ID and old and new values ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity/monitoring-health/concept-audit-logs)), and Google's admin log adds a "By an agent" attribute and exports up to 100,000 rows ([Google](https://support.google.com/a/answer/4579579?hl=en)). Unit Toko's log should show Waktu (WIB), Pelaku (name, role and scope, or Sistem/Agen), Tindakan, Objek and Status, open each entry into a Sebelum → Sesudah drawer with the linked approval and any "atas nama" context, and export to CSV. No sources covered duplicate-person linking, so Keterkaitan akun has no external model.

|---|---|---|---|
| Peninjauan pesanan (`/ordering/reviews`), branch view | Rule, budget-after and expiry columns; oldest first; per-line reject; reason required on every path; step-up per decision or batch; deep-link-only notifications | Amazon Business, Coupa, ServiceNow, BNIdirect, Sprout | Best practice |

## Numbers and formats: one canonical code per document, regulated numbers verbatim

**Regulated identifiers are issued by DJP or Peruri and must be stored and shown exactly as issued, never generated.** The NSFP has 17 digits ([IKPI](https://ikpi.or.id/format-baru-nsfp-era-coretax-kini-17-digit-dan-diberikan-otomatis/)) and should be stored as a string such as `04002600000012345` and displayed grouped as `04.00.26.00000012345`, following the transaction code, status, year and eleven-digit serial grouping Klikpajak describes ([Klikpajak](https://klikpajak.id/blog/cara-mengatasi-nomor-faktur-pajak-tidak-urut-dan-ketentuannya/)); one commercial source gives a different split that contradicts the regulation, which takes precedence. NPWP and NITKU are digit strings of 16 and 22 characters, and the e-Meterai carries a 22-digit serial ([Bisnis.com](https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20211004/9/1450103/resmi-diluncurkan-ini-kelebihan-meterai-elektronik-atau-e-meterai)). DJP states there is no sanction for using NSFP serials out of order ([Klikpajak](https://klikpajak.id/blog/cara-mengatasi-nomor-faktur-pajak-tidak-urut-dan-ketentuannya/)), and no law requiring gap-free commercial invoice numbers was found. Gap-free, explainable numbering is an audit expectation, not a statute.

**Internal numbering is pure custom, and the custom suits letters better than software.** Indonesian letters use `{urut}/{kode}/{instansi}/{bulan Romawi}/{tahun}` and restart the counter each year ([Gramedia](https://www.gramedia.com/literasi/cara-membuat-nomor-surat/)), and Indonesian ERPs build formats from text, counter, month and year parts ([MASERP](https://intercom.help/maserp/id/articles/5396310-cara-setting-nomor-otomatis)). Tokopedia's buyer invoice reads `INV/20230720/MPL/3354884307`, with the date inside the number ([Scribd copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/691768676/Invoice-Tokopedia)). Shopee's order-number format could not be verified, so no date should ever be parsed out of it. Roman months sort wrongly as text (`X` before `IX`) and slashes break file names and URLs, so the recommended canonical form is **`{JENIS}-{YYMM}-{NNNN}`**. The number is assigned when a document is *issued*, not when a draft is created; it is never reused; a voided document keeps its number with the status "Batal"; and the counter is allocated atomically per type and period. A yearly reset only works when the year is part of the number, otherwise numbers repeat ([Odoo](https://odoobooks.readthedocs.io/en/12.0/accounting/invoice/invoice_numbers_by_financial_year.html)). Choose monthly for short numbers that are easy to say aloud, or yearly if auditors want one series per fiscal year. The letter-style form can still appear on the printed "Nomor:" line of a BAST or surat jalan.

**Display and formatting follow EYD V and the time-zone decree.** EYD V writes Rupiah as **`Rp225.000.000.000,00`**, with no space or period after "Rp", dots for thousands and a comma for decimals, and puts **no thousands dots** in identifiers such as bank account numbers ([EYD V](https://ejaan.kemendikdasmen.go.id/eyd/penggunaan-tanda-baca/tanda-titik/)). `Intl.NumberFormat` with the `id-ID` currency style inserts a space after "Rp" (a local Node 24.3 test run on 21 October 2026), so the formatter should format the number and prepend a literal "Rp", using `Rp1.250.000` in lists and `Rp1.250.000,00` with terbilang on formal documents. Times use a dot (`14.05`) and always carry a zone label: Indonesia's three legal zones are WIB (UTC+7), WITA (UTC+8) and WIT (UTC+9) ([Keppres 41/1987](https://web.archive.org/web/20160420185920/http://dapp.bappenas.go.id/upload/pdf/KEPPRES_1987_041.pdf)), and BNI branches span all three, so the system stores UTC and renders in the viewer's or branch's zone. Dates read `21 Oktober 2026` on documents and `21 Okt 2026` in tables, with `21/10/2026` only where space is tight and never the US month-first order. Phones are stored in E.164 (`+6281234567890`) and shown as `0812-3456-7890` ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telephone_numbers_in_Indonesia)), and postal codes are five-digit strings ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postal_codes_in_Indonesia)). Indonesian-registered GTINs start with **899**, and prefixes **020–029** are reserved for restricted-circulation internal codes ([GS1](https://www.gs1.org/prefixes)), so Unit Toko should scan manufacturer GTINs, print its own labels in the 2xx range, and never invent 899 numbers. Courier resi are opaque strings that an extra space alone can break ([Ship24](https://www.ship24.com/couriers/jne-express-tracking)); they should be stored trimmed and in upper case, shown in monospace next to the courier name, and copied without spaces. Every document number gets a copy button and is matched case-insensitively while ignoring hyphens and slashes, and random references use Crockford Base32, which omits the ambiguous letters I, L, O and U ([Crockford](https://www.crockford.com/base32.html)).

## Conclusion

The research moves the centre of gravity of Unit Toko's back office. The obvious models, Shopee and Tokopedia, turn out to matter least for the hard parts: their growth features clash with contract pricing, and Tokopedia has even removed the explicit acceptance step a B2B order needs. The useful templates come from Indonesian government and BUMN procurement, where a signed, inspected BAST drives billing, and from Indonesian SME accounting, where every change is a dated, approvable document. Unit Toko's segregated pages and its rule that a different, freshly verified person must approve are already stricter than what Accurate, Odoo or Coupa enforce by default. What staff lack is visibility: counts that say what is waiting, a case that shows where one purchase stands, and a checker view that explains instead of silently refusing.

The second shift is that the legal burden sits in one small, unforgiving area, and it is mostly arithmetic and identity. The withholding thresholds, the 11/12 base, the 22-digit branch identifier and the 20th-of-the-month deadline each turn a small data error into a cancelled faktur, a phantom underpayment or a late penalty. That argues for treating branch tax data and invoice calculation as the koperasi's most protected records, checked by a second person the way prices are, and for settling Unit Toko's PKP status, the transaction code and the BAST signing requirement with BNI and a tax adviser before go-live.
